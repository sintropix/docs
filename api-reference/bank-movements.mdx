A Bank Movement is a single transaction line pulled from a bank feed or entered manually against a Bank Account. Every Movement carries a workflow `status` that Sintropix computes on read: `unbooked`, `booked_pending_assign`, `reconciled`, or `excluded`. This page covers listing, editing, booking, and matching Bank Movements, individually or in bulk. It also covers assigning the rest of an overpaid Match, and recording an internal transfer between two Bank Accounts.

## Status

Read endpoints enrich each Movement with a `status` field computed from its booking and its Suspense line:

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `unbooked` | Movement has no Ledger Entry yet. |
| `booked_pending_assign` | Movement is booked to a Ledger Entry, but part of the Movement still sits on the [Suspense Account](#overpayments-and-the-suspense-account). |
| `reconciled` | Movement is booked, and its Ledger Entry has no Suspense line. |
| `excluded` | Movement is [excluded from the books](#exclude-a-movement-from-the-books): someone recorded that it will never be booked. |

You can also filter (`status=...`) and group (`groupBy=status`) the `GET /movements` listing by this value. Only `unbooked` and `booked_pending_assign` are pending work. A listing with no `status` filter lists every Movement, `excluded` ones included. Use `status=notExcluded` to list every Movement headed for the books, and `status=excluded` to list only the excluded ones.

## Credit card movements

Erwin can load the movements of a credit card into the card's Bank Accounts. To run it, ask Erwin in a Slack channel bound to the Entity's Organization through an [Agent Channel](/api-reference/agent-channels). There are two ways to load a card:

- **From a statement** (estado de cuenta facturado). Use this for each monthly statement. The statement is final, and after the load the Bank Account's balance equals the statement's billed balance.
- **From unbilled movements** (movimientos no facturados). Use this every few days during the month, so your team can reconcile card spend before the statement arrives. These movements are provisional.

Both ways follow the same rules:

- Each section of a card is a separate `card` Bank Account. The national (CLP) and international (USD) sections are two Bank Accounts. Erwin finds each one by the card's last 4 digits and the currency. Erwin never creates a Bank Account. It stops and asks if it finds none or more than one.
- Erwin loads every movement the bank shows, except one the bank marks as a pending authorization. It copies the `movementDate`, `amount`, and `side` exactly as the bank shows them. A charge is a `credit`, and a payment or refund is a `debit`.
- The `description` is the merchant when the bank shows it apart from the movement's text. Otherwise it is the bank's text for the movement. Erwin copies either one exactly as the bank shows it.
- Every movement gets a key in `externalRef` that never changes.
- `metadata` holds only these labels, in this order, and only when the bank shows the fact: `Tarjeta`, `Rubro`, `Ciudad`, `País`, `Monto origen`, `Cuota`, `Monto total compra`, `Fecha de compra`, `Referencia banco`, and `Leído de`. Erwin attaches no files.
- `Tarjeta` names the card that made the movement when the section has more than one card: its last 4 digits and `titular` or `adicional`, or `adicional` alone when the bank hides the card's digits.
- Erwin sets `partnerId` only when it is sure of the Partner. It never creates a Partner or changes one a movement already has.
- Erwin never creates adjustment movements to make a balance agree, never deletes a movement, and never books one. It reports each difference instead.

### From a statement

Send the statement file (PDF or Excel) in the Slack channel, or ask Erwin to download it from the bank's site. Before it writes anything, Erwin checks that:

- the statement adds up: the previous balance, plus charges, minus payments and refunds, equals the billed balance.
- the Bank Account's `runningBalance` on the day before the period starts is minus the previous statement's balance. A debt reads negative. If the Bank Account has no movements yet, Erwin first creates a `Saldo inicial` movement for the previous balance.
- the period has no movements from other loads, such as unbilled movements. If it does, Erwin stops and reports them for a person to reconcile.

Erwin then asks a person to approve the load, with the period, line count, totals, and check results. Each statement needs its own approval, even within the same conversation. Erwin dates a line from before the period at the period's start, and puts its own date in `Fecha de compra`. It dates installments (cuotas) at the statement's billing date (fecha de facturación). The key of each line is `estado:<billing date>:<line number>`, for example `estado:2026-08-25:001`. A statement movement is final, and later loads never change it. After the load, Erwin reports whether the closing `runningBalance` equals minus the billed balance.

### From unbilled movements

Erwin reads the bank's site, so the Entity needs a login Credential for the bank, such as a [Bank Login](/api-reference/integrations#portal-logins) stored in **Integraciones**. BCI, BICE, Itaú, and Santander are supported. Erwin signs in to BCI, BICE, Itaú, and Santander Personas by himself. For Santander Empresas, a person signs in by hand and Erwin continues in their session. See [Sign in to a bank by hand](/api-reference/agent-channels#sign-in-to-a-bank-by-hand). At Itaú, Erwin reads the card's unbilled purchases in pesos and in dollars. If the login reaches more than one company at Itaú, Erwin picks the company whose RUT is the Entity's `taxId`, and stops if none matches. If Santander can't load a card's movements, Erwin retries up to 3 times. If it still fails, it goes on with the next card or section, keeps what it already read, and reports the one that failed with the site's message. Each run rereads the whole open period, from the day after the last billing date to 2 days before today. It skips today and yesterday, because the bank can still reverse those movements.

The key is `no-facturado:<bank ID>`. When the bank shows no ID, the key is `no-facturado:<date>:<amount in minor units>:<side>:<n>`, where `n` counts the movements with the same date, amount, and side. Erwin pairs each site movement with an existing Movement by key first. If there is no key match, it pairs with the one Movement that has the same amount and side, dated at most 3 days away. Then:

- An unpaired site movement is created.
- A paired `unbooked` Movement gets the site's current date, description, amount, and metadata. A booked one gets only new metadata and a Partner. Erwin replaces the whole `metadata` list rather than adding to it.
- Erwin changes only Movements whose key starts with `no-facturado:`. It reports pairs with Movements from other loads without changing them.
- A `no-facturado:` Movement the site no longer shows is reported, not deleted. It is often a reversed authorization.

You can run it as often as you like without creating duplicates. There is no balance check. Your team reconciles the unbilled movements against the statement at month-end close.

## Bank account movements

Erwin can load the movements of a checking account, in pesos or in dollars, from the bank's site. Each movement carries what the site knows beyond the bank's description: who paid or received the money, the operation number, and often the invoice the payment is for. Your team can then reconcile from Sintropix without opening the bank. To run it, ask Erwin in a Slack channel bound to the Entity's Organization through an [Agent Channel](/api-reference/agent-channels).

Erwin reads the bank's site, so the Entity needs a login Credential for the bank. If the login reaches more than one company at the bank, Erwin picks the company whose RUT is the Entity's `taxId`, and stops if none matches. At BCI, Erwin reads the counterpart of each transfer of a CLP account. For a USD account, it reads the payment order behind each movement, with the ordenante, the payment reference, the amount the ordenante sent, and the fees the banks took, from its SWIFT message. When BCI offers the SWIFT message as a file, Erwin attaches it to the Movement. It also reports payment orders that reached the bank but aren't in the account yet.

Erwin follows these rules:

- Erwin finds the `bank` Bank Account by its account number and currency. It never creates a Bank Account, and stops and asks if it finds none or more than one.
- Each run reads from 3 days before the newest Movement of the Bank Account to today. If the Bank Account has no Movements, Erwin asks from which date to load.
- It copies the `movementDate` and `amount` exactly as the bank shows them. A credit to the account (abono) is a `debit`, and a charge (cargo) is a `credit`.
- The `description` is the bank's text, then the counterpart's name and RUT when the bank shows them, joined with ` · `, the same format a [bank feed](/api-reference/bank-feeds) writes. For example: `ORDEN DE PAGO RECIBIDA · BARRIOS Y FUENTES BAFUR`.
- The key in `externalRef` is `<bank>:<account number>:op:<operation number>`, for example `bci:19864345:op:3211420`. When the bank shows no operation number, the key is `<bank>:<account number>:<date>:<amount in minor units>:<side>:<n>`, where `n` counts the movements with the same date, amount, and side, from `1`. The key never changes.
- `metadata` holds the facts the bank shows about the movement that tell who moved the money, why, and how much arrived. It holds only what the bank shows: never Erwin's notes, file names, or internal codes, and never the movement's date, amount, side, or description. Values are copied as the bank shows them, and dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Common facts use the same label at every bank. These lines come first, in this order: `Contraparte`, `RUT de contraparte`, `Cuenta de contraparte`, `Institución de contraparte`, `País de la contraparte`, `Comentario`, `N° de operación`, `Fecha valor` (only when it differs from the movement's date), `Referencia de pago`, `Monto instruido` (the amount and currency the payer sent, such as `:33B:` of a SWIFT message), and `Gastos` (the fees the banks took, such as `:71F:` of a SWIFT message). Other facts the bank shows come after them, each with a Spanish label that names the fact.
- Erwin [attaches](/api-reference/attachments) to the Movement each comprobante of it that it has as a file: the SWIFT message of a payment order, a transfer comprobante the bank's site offers, or a proof of payment the counterpart sent by email. It attaches each file once, and lists the files it attached in its report.
- Erwin sets `partnerId` only when it is sure of the Partner: the counterpart's RUT is the RUT of exactly one Partner, earlier Movements with the same counterpart all have the same Partner, or the counterpart names exactly one Partner. It never creates a Partner or changes one a Movement already has.

Erwin pairs each site movement with an existing Movement the same way as for [unbilled card movements](#from-unbilled-movements): by key first, then with the one Movement that has the same amount and side, dated at most 3 days away. Then:

- An unpaired site movement is created.
- A paired `unbooked` Movement gets the site's description and metadata. A booked one gets only new metadata and a Partner, and Erwin reports any other difference. Erwin replaces the whole `metadata` list rather than adding to it.
- Erwin changes only Movements whose key starts with `<bank>:<account number>:`. It reports pairs with Movements from other loads without changing them.

Erwin never creates adjustment movements to make a balance agree, never deletes a Movement, and never books one. It reports the bank's balance next to the Bank Account's balance, the movements it could not pair, and the counterparts it left without a Partner.

**Accounts with a bank feed.** On a Bank Account with a [feed](/api-reference/bank-feeds), the feed creates the Movements and rewrites their description and its own labels at each sync. Erwin never creates Movements there. When you ask it to add the site's facts, it only sets a missing Partner and adds lines for the facts the feed doesn't write, after the lines the Movement already has. It never changes a line the feed writes.

### Ontop wallet

[Ontop](https://www.getontop.com) pays a company's foreign contractors from a prepaid USD wallet. Erwin loads the wallet like any other bank account, with the rules above, from the wallet history of Ontop's site. Ask *"carga Ontop"* to load the wallet and then the [Ontop invoices](/api-reference/invoices#ontop-invoices) in one go.

Before the first load:

- Add a login Credential for Ontop to the Entity in **Integraciones**. If Ontop shows a captcha or asks for a code, a person [signs in by hand](/api-reference/agent-channels#sign-in-to-a-bank-by-hand).
- Create a USD `bank` Bank Account for the wallet. The wallet has no account number, so Erwin uses the company's client id in Ontop instead. Ask Erwin to note the client id and the wallet's Bank Account in his [wiki](/core-concepts).

Erwin loads only the rows that succeeded, and skips failed ones. Each row becomes one Movement:

| Row in Ontop | What it is | `side` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top-up by wire (`TOP_UP`, `WT` or `USAWT`) | A wire from a bank account of the company | `debit` |
| Top-up by card (`TOP_UP`, `CC`) | A top-up charged to a card of the company | `debit` |
| Money from Ontop (`TOP_UP`, `MAN`) | Money Ontop put in, such as a cashback | `debit` |
| Payment (`PAYMENT`, `PRPDF`) | A payment to the contractors from the wallet | `credit` |

- The `amount` is what entered or left the wallet. For a top-up by card, that excludes the card's fee.
- The `description` is the row's type, payment method code, and reference, joined with ` · `, for example `TOP_UP · MAN · Cashback`.
- Ontop shows no operation number, so the key is `ontop:<client id>:<date>:<amount in minor units>:<side>:<n>`.
- After the load, Erwin reports the wallet's balance in Ontop next to the Bank Account's balance.

Erwin only reads Ontop. It never pays, tops up, approves, signs, or changes anything on Ontop's site.

<Note>
  Most routes on this page are nested under a Bank Account. The base path is `/api/entities/:entityId/bank-accounts/:bankAccountId/movements`, and `:movementId` is a UUID. The one exception is [`GET /bank-movements/pages`](#get-/api/entities/entityid/bank-movements/pages), which sits directly under the Entity and lists the Movements of every Bank Account.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Entities with Bases.** A Bank Movement is a fact every [Basis](/api-reference/bases) of the Entity shares, so booking or matching one always posts a common Ledger Entry. `POST /book`, `POST /match`, their bulk forms, and the Follow-up acts that book a Movement take no `basisId`, and `GET /movements/:movementId/match-candidates` lists lines of common Entries only. See [Facts are common](/api-reference/bases#facts-are-common).
</Note>

## The Bank Movement object

Every read and write on this page returns Movements in this shape. Amounts are non-negative integers in the account currency's minor units; the direction is carried by `side`.

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Movement id. |
| `entityId` | uuid | Owning Entity. |
| `bankAccountId` | uuid | Bank Account the Movement sits on. |
| `movementDate` | string (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | Date the bank reported the line. |
| `providerTime` | ISO 8601 timestamp \| null | The time the [Provider](/api-reference/bank-feeds#provider-time) reported for the line. It only orders the Movements of one day. `null` when the Provider gives no time it can be trusted for, and on every Movement you create or Erwin loads. |
| `amount` | integer | Magnitude in minor units, never negative. |
| `side` | `debit` \| `credit` | Direction against the Bank Account's mapped Ledger Account. `debit` is money in for a `bank` account. |
| `description` | string | The description the bank reported. On a fed Bank Account, each [sync](/api-reference/bank-accounts#sync-a-bank-account-feed) keeps it at the Provider's newest text, booked or not. |
| `externalRef` | string \| null | The bank's own reference for the line. Unique per Bank Account when present. |
| `partnerId` | uuid \| null | Counterparty Partner. |
| `ledgerEntryId` | uuid \| null | The Ledger Entry that books this Movement. `null` while unbooked. |
| `ledgerLineId` | uuid \| null | The bank line of that entry. `null` while unbooked. |
| `ledgerAccountId` | uuid \| null | The Ledger Account that bank line sits on. `null` while unbooked. |
| `metadata` | array of `{ label, value }` | Additional Information lines (up to 50). |
| `followUp` | object \| null | The [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups) on this Movement, which marks its pending work. `null` when there is none. |
| `excludedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp \| null | When the Movement was [excluded from the books](#exclude-a-movement-from-the-books). `null` when it isn't excluded. |
| `exclusionReason` | string \| null | Why it was excluded. `null` exactly when `excludedAt` is null. |
| `status` | `unbooked` \| `booked_pending_assign` \| `reconciled` \| `excluded` | Derived booking status. |
| `runningBalance` | integer | Balance of the Bank Account after this Movement, counting every Movement up to it in [date order](#order-of-movements) regardless of filters. |
| `partnerName` | string \| null | Name of `partnerId`, for display. |
| `partnerWebsite` | string \| null | Website of `partnerId`, as a bare domain, for showing the Partner's [logo](/api-reference/partners#get-/api/organizations/organizationid/partners/partnerid/logo). |
| `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | Row timestamps. |

### Order of movements

Movements are in date order: by `movementDate`, then by `providerTime`, then by `createdAt`, then by `id`. Within one day, a Movement without a `providerTime` comes before every Movement of that day that has one. The running balance follows this order, so a conversion reads after the deposits that funded it earlier that day.

A Movement keeps the `providerTime` it first landed with. A later sync can fill it on a Movement that has none, booked or not, but never changes one the Movement already holds.

Write answers also return the Bank Account with its computed summary:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Bank Account id. |
| `entityId` | uuid | Owning Entity. |
| `name` | string | Display name. |
| `kind` | `bank` \| `card` | Account kind. |
| `ledgerAccountId` | uuid | Ledger Account where booked activity accumulates. |
| `currency` | string | ISO 4217 code, for example `CLP`. |
| `accountNumber` | string \| null | The number the bank knows the account by. |
| `bankCode` | string \| null | CMF code of the bank that holds the account. |
| `feed` | object \| null | `{ integrationId, integrationKind, providerAccountId, institutionName, lastSyncedAt, tieOut }` when an [Integration](/api-reference/bank-feeds) feeds the account; `null` otherwise. See [Bank Accounts](/api-reference/bank-accounts) for each field. |
| `balance` | integer | Signed balance on the Ledger Account's normal side. |
| `unreconciledCount` | integer | Movements still to reconcile: `unbooked` and `booked_pending_assign`. Excluded Movements don't count. |
| `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | Row timestamps. |

## GET `/movements`

List Movements on a Bank Account. Returns a paginated `{ movements, nextCursor }` object.

**Query parameters**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cursor` | string | No | The `nextCursor` value from the previous page. Cannot be combined with `aroundMovementId`. |
| `aroundMovementId` | uuid | No | Return the page that contains this Movement instead of continuing a walk. |
| `limit` | integer | No | Page size, `1` to `100`. Default `50`. |
| `status` | `unbooked` \| `booked_pending_assign` \| `reconciled` \| `excluded` \| `unreconciled` \| `notExcluded` | No | Filter by booking status. `unreconciled` keeps every Movement still to reconcile, both `unbooked` and `booked_pending_assign`. `notExcluded` keeps every status but `excluded`. Omit it to list every status, `excluded` included. |
| `followUpKind` | `proposal` \| `question` \| `waiting` \| `none` | No | Filter by the kind of the Movement's [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups). `none` keeps Movements with no Follow-up. |
| `partnerId` | uuid | No | Filter by counterparty Partner. |
| `side` | `debit` \| `credit` | No | Filter by direction. |
| `movementDateFrom` | string (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | No | Earliest movement date, inclusive. |
| `movementDateTo` | string (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | No | Latest movement date, inclusive. |
| `amountMin` | integer | No | Minimum magnitude in minor units. Matched against the magnitude on either side. |
| `amountMax` | integer | No | Maximum magnitude in minor units. |
| `search` | string | No | Up to 100 characters. Matches the description and the Partner name anywhere, ignoring case. Matches the bank reference only from its start: the whole reference or a prefix of it, ignoring case, with `%` and `_` read as plain characters. A search that reads as an amount also matches the Movement's amount; see [Search by amount](/api-reference/overview#search-by-amount). Blank is ignored. |
| `erpThread` | `with` \| `without` | No | `with` keeps only Movements that have an [ERP Thread](/api-reference/notifications#talk-about-a-record-in-its-erp-thread), the conversation about the Movement in the Organization's Chat; `without` keeps only Movements with none. Omit to return both. Group counts follow this filter. |
| `groupBy` | `none` \| `partner` \| `movementMonth` \| `status` | No | Attach a `group` object to every row. Default `none`. `bankAccount` is refused with `400`, because this listing covers one Bank Account. |
| `sort` | `movementDate` \| `partnerName` \| `description` \| `amount` | No | Sort field. Default `movementDate`. Sorting by `amount` sorts the signed amount, money in above money out. |
| `sortDirection` | `asc` \| `desc` | No | Default `desc`. Movements with the same sort value follow the [date order](#order-of-movements) within it, in the same direction. With `desc`, the newest `providerTime` of a day comes first and the Movements without one come last. |

**Response**

Each row is a Bank Movement object plus `attachmentCount` (integer, the number of [attachments](/api-reference/attachments) on the Movement), `hasErpThread` (boolean, `true` when the Movement has an ERP Thread), and `group`: `null` when `groupBy=none`, otherwise `{ by, value, key, movementCount, startsGroup }` where `by` is the `groupBy` value, `value` is the Partner name (nullable), the `YYYY-MM` month, or the status, `movementCount` is the size of the group across the whole result, and `startsGroup` is `true` on the first row of the group. `nextCursor` is `null` on the last page.

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/bank-accounts/$BANK_ACCOUNT_ID/movements?status=unbooked&limit=25" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

To list only the unbooked Movements that nobody has written about yet, add `erpThread=without`:

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/bank-accounts/$BANK_ACCOUNT_ID/movements?status=unbooked&erpThread=without" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

## GET `/api/entities/:entityId/bank-movements/pages`

List the Movements of every Bank Account of the Entity in one listing, grouped by Bank Account by default. Use it to build one queue of the work still to reconcile instead of reading each Bank Account in turn. It returns the same paginated `{ movements, nextCursor }` object as [`GET /movements`](#get-/movements).

**Query parameters**

It takes the parameters of `GET /movements` except `amountMin` and `amountMax`, because the Bank Accounts can hold different currencies. `groupBy` also takes `bankAccount`, and defaults to it. Any other parameter, including `amountMin` and `amountMax`, is refused with `400`.

**Response**

Each row has the shape of a `GET /movements` row. Read `bankAccountId` to know which account a Movement sits on. `runningBalance` is the balance of that Movement's own Bank Account.

With `groupBy=bankAccount`, `group.value` is the Bank Account name and `group.key` is `bankAccount:<bankAccountId>`. Groups come in alphabetical order of the account name.

To list every Movement still to reconcile, across every account:

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/bank-movements/pages?status=unreconciled" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

In the app, the same listing is at `/{entityId}/banks/movements`. See [Link to the app](/guides/app-links).

## GET `/movements/:movementId/match-candidates`

List the open Ledger Entry Lines and the unbooked Invoices that could settle this Movement, ranked by Sintropix. On an Entity with [Bases](/api-reference/bases), only lines of common Entries are candidates, and the read takes no `basisId`. The order is: exact amount first, then Partner evidence, then the distance between the dates, then the newest entry or issue date, then id. At most 200 candidates, cut after the ranking.

**Query parameters**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `q` | string | No | Search text. Matches the document number, the entry memo, the account code and name, the Partner's name, legal name, and tax ID, and the amount. Names, memos, and account names match ignoring accents. The search runs before the cut, so it reaches candidates past the first 200. |

**Response:** `{ candidates: [...], highConfidenceCandidateId }`. `highConfidenceCandidateId` names the recommended candidate (its `line.id`, or its `invoice.id` for an unbooked Invoice), or is `null` when no single candidate stands out. Each candidate has a `kind`:

- `line`: an open Ledger Entry Line on the side that settles the Movement.
- `unbookedInvoice`: an Invoice of kind `invoice` that isn't booked yet, so it has no line to settle. Book the Invoice first; it then appears as a `line` candidate. A credit note or a [`tax_only`](/api-reference/invoices#tax-only-documents) document is never an `unbookedInvoice` candidate. It carries `kind`, `invoice` (`{ id, documentNumber, issueDate, total, currency, direction, documentKind, isExpenseRendition }`), `partner`, and `evidence`.

Every candidate carries `evidence`: `partnerEvidence` (`{ assigned, name, taxId }`, booleans that say whether the Partner is the Movement's Partner, or its name or tax ID appears in the description), `amountRelation` (`exact` \| `underpays` \| `overpays`, where `underpays` means the Movement is smaller; `null` on a foreign-currency Bank Account), and `dateDistanceDays` (integer).

A `line` candidate has these fields:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kind` | `line` | Candidate kind. |
| `line` | object | The open Ledger Entry Line: `id`, `entryId`, `accountId`, `entityId`, `functionalAmount`, `side`, `partnerId`, `transactionCurrency`, `transactionAmount`, `transactionFxRate`, `openAmount` (remaining unsettled amount, nullable), `openTransactionAmount` (units of `transactionCurrency` still open on a foreign line, `null` on a domestic line), `createdAt`, `updatedAt`. |
| `entry` | object | `{ id, entryDate, memo }` of the entry the line belongs to. |
| `account` | object | `{ id, code, name }` of the Ledger Account the line sits on. |
| `partner` | object \| null | `{ id, name, legalName, taxId }` when the line names a Partner. |
| `invoice` | object \| null | `{ id, documentNumber, issueDate, isExpenseRendition }` when the line is an Invoice's control line. `isExpenseRendition` is `true` for an [Expense Rendition](/api-reference/expense-claims), which the app names **Rendición** instead of **Factura**. |
| `evidence` | object | See above. |

Pass the candidate's `line.id` as `lineId` to `POST /movements/:movementId/match`.

## Overpayments and the Suspense Account

A Movement can pay more than the document you match it to. For example, a customer transfers 1,000,000 against an invoice with 700,000 open. The extra 300,000 might pay another invoice, belong to another Partner, or cover an invoice that has not arrived yet. Sintropix doesn't credit it to the invoice's Partner. It books the rest on the Entity's Suspense Account instead.

The Suspense Account is an `asset` Open-Item Account with sub-type `suspense`. Each Entity has at most one. See [Chart of Accounts](/api-reference/chart-of-accounts) to create or mark it. Its lines carry no Partner, and only Match, Assign, and Unassign write them.

The flow for the example above:

| Step | Ledger Entry lines | Movement `status` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Match invoice A (700,000 open) | Bank debit 1,000,000, Clients credit 700,000, Suspense credit 300,000 | `booked_pending_assign` |
| Assign 300,000 to invoice B | Bank debit 1,000,000, Clients credit 700,000, Clients credit 300,000 | `reconciled` |
| Unassign the 300,000 line | Back to the first Entry. Invoice B reopens. | `booked_pending_assign` |

Keep these rules in mind:

- A Match that leaves a rest is refused with `BANK_MOVEMENT_SUSPENSE_ACCOUNT_MISSING_ERROR` when the Entity has no Suspense Account. A Match with no rest works without one.
- Assign and Unassign edit the Match's Ledger Entry in place. They do not create a new Entry. They work only on an active Entry dated after the Lock Date.
- To undo the whole Match, unbook the Movement. [Reversing](/api-reference/ledger-entries#post-/api/entities/entityid/ledger/entries/entryid/reverse) the Entry is refused while it still has a Suspense line.
- On a foreign-currency Bank Account, the Suspense line is in the Bank Account's currency when every counterpart line is in that currency. Otherwise it's in the functional currency.
- The Suspense Account cannot be a Match or Assign target, and you cannot post to it through manual entries or Reclassify.

## POST `/movements/:movementId/assign`

Move part or all of a Movement's Suspense line into new lines of the same Ledger Entry. Use it when you find out what the rest of an overpaid Match pays. Requires `x-audit-actor` for API-key callers.

Assign works in two ways:

- **Settle an open item.** Set `lineId` to the open line to settle, such as another Invoice's control line. Include a settlement line on that line's account. Sintropix allocates it against the target, up to the target's Open Amount, the same way as Match.
- **Categorize to accounts.** Set `lineId` to `null` and send plain lines on any account, for example other income or a bank fee.

The Suspense line shrinks by the net amount the new lines take. It goes away when nothing is left, and the Movement then becomes `reconciled`.

**Query param:** `dryRun` (boolean, optional). `true` runs the write and rolls it back. See [Dry run](#dry-run).

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lineId` | uuid \| null | Yes | The open Ledger Entry Line to settle, for example `candidates[].line.id` from `GET /match-candidates`. Pass `null` to assign to plain account lines. |
| `newCounterpartLines` | array of line objects | Yes | Counterpart lines, in the same shape as `POST /book`, each with an amount above `0` (`functionalAmount` on a domestic line, `transactionAmount` on a foreign one). A settlement line follows the same rules as on `POST /match`. Together they cannot take more than the Suspense line holds. The array can be empty only when you send `exchangeDifferenceLineId`: the Exchange Difference Line alone then takes the difference the Match left on the Suspense line. |
| `exchangeDifferenceLineId` | uuid | No | Same rule as `POST /book`. If the Entry already keeps an Exchange Difference Line, don't send another one: Sintropix updates the kept line. |
| `exchangeDifferenceReason` | string | No | Same rule as `POST /match`. |

**Response:** `{ movement, bankAccount, reconciliation }`, the same shape as `POST /match`.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/bank-accounts/$BANK_ACCOUNT_ID/movements/$MOVEMENT_ID/assign" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "lineId": "7e8f9a0b-1c2d-4e3f-8a4b-5c6d7e8f9a0b",
    "newCounterpartLines": [
      {
        "id": "8f9a0b1c-2d3e-4f4a-8b5c-6d7e8f9a0b1c",
        "accountId": "2c3d4e5f-6a7b-4c8d-9e0f-1a2b3c4d5e6f",
        "functionalAmount": 300000,
        "side": "credit",
        "partnerId": "b2c3d4e5-f6a7-4b8c-9d0e-1f2a3b4c5d6e"
      }
```

  Assign is not idempotent. If a request may have succeeded, read `GET /movements/:movementId/reconciliation` before you retry it.

## POST `/movements/:movementId/unassign`

Move one counterpart line of the Movement's Ledger Entry back to the Suspense line. Sintropix removes the line's Allocations, so any document it settled reopens. Use it to correct an Assign or a Match line without unbooking the whole Movement. Requires `x-audit-actor` for API-key callers.

The Entity must have a Suspense Account. Sintropix creates the Suspense line if the Entry has none. You cannot unassign the bank line, the Suspense line, or a line that another record holds, such as a Fixed Asset. A line in an [Intercompany Link](/api-reference/intercompany-links) returns `409` with `INTERCOMPANY_LINK_LINE_ALREADY_LINKED_ERROR`, and `params.intercompanyLinkId` names the Link. Unlink it first, then Unassign.

If the Entry keeps an Exchange Difference Line, Sintropix recomputes it for the gap that's left, or removes it when no gap is left.

**Query param:** `dryRun` (boolean, optional). `true` runs the write and rolls it back. See [Dry run](#dry-run).

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lineId` | uuid | Yes | The counterpart line to move back to the Suspense line. Take it from `reconciliation.entry.lines`. |

**Response:** `{ movement, bankAccount, reconciliation }`.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/bank-accounts/$BANK_ACCOUNT_ID/movements/$MOVEMENT_ID/unassign" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "lineId": "8f9a0b1c-2d3e-4f4a-8b5c-6d7e8f9a0b1c" }'
```

### Match, Assign, and Unassign errors

Each error answers `409` with the `code` and the `params` shown. Amounts are functional amounts in minor units, except where a row says otherwise. For the foreign-currency errors, see [Foreign-currency errors](#foreign-currency-errors).

Match and Assign check the settlement line with the same `LEDGER_ENTRY_SETTLED_LINE_*` and `LEDGER_ENTRY_SETTLEMENT_*` codes as a [manual entry that settles open lines](/api-reference/ledger-entries#settlement-errors).

<Note>
  These codes replace `BANK_MOVEMENT_MATCH_LINE_NOT_OPEN_ERROR`, `BANK_MOVEMENT_MATCH_SIDE_INCOMPATIBLE_ERROR`, `BANK_MOVEMENT_MATCH_SETTLEMENT_LINE_PARTNER_MISMATCH_ERROR`, `BANK_MOVEMENT_MATCH_SETTLEMENT_LINE_DENOMINATION_MISMATCH_ERROR`, `BANK_MOVEMENT_MATCH_SETTLEMENT_EXCEEDS_OPEN_AMOUNT_ERROR`, and `BANK_MOVEMENT_MATCH_TARGET_ON_SUSPENSE_ACCOUNT_ERROR`, which Sintropix no longer returns. A target on the Suspense Account now answers `LEDGER_ENTRY_SETTLED_LINE_NOT_OPEN_ITEM_ERROR`.
</Note>

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |

| `BANK_MOVEMENT_ASSIGN_TAKES_NOTHING_FROM_SUSPENSE_LINE_ERROR` | The new lines take nothing from the Suspense line, for example because lines on the Movement's own side outweigh the others. `params: { suspenseLineId, assignedAmount }`. |

## Foreign currency and the exchange difference

Book, Match, Assign, and Unassign, single or bulk, value foreign lines the way a [manual Ledger Entry](/api-reference/ledger-entries#foreign-currency-lines) does. So do the bank acts of a [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups) Plan when it's approved. A foreign line states its currency and its units, and Sintropix works out its functional amount. You never send a rate or a functional amount for a foreign line.

### The bank line

Sintropix builds the bank line from the Movement. On a Bank Account in the Entity's functional currency, the bank line is domestic. On a Bank Account in another currency, it's foreign, and Sintropix values it at the rate the Entity's [Rate Source](/api-reference/fx-rates#rate-sources) published for the Movement's date, or the last date before it.

To use another rate, send `bankLineOverride` on a Book or a Match, with `overrideFxRate` and `overrideFxRateReason` (1 to 500 characters). The override also values every other line of the request that's in the Bank Account's currency and settles nothing, such as a split or a fee line. A Bank Account in the functional currency refuses it with `BANK_MOVEMENT_BANK_LINE_OVERRIDE_FORBIDDEN_ERROR`. A Match with an Exchange Difference Line refuses it too; see [Rate overrides and the exchange difference](#rate-overrides-and-the-exchange-difference).

### Settle a foreign open item

The line that settles a foreign open item, such as an Invoice in USD, states the item's `transactionCurrency` and the units it settles, with no `functionalAmount`. Read the units still open in `openTransactionAmount` on a [match candidate](#get-/movements/movementid/match-candidates). When no Movement pays the item, a [manual Ledger Entry](/api-reference/ledger-entries#settle-open-lines) can settle it the same way.

- Sintropix values the settlement line at the item's carrying amount: all the open units take the whole open functional amount, and fewer units take their share of it. The line's `transactionFxRateOrigin` is `settled_item`.
- A settlement line never takes a rate of its own. `overrideFxRate` on it is refused with `LEDGER_ENTRY_LINE_OVERRIDE_ON_SETTLEMENT_ERROR`.
- The Allocation records the units it settles.
- A partial settlement is refused with `LEDGER_ENTRY_SETTLEMENT_PARTIAL_NOT_MEASURABLE_ERROR` when its share of the carrying amount rounds to `0`, or to all of the open amount while units stay open. `params.minimumUnits` and `params.maximumPartialUnits` give the range of partial units the item accepts, or `null` when it accepts none. Settling all the open units always works.

What the Movement paid and what the books carried for the item usually differ. That difference is an exchange difference.

### The Exchange Difference Line

When the valued lines leave a gap, declare an Exchange Difference Line: send `exchangeDifferenceLineId`, a new UUID. Don't send a line of your own for the difference, and don't name its account. Sintropix fills in the line:

- **Amount:** the gap the valuation leaves, in the functional currency.
- **Side:** `credit` for a gain, `debit` for a loss.
- **Account:** the Entity's [Exchange Difference Account](/api-reference/chart-of-accounts#exchange-difference-accounts) for that side. A gain goes to the tagged `other_income` account and a loss to the tagged `other_expense` account. When the Entity tagged a single account, it takes both. With no account for the side, the write is refused with `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_ACCOUNT_MISSING_ERROR`.
- **Ground:** `exchangeDifferenceGround` on the line says why it exists. `settlement` means the act settles an open item. `conversion` means the act settles nothing and moves money between two Bank Accounts of the Entity kept in different currencies: a counterpart line posts to the Ledger Account of a Bank Account in another currency. A third ground, `revaluation`, appears only on the computed lines of a [Revaluation Entry](/api-reference/ledger-entries#revaluation-entries). You never declare it.

An Entry holds one Exchange Difference Line. Once a Match or an Assign declares it, later Assigns and Unassigns of the Movement keep it and update its amount, or remove it when no gap is left.

Use a [dry run](#dry-run) to learn whether you need the line. `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_REQUIRED_ERROR` answers the gap and its side, so add `exchangeDifferenceLineId` and run it again. `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_WITHOUT_GAP_ERROR` means there's no difference, so leave the field out.

### Rate overrides and the exchange difference

Sintropix measures the exchange difference against the published rate, so a Match or an Assign with an Exchange Difference Line takes no rate of your own. This applies to a Match or an Assign that declares the line, and to an Assign on a Movement whose Entry already keeps one. Each of these is refused with `409` and `LEDGER_ENTRY_LINE_OVERRIDE_ON_SETTLEMENT_ERROR`:

- **A counterpart line with `overrideFxRate`.** `params.lineId` is the line's `id`.
- **`bankLineOverride` on the Match.** `params.lineId` is `null`, because Sintropix builds the bank line.
- **An Assign on a Movement whose Match booked the bank line with `bankLineOverride`.** The Suspense line carries that rate, so `params.lineId` is `null`.

To record a fee or a payment at a rate of your own, send it as a domestic line with its `functionalAmount`. Or leave out the Exchange Difference Line, and leave the rest on the Suspense Account until you assign it.

When one Movement pays items in two currencies, settle the item in the other currency first, and declare the Exchange Difference Line on the last request. Otherwise an Assign or an Unassign can answer `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_WITH_REST_ERROR`.

### The rate bound

When a Match, a bulk Match, an Assign, or a Follow-up act settles an item, and a counterpart line is in another currency than the Bank Account's, Sintropix checks the rate the settlement implies. It doesn't matter which line is the target. A [manual entry that settles open lines](/api-reference/ledger-entries#settle-open-lines) in two denominations is checked the same way, against the published rate of its entry date.

- **Implied rate:** the functional amount the Movement pays for the settlement, divided by the units settled. A fee or another item on its own line stays out of it.
- **Bound:** the implied rate may differ from the published rate of the Movement's date by at most 5%.

Within the bound, the write books. Beyond it, or when no rate is published, it's refused with `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_RATE_UNEXPLAINED_ERROR`, unless the request carries `exchangeDifferenceReason` (1 to 500 characters). The refusal carries both rates, the published rate's date and source, the distance, the bound, and the difference it would book.

When you get that refusal, try these in order:

1. Check the units. Settle fewer and leave the rest of the item open.
2. Give a fee or another item its own line.
3. If the bank really applied that rate, send `exchangeDifferenceReason` and say so, citing the bank's document.
4. Otherwise, match without `exchangeDifferenceLineId` and leave the rest on the Suspense Account.

With a reason, the Exchange Difference Line stores it in `exchangeDifferenceReason`, and the published rate it was measured against in `exchangeDifferencePublishedRate` (`null` when none was published). Both appear in the [audit trail](/api-reference/audit-trail).

A reason the write doesn't need is refused with `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_REASON_NOT_NEEDED_ERROR`. `params.cause` says why:

| `cause` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `no_exchange_difference_line` | The request declares no Exchange Difference Line. |
| `not_a_settlement` | The act settles no open item, such as a Book or a conversion. |
| `same_denomination` | Every counterpart line is in the Bank Account's currency. |
| `within_bound` | The implied rate is within 5% of the published rate. |

### Example

A Chilean Entity has a USD 1,000.00 receivable Invoice, booked at 943.25, so it carries CLP 943,250. The customer pays CLP 950,000 into a CLP Bank Account. The Match settles the Invoice's 100000 open units and declares an Exchange Difference Line:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/bank-accounts/$BANK_ACCOUNT_ID/movements/$MOVEMENT_ID/match?dryRun=true" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "4a5b6c7d-8e9f-4a0b-8c1d-2e3f4a5b6c7d",
    "entryDate": "2026-11-12",
    "memo": "Payment of invoice 1077",
    "lineId": "5b6c7d8e-9f0a-4b1c-8d2e-3f4a5b6c7d8e",
    "exchangeDifferenceLineId": "6c7d8e9f-0a1b-4c2d-8e3f-4a5b6c7d8e9f",
    "newCounterpartLines": [
      {
        "id": "7d8e9f0a-1b2c-4d3e-8f4a-5b6c7d8e9f0a",
        "accountId": "2c3d4e5f-6a7b-4c8d-9e0f-1a2b3c4d5e6f",
        "side": "credit",
        "partnerId": "a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a7b-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d",
        "transactionCurrency": "USD",
        "transactionAmount": 100000
      }
    ]
  }'
```

The settlement line takes the Invoice's carrying amount, CLP 943,250. The Exchange Difference Line takes the gap, a CLP 6,750 credit (a gain), on the Entity's Exchange Difference Account. The implied rate is 950.00. If the published rate for 12 November is 948.10, that's within 5%, so the Match needs no reason. Send the same body without `dryRun` to book it.

### Foreign-currency errors

Each error answers `409` with the `code` and the `params` shown. Amounts are functional amounts in minor units.

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_REQUIRED_ERROR` | The valued lines leave a gap, and the request declares no Exchange Difference Line. `params: { gap, exchangeDifferenceSide }`. |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_NOT_ALLOWED_ERROR` | The request declares an Exchange Difference Line on an act that settles nothing and converts nothing. `params: { lineId }`. |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_WITHOUT_GAP_ERROR` | The request declares an Exchange Difference Line, but the valued lines already balance. `params: { lineId }`. |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_WITH_REST_ERROR` | An Assign or an Unassign would leave a rest on the Suspense line beside the Exchange Difference Line while a line is in another currency than the Bank Account's. `params: { lineId }`. |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_LINE_REPEATED_ERROR` | An Assign declares an Exchange Difference Line on a Movement whose Entry already keeps one. `params: { lineIds }`. |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_ACCOUNT_MISSING_ERROR` | The Entity has no Exchange Difference Account for the line's side. `params: { functionalAmount, side }`. |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_LINE_OVERRIDE_ON_SETTLEMENT_ERROR` | A settlement line carries `overrideFxRate`, or a Match or an Assign with an Exchange Difference Line carries a rate of your own on any line. See [Rate overrides and the exchange difference](#rate-overrides-and-the-exchange-difference). `params: { lineId }`, `null` when the rate is on the bank line or the Suspense line. |
| `BANK_MOVEMENT_BANK_LINE_OVERRIDE_FORBIDDEN_ERROR` | `bankLineOverride` on a Bank Account in the functional currency. `params: { bankAccountCurrency }`. |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_SETTLEMENT_PARTIAL_NOT_MEASURABLE_ERROR` | The partial units' share of the carrying amount rounds to `0`, or to all of the open amount. `params: { lineId, openUnits, minimumUnits, maximumPartialUnits }`. |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_RATE_UNEXPLAINED_ERROR` | The implied rate is beyond the bound, or no rate is published, and the request has no `exchangeDifferenceReason`. `params: { lineId, transactionCurrency, impliedRate, publishedRate, publishedRateDate, rateSource, distance, bound, functionalAmount, side }`. `distance` and `bound` are fractions, such as `"0.05"`. |
| `LEDGER_ENTRY_EXCHANGE_DIFFERENCE_REASON_NOT_NEEDED_ERROR` | The request sends `exchangeDifferenceReason` where it isn't needed. `params: { cause }`. |

The valuation of foreign lines can also answer the refusals of a [manual Ledger Entry](/api-reference/ledger-entries#foreign-currency-lines), such as `LEDGER_ENTRY_LINE_FX_RATE_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`.

### In the app

When you match a Movement to a foreign item, the panel proposes the units at the published rate. The proposal is capped at the item's open units, and moved to the nearest partial the item accepts. When that move would cross the 5% bound, the field stays empty, and you type the units the Movement pays. The **Diferencia de cambio** row shows the amount and whether it's a **Ganancia** or a **Pérdida**.

When the bound asks for a reason, the panel shows the implied rate, the published rate, and the distance, and asks for **Motivo de la diferencia de cambio**. **Conciliar** stays disabled until you fill it in.

## Dry run

Add `?dryRun=true` to Book, Match, Assign, or Unassign, single or bulk, to read what Sintropix would book without booking it. It works like the [dry run of a manual entry](/api-reference/ledger-entries#dry-run):

- It runs the whole write, with every check and the valuation, then rolls it back. It answers `200` with the answer the write would give plus `dryRun: true` (on each element of a bulk answer), or with the refusal the write would give.
- It stores nothing. The entry and line ids stay free, so you can send the same body without `dryRun` to book it.
- It counts as a write for permissions, and needs the write's [audit headers](/api-reference/overview).

Read each line's `functionalAmount`, `transactionFxRate`, and `transactionFxRateOrigin`, and the Exchange Difference Line, in `reconciliation.entry.lines`.

## Record an internal transfer

An Internal Transfer moves money between two Bank Accounts of the same Entity, for example from checking to savings or through a currency conversion. It is never income or expense. There is no dedicated transfer endpoint. You record a transfer with `POST /book` on the first leg and `POST /match` on the second.

Each leg of the transfer passes through a bridge account. A bridge account is an open-item Ledger Account with sub-type `internal_transfer` (see [Chart of Accounts](/api-reference/chart-of-accounts)). An open line on the bridge account is money in transit. The Allocation that joins the two bridge lines shows that both legs arrived.

1. Book the first leg, the one that left first, with `POST /movements/:movementId/book`. Put one counterpart line on the bridge account, on the side opposite to the bank line, in the leg's currency: units on a foreign leg, a `functionalAmount` on a domestic one. Give a bank fee its own line. Keep the id of the bridge line.
2. Match the second leg with `POST /movements/:movementId/match`, with `lineId` set to the open bridge line. Its settlement line goes on the bridge account, on the opposite side, in the bridge line's denomination: its `transactionCurrency` and open units when the bridge line is foreign, its open `functionalAmount` when it's domestic. Sintropix values the settlement line at the bridge line's carrying amount.
3. When a leg is foreign, the second leg's bank line is valued at the rate of its own date, so the two legs usually differ in the functional currency. Add `exchangeDifferenceLineId` to the Match, and Sintropix books the difference on the Entity's Exchange Difference Account. See [The Exchange Difference Line](#the-exchange-difference-line). Two domestic legs never need it.

Run each request with [`?dryRun=true`](#dry-run) first to read the valued lines. If only one leg has arrived, book it and leave its bridge line open until the other leg arrives.

If both legs are already booked, you can join the two bridge lines with [Match Lines](/api-reference/allocations) (`POST /api/entities/:entityId/ledger/allocations/match`). Sintropix doesn't refuse unequal bridge lines: the Allocation takes the smaller amount, and the difference stays as an Open Amount on the bridge account.

```bash
# Leg A: money out of checking, into the bridge account
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/bank-accounts/$CHECKING_ID/movements/$MOVEMENT_A_ID/book" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "0c1d2e3f-4a5b-4c6d-8e7f-8a9b0c1d2e3f",
    "entryDate": "2025-12-30",
    "memo": "Transfer to savings",
    "newCounterpartLines": [
      { "id": "1f2a3b4c-5d6e-4f7a-8b9c-0d1e2f3a4b5c", "accountId": "6d7e8f9a-0b1c-4d2e-8f3a-4b5c6d7e8f9a", "functionalAmount": 150000, "side": "debit" }
    ]
  }'

# Leg B: money into savings, settling the bridge line of leg A
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/bank-accounts/$SAVINGS_ID/movements/$MOVEMENT_B_ID/match" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "1d2e3f4a-5b6c-4d7e-8f8a-9b0c1d2e3f4a",
    "entryDate": "2025-12-30",
    "memo": "Transfer from checking",
    "lineId": "1f2a3b4c-5d6e-4f7a-8b9c-0d1e2f3a4b5c",
    "newCounterpartLines": [
      { "id": "2a3b4c5d-6e7f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d", "accountId": "6d7e8f9a-0b1c-4d2e-8f3a-4b5c6d7e8f9a", "functionalAmount": 150000, "side": "credit" }
    ]
  }'
```

## Exclude a movement from the books

Some Movements should never be booked, and deleting a fed Movement doesn't help because the next sync lands it again. Exclude it instead. An **Exclusion** records, with a reason, that the Movement will never be booked. It takes the Movement out of the pending work, and you can lift it at any time.

An excluded Movement:

- Has `status: "excluded"`. A listing with no `status` filter still lists it, and the app crosses those rows out. It's left out of `status=notExcluded`, of `status=unreconciled`, and of `unreconciledCount`.
- Can't be booked, matched, or assigned, single or bulk, and can't hold a [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups). Excluding the Movement clears its Follow-up. These writes are refused with `409` and `BANK_MOVEMENT_EXCLUDED_ERROR`.
- Stays landed. A bank feed sync skips it.

A booked or matched Movement can't be excluded: unbook it first. Anyone who can book a Movement can exclude or include it. A `viewer` is refused with `403`. Each Exclusion and Inclusion appears in the Movement's [audit trail](/api-reference/audit-trail).

### PUT `/movements/:movementId/exclusion`

Exclude an unbooked Movement from the books.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `reason` | string | Yes | Why the Movement will never be booked. Trimmed, non-empty, up to 1,000 characters. |

**Response:** `{ movement, bankAccount }`, with `excludedAt` and `exclusionReason` set on the Movement.

```bash
curl -X PUT "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/bank-accounts/$BANK_ACCOUNT_ID/movements/$MOVEMENT_ID/exclusion" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "reason": "Cargo duplicado por el banco, revertido el mismo día." }'
```

### DELETE `/movements/:movementId/exclusion`

Include an excluded Movement in the books again. It goes back to `unbooked`.

**Response:** `{ movement, bankAccount }`, with `excludedAt` and `exclusionReason` null on the Movement.

### Exclusion errors

| Code | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BANK_MOVEMENT_ALREADY_RECONCILED_ERROR` | `409` | The Movement is booked or matched. Unbook it before you exclude it. |
| `BANK_MOVEMENT_EXCLUDED_ERROR` | `409` | The Movement is already excluded. |
| `BANK_MOVEMENT_NOT_EXCLUDED_ERROR` | `409` | The Movement isn't excluded, so there's nothing to include. |

A blank `reason`, or one longer than 1,000 characters, is refused with `400`. Each error carries the `movementId` in `params`; `BANK_MOVEMENT_ALREADY_RECONCILED_ERROR` also carries the `ledgerEntryId` that books it.

## Status codes

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `200` | Success (`GET`, `PATCH`, `PUT`, and `DELETE`), and a [dry run](#dry-run). |
| `201` | Success of any `POST`, including the actions on an existing Movement such as `/book` and `/match`. |
| `400` | Validation error, including a field the route doesn't define such as `bankLineFxRate`, or missing `x-audit-actor` on a key call. |
| `403` | Read-only key attempted a mutation. |
| `404` | Entity, Bank Account, or Movement not found. |
| `409` | Domain conflict (already booked, already matched, duplicate `id` or `externalRef`, and so on). |
