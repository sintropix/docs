An Integration is a linked external system: the credentials, tokens, and configuration Sintropix stores to reach that system on the Organization's behalf. Every grant to an external account is an Integration, including the banks and payment providers that feed Bank Accounts. Integrations are the credential side. The feeds are the recurring pull jobs that import data through them.

What an Integration can feed depends on its kind:

| Kind | Feeds |
| --- | --- |
| `sii_mirror` | Up to two [Sources](/api-reference/sources), through [Source Feeds](/api-reference/source-feeds): one for DTEs (`dte`) and one for boletas de honorarios (`honorarium`) |
| `bsale`, `wasabil` | A [Source](/api-reference/sources), through a [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds) of DTEs (`dte`) |
| `fintoc`, `plaid`, `global66`, `cardda`, `dlocal`, `mercadopago`, `polar`, `stripe` | One or more [Bank Accounts](/api-reference/bank-accounts), through [bank feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds) |
| `xpendit` | A [Source](/api-reference/sources) that holds Expense Renditions for each of its Subsidiaries, through a [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds#expense-feeds) of expenses (`expense`). Each sync lands the expenses Xpendit approved as approved [Expense Claims](/api-reference/expense-claims). You can also [read its expenses](#read-what-xpendit-holds) on demand |
| `mercadolibre`, `buk`, `notion`, `rho` | Nothing yet |
| `gmail`, `outlook`, `mailbox` | Nothing. You search and read their email on demand instead. See [Read email](#read-email) |
| `google_sheets` | Nothing. It receives the General Ledger instead. See [Google Sheets](#google-sheets) |
| `google_drive` | Nothing. Erwin and the MCP server read the Google Sheet you picked instead. See [Files for Erwin and the MCP](#files-for-erwin-and-the-mcp) |
| `metabase` | Nothing. Erwin and the MCP server read the Metabase questions you choose instead. See [Metabase](#metabase) |

An Organization can hold more than one Integration at a time (for example, a Bsale document feed alongside a Fintoc bank and a Mercado Pago authorization). Each external account can be connected only once.

The `fintoc` kind connects a bank login through the [Fintoc](https://fintoc.com) widget. Only `staff` can connect or reconnect a bank through Fintoc. A `client` connects a bank by storing its [Bank Login](#portal-logins). The `global66` kind connects Global66 with a client id and a client secret, and discovers every active wallet of that client. The `cardda` kind connects a Cardda company with its API key, and the `dlocal` kind connects a dLocal Go merchant with its API key and secret key. Sintropix reads the Provider before it stores the credentials, so credentials that cannot read are refused at connect.

The `plaid` kind connects a US bank through [Plaid](https://plaid.com) Link. Plaid Link shows US banks only, in English, and asks for access to the bank's transactions, with up to 730 days of history. Your bank password goes to Plaid and never reaches Sintropix. It can feed Bank Accounts with the bank's posted transactions. See [`POST /integrations/plaid/link-tokens`](#post-/integrations/plaid/link-tokens) and [Bank Feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds#plaid).

The `mercadopago` kind connects a Mercado Pago account through OAuth. It can feed a Bank Account with the account's available money. See [Bank Feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds#mercado-pago).

The `gmail` and `outlook` kinds connect an email account: a Gmail account or a Microsoft Outlook account. You connect it from the app. Sintropix only reads: it never sends, changes, or deletes email. An Organization can connect several email accounts, but each account can be connected only once. You can [search and read](#read-email) the email of a connected account through the API.

The `mailbox` kind is an email address that Sintropix holds for the Organization, such as `facturas@acme.sintropix.com`. You create it in Sintropix: there's no outside account to connect. An Organization can hold several Mailboxes, all at its own email domain. See [Mailbox](#mailbox).

The `google_sheets` kind connects one Google Sheet through Google OAuth. Sintropix writes the General Ledger of every Entity of the Organization to that Sheet, or the group's Consolidated Ledger, from the Fiscal Year you choose to the current one, every four hours and whenever you ask. See [Google Sheets](#google-sheets).

The `google_drive` kind connects one Google Sheet that you pick in Google's file picker, so that Erwin and the assistants you connect to the [MCP server](/guides/mcp-server) can read it. Sintropix reaches that Sheet only, not the rest of your Drive, and reads it as it is each time someone asks. It never writes to it. See [Files for Erwin and the MCP](#files-for-erwin-and-the-mcp).

The `metabase` kind connects a [Metabase Cloud](https://www.metabase.com/cloud) site with an API key. You choose which of its questions Erwin and the assistants you connect to the MCP server can read, and Sintropix runs a question in Metabase each time someone reads it. It never changes anything in Metabase. See [Metabase](#metabase).

The `xpendit` kind connects the enterprise of an Xpendit account, the expense tool, with its API key. You can read the expenses and funds of each of its Subsidiaries, and route each Subsidiary to a Source of Expense Renditions. See [Xpendit](#xpendit).

The `stripe` kind connects a [Stripe](https://stripe.com) account through OAuth, by installing the Sintropix Stripe App in that account with read-only access. It can feed a Bank Account with each currency of the account's Stripe payments balance. See [Stripe](#stripe) and [Bank Feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds).

The `buk` kind connects a Buk tenant, the payroll system, with its address and an API key. It feeds nothing yet. You can [list the Empresas](#get-/integrations/integrationid/provider-accounts) the key reaches. See [Buk](#buk).

The `rho` kind connects a business on [Rho](https://www.rho.co), the US business banking platform, with a read-only API access token. It feeds nothing yet, but you can preview every account of the business with its current balance, Treasury and rewards accounts included. See [Rho](#rho).

The `notion` kind connects a Notion workspace through OAuth, with read-only access to the pages and databases you share at consent. It feeds nothing yet, but you can preview each database it reaches. See [Notion](#notion).

The `polar` kind connects a [Polar](https://polar.sh) account through OAuth. You grant Sintropix read-only access to the account's organizations, orders, payments, refunds, disputes, transactions, and payouts. If you clear the transactions or payouts permission on Polar's consent screen, Sintropix refuses the connection and stores nothing. Sign in to Polar as a user with the owner, admin, or finance role in the organization: Polar shows an organization's money to those roles only. It can feed a USD Bank Account with each organization's Polar balance. Sintropix only reads: it never charges, refunds, or withdraws money. See [Bank Feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds#polar).

To connect a bank that no Provider reads, such as a credit card Fintoc can't fetch, store its login as a [Bank Login](#portal-logins). To connect the Getnet merchant portal, store its login as a [Getnet Login](#portal-logins). To give Sintropix access to Clay, the Chilean ERP you migrate your books from, store it as a [Clay Login](#portal-logins). None of these is an Integration kind, but the app lists them with the Integrations.

The `sii_mirror` kind connects the SII, the Chilean tax authority, and is the only SII document Provider. You connect it with the company's RUT and its SII password (Clave), and the SII checks them at once. It then reads the RCV registers of that RUT from Sintropix's Chilean SII mirror. The mirror reads the SII registers every 4 hours, so a document reaches it a few hours after the SII records it. See [SII](#sii).

In the app, open **Configuración → Integraciones** and click **Conectar integración**. The catalog lists every option with its logo, and you can search it or filter it by tag: **Banco**, **Pagos**, **Ventas**, **Facturador**, **Impuestos**, **Rendiciones**, **Correo**, **ERP**, or **Remuneraciones**. Each Chilean bank is its own option, and you can find it by its short name too, such as "bci". Pick an option and fill in its form. For a Provider that uses OAuth, such as Google or Mercado Pago, you continue on the Provider's page and return to Sintropix afterwards.

Connecting asks for no Entity. When the connection is stored, its panel opens on the step it still lacks, where you route it to the Entities (**Entidades legales**) of the Organization. For a document Provider, pick the Source each Document Family lands in, from the Sources of every Entity grouped by Entity, and the date to read from. You can also create a Source in any Entity. For a bank Provider, link each of its accounts to a Bank Account of any Entity, or create one in the Entity you pick. Until you finish that step, the list shows the connection as **Falta elegir fuentes** or **Falta elegir cuentas**.

The list shows every connection of the Organization. Each Integration's row names the Entities its feeds reach, or **Sin asignar** until a feed routes it. A Portal Login's row names its own Entity.

## Integrations belong to the Organization

An Integration belongs to the [Organization](/api-reference/organizations), not to one Entity. Its feeds route it: each [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds) or [bank feed](/api-reference/bank-feeds) names the Entity whose Source or Bank Account it fills. One Integration can feed several Entities of its Organization. For example, one bank login can feed a Bank Account in each of two Entities. No tax id decides or limits where a feed lands: any feed can land in any Entity of the Organization.

The routes follow the same split:

- Every Integration route lives under `/api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations`: connect, list, the Fintoc Link Intent, the Plaid Link token, reconnect, the replacement of an API key, the purpose, the Basis, the start, and the mode of a Google Sheets export, the SII legal representative's Clave, the discovered accounts, the Provider Accounts, the General Ledger Export, a Mailbox and its Conversations, the email of a Gmail, Outlook, or Mailbox account, the expenses of an Xpendit account, the accounts of a Rho business, the preview of a Notion database, the files Erwin and the MCP read, and disconnect.
- Every feed route lives under the Entity it lands in, `/api/entities/:entityId/...`. So does the copy of an [email attachment](#post-/api/entities/entityid/email-attachments) into an Entity. They take an Integration of that Entity's Organization. An Integration of another Organization answers `404` with `INTEGRATION_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`.

Connecting, reconnecting, disconnecting, setting the purpose of an Integration, and changing the Basis, the start, or the mode of a Google Sheets export need the `books` write permission in the Organization, which a `client` or `staff` caller holds. A `viewer` can only list Integrations, and can't read the email of a Gmail, Outlook, or Mailbox account, the expenses of an Xpendit account, the preview of a Notion database, or the [files](#who-can-read-the-files) of an Integration. An API key confined to one Entity:

- Lists every Integration of its Organization, but sees only the Sources and Bank Accounts of its own Entity.
- Can't connect, reconnect, renew, or disconnect an Integration, replace its API key, run a General Ledger Export, read or copy the email of a Gmail, Outlook, or Mailbox account, or read the expenses of an Xpendit account, the preview of a Notion database, or the files of an Integration. It gets `403` with `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`.
- Adds and removes only the feeds that land in its own Entity.

An Entity whose feed routes an Integration of its Organization, or whose Basis a Google Sheets Integration exports, can't move to another Organization. See [`PATCH /api/entities/:entityId/organization`](/api-reference/entities#patch-/api/entities/entityid/organization).

## The Integration object

The access token is stored encrypted and is never returned on any read.

| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Integration id. |
| `organizationId` | uuid | Owning Organization. |
| `kind` | `bsale` \| `wasabil` \| `sii_mirror` \| `fintoc` \| `global66` \| `cardda` \| `dlocal` \| `plaid` \| `mercadopago` \| `mercadolibre` \| `gmail` \| `outlook` \| `mailbox` \| `polar` \| `google_sheets` \| `google_drive` \| `metabase` \| `xpendit` \| `stripe` \| `buk` \| `notion` \| `rho` | The external system. |
| `providerRef` | string \| null | The provider's own handle for the connected account (for Bsale, the instance number; for `sii_mirror`, the company RUT the SII accepted at connect, unformatted; for `fintoc`, the Fintoc Link; for `plaid`, the Plaid Item id; for Mercado Pago and Mercado Libre, the account ID; for `gmail` and `outlook`, the account's email address; for `mailbox`, its address; for `polar`, the email address of the Polar user who granted access; for `google_sheets` and `google_drive`, the id of the Google Sheet; for `xpendit`, the id of the Xpendit enterprise; for `stripe`, the Stripe account id, such as `acct_1Q2w3E4r5T6y7U8i`; for `buk`, the tenant, the subdomain of its `buk.cl` address; for `metabase`, the host of the Metabase Cloud site, such as `acme.metabaseapp.com`; for `notion`, the id of the Notion workspace; for `rho`, the id of the Rho business). `null` when a document provider could not determine it at connect time. |

| `institutionName` | string \| null | The bank's display name, as the Provider reports it. Only `fintoc` and `plaid` state it; `null` for every other kind. |

| `accountName` | string \| null | For `stripe`, the business name of the Stripe account, or its Dashboard name when it has no business name, as read at connect. For `google_drive`, the title of the Sheet when it was connected. `null` for every other kind. |
| `country` | string \| null | For `stripe`, the country of the Stripe account, as an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code such as `US`, read at connect. `null` for every other kind. |
| `defaultCurrency` | string \| null | For `stripe`, the default currency of the Stripe account, as an uppercase ISO 4217 code such as `USD`, read at connect. `null` for every other kind. |
| `feedableFamilies` | array | The [Document Families](/api-reference/source-feeds#document-families) this Integration's provider can feed: `["dte", "honorarium"]` for `sii_mirror`, `["dte"]` for `bsale` and `wasabil`, `["expense"]` for `xpendit`. Empty for every kind that feeds no documents. |
| `sourceFeeds` | array | The Source Feeds that read through this Integration, in the shape documented on [Source Feeds](/api-reference/source-feeds). Each Entity holds at most one Feed per family. Empty when the Integration feeds no Source. |
| `fedBankAccounts` | array | `{ id, name, entityId }` of each Bank Account this Integration feeds, in any Entity, in name order. Empty when it feeds none. |
| `routedEntities` | array | `{ id, name }` of each Entity the Integration's feeds reach, in name order. Empty while no feed routes it. |
| `basisId` | uuid \| null | For `google_sheets`, the [Basis](/api-reference/bases) the export reads for the Entity that keeps it. `null` for every other kind, when no Basis was picked, and on a `group` export, which reads no Basis. [Change it](#put-/integrations/integrationid/basis) at any time. |
| `basisRequiredEntityIds` | uuid[] | For `google_sheets`, the Entities that keep Bases and whose Basis the Integration doesn't name. While it isn't empty, the export is refused. See [Pick the Basis](#pick-the-basis). Always empty on a `group` export and for every other kind. |
| `generalLedgerExportStart` | `current_fiscal_year` \| `since_fiscal_year` \| `all_history` \| null | For `google_sheets`, the first Fiscal Year the export writes. `current_fiscal_year` until you [change it](#put-/integrations/integrationid/general-ledger-start). `null` for every other kind. See [Choose where the export starts](#choose-where-the-export-starts). |
| `generalLedgerExportSinceYear` | integer \| null | For `google_sheets` with `generalLedgerExportStart` set to `since_fiscal_year`, the calendar year the first exported Fiscal Year starts in, such as `2024`. `null` otherwise. |
| `generalLedgerExportMode` | `per_entity` \| `group` \| null | For `google_sheets`, what **Libro mayor** holds: each Entity's books (`per_entity`, the default) or the group's Consolidated Ledger (`group`). `null` for every other kind. See [Export the group](#export-the-group). |
| `generalLedgerExportFirstFiscalYear` | integer \| null | For `google_sheets`, the calendar year in which the earliest Fiscal Year with a live Ledger Entry starts, across every Entity the export writes, such as `2024`. A `since_fiscal_year` start earlier than this year writes the same as `all_history`. `null` before the first live Ledger Entry, and always `null` for every other kind. See [Choose where the export starts](#choose-where-the-export-starts). |
| `groupExportRefusal` | object \| null | For `google_sheets`, why the Organization's books can't be exported as one group: `{ reason, entityIds }`, where `reason` is `single_entity`, `entity_keeps_bases`, or `fiscal_years_differ`, and `entityIds` names the Entities the reason is about. `null` when the group can be exported, and always `null` for every other kind. See [Export the group](#export-the-group). |
| `lastGeneralLedgerExport` | object \| null | For `google_sheets`, the last [General Ledger Export](#the-general-ledger-export-object). `null` until the first export runs, and always `null` for every other kind. |
| `scopes` | string[] | The permissions the Provider granted. Set for `mercadopago`, `mercadolibre`, `polar`, `google_sheets`, `google_drive`, and `stripe`; empty for every other kind. |
| `tokenExpiresAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp \| null | When the stored access of a `mercadopago`, `mercadolibre`, `polar`, `google_sheets`, `google_drive`, or `stripe` Integration expires. `null` for every other kind. |
| `purpose` | string \| null | What the account is for, in your own words, such as "Facturas de proveedores de Acme". It helps a person or an agent pick the right account when the Organization holds several. Up to 500 characters. `null` until you [set it](#put-/integrations/integrationid/purpose). |
| `grantHealth` | `working` \| `failed` \| `unknown` \| null | For `gmail` and `outlook`, whether the account's access still works, checked on each list. `failed` means the access expired or was revoked: disconnect the Integration and connect the account again. `unknown` means Sintropix couldn't check it right now. For `mailbox`, `working` when Sintropix reaches its inbox and `unknown` otherwise; a Mailbox holds no access that can expire, so it's never `failed`. `null` for every other kind. |
| `holdsFiles` | boolean | Whether the Integration's kind holds [files for Erwin and the MCP](#files-for-erwin-and-the-mcp): `true` for `google_drive` and `metabase`, `false` for every other kind. Only an Integration with `holdsFiles: true` answers the [file routes](#get-/integrations/integrationid/files). |
| `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | Row timestamps. |

## POST `/integrations`

`POST /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations`

Create an Integration of the Organization by handing Sintropix a provider-issued access token. The token is verified against the provider once, then stored encrypted. Connecting asks for no Entity: route the Integration to Entities afterwards, through its feeds.

This route connects the document Providers, the bank Providers, Xpendit, Buk, Metabase, and Rho. Mercado Pago, Mercado Libre, Gmail, Outlook, Polar, Google Sheets, Google Drive, Stripe, and Notion use OAuth and are connected from **Configuración → Integraciones** in the Sintropix app. You cannot create them through this endpoint. A Mailbox has its own route: [`POST /integrations/mailbox`](#post-/integrations/mailbox).

**Body.** The shape depends on `kind`. Every kind takes an `id`:

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Client-generated id. Retrying the same `POST` with the same id is rejected instead of creating a duplicate. |
| `kind` | `bsale` \| `wasabil` \| `xpendit` \| `buk` \| `metabase` \| `rho` \| `sii_mirror` \| `fintoc` \| `plaid` \| `global66` \| `cardda` \| `dlocal` | Yes | The external system to link. `mercadopago`, `mercadolibre`, `gmail`, `outlook`, `mailbox`, `polar`, `google_sheets`, `google_drive`, `stripe`, and `notion` are not accepted here. |

Each kind adds its own fields:

| Kind | Fields | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bsale`, `wasabil`, `xpendit`, `rho` | `accessToken` (string) | The provider's API access token. For `xpendit`, the API key of the enterprise. For `rho`, an API access token of the Rho business. See [Create the token in Rho](#create-the-token-in-rho). Whitespace is trimmed and must be non-empty. |
| `buk` | `tenant` (string), `accessToken` (string) | The address of your Buk, such as `acme.buk.cl` or a full `https://acme.buk.cl/...` address, and its API key. Sintropix keeps only the subdomain, and sends the key only to that `buk.cl` address. An address outside `buk.cl` is refused with `400`. The key must be non-empty after trimming. |
| `metabase` | `host` (string), `accessToken` (string) | The address of your Metabase Cloud site, such as `acme.metabaseapp.com`, just `acme`, or any `https://acme.metabaseapp.com/...` address, and a Metabase API key. Sintropix keeps the host, `acme.metabaseapp.com`, and sends the key only to that host. An address outside `metabaseapp.com` is refused with `400`, so a self-hosted Metabase can't connect. The key must be non-empty after trimming. See [Metabase](#metabase). |
| `sii_mirror` | `rut` (string), `clave` (string) | The company's RUT and its SII password (Clave). The RUT takes the same format and check-digit rule as a [Portal Login](#post-/portal-logins) `rut`. The Clave must be non-empty. See [SII](#sii). |
| `fintoc` | `exchangeToken` (string) | The token the Fintoc widget mints after you [create a link intent](#post-/integrations/fintoc/link-intents) and the user completes the widget. |
| `plaid` | `publicToken` (string) | The public token Plaid Link mints after you [create a Link token](#post-/integrations/plaid/link-tokens) and the user signs in to the bank. Connecting the same Plaid Item twice is refused with `409`. |
| `global66` | `clientId` (string), `clientSecret` (string) | Your Global66 client credentials. The Integration [discovers every active wallet](/api-reference/bank-feeds#global66) of that client, so you don't name the accounts. A client id can be connected only once: connecting it again answers `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR`. |
| `cardda` | `apiKey` (string) | The Cardda API key. It selects the company, and the Integration discovers that company's one CLP wallet. |
| `dlocal` | `apiKey` (string), `secretKey` (string) | The dLocal Go API key and its paired secret key. They name the merchant, and the Integration discovers its one balance. |

For `sii_mirror`, the SII checks the RUT and Clave before Sintropix creates the Integration, so the request can take up to about a minute. A Clave the SII refuses answers `422` with `SII_CLAVE_REFUSED_ERROR`. When the SII gives no answer in time, the request answers `503` with `SII_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR` and creates nothing. Retry in a few minutes. When the SII answers the sign-in with a page Sintropix doesn't recognize, the request answers `424` with `SII_ANSWER_UNRECOGNIZED_ERROR` and creates nothing. `params.siiAnswer` carries the text of the SII's page.

Credentials the Provider refuses answer `422` with `INTEGRATION_CONNECT_REJECTED_ERROR`. An API key Xpendit, Buk, or Metabase refuses, or a token Rho refuses, answers `422` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR`. A Rho token that lacks a scope Sintropix needs answers `422` with `INTEGRATION_CONNECT_REJECTED_ERROR`. A Buk address that holds no tenant, or a key that reaches no Empresa, answers `422` with `INTEGRATION_CONNECT_REJECTED_ERROR`. So does a Metabase address that holds no site, a Metabase key in the Administrators group, or a Metabase key that reaches no question. An external account another Integration already holds answers `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR`. See [An account that's already connected](#an-account-thats-already-connected).

- **Response** (`201`): the Integration object (its `sourceFeeds` and `fedBankAccounts` are empty on creation).

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "d3e4f5a6-b7c8-4d9e-8f0a-1b2c3d4e5f6a",
    "kind": "bsale",
    "accessToken": "b7f3c9e1a2d4f6081e3c5a7b9d1f2e4c"
  }'
```

Connect the SII with the company's RUT and Clave:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
```

### An account that's already connected

An external account, such as a Global66 client, a Fintoc Link, a Bsale instance, or an SII RUT, can be held by only one Integration across Sintropix. A connect or reconnect that names an account another Integration already holds creates nothing. It answers `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR`, and `params` describes the account:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kind` | string | The Integration kind of the refused connect, such as `global66` or `bsale`. |
| `providerRef` | string | The Provider's handle for the account, as the [Integration object](#the-integration-object) states it. For some kinds, such as `global66`, it's an opaque value, so don't show it to people. |
| `institutionName` | string \| null | The bank's display name for a `fintoc` or `plaid` connect; `null` for every other kind. |
| `connectedInThisOrganization` | boolean | `true` when an Integration of this Organization already holds the account. `false` when another Organization holds it. The other Organization is never named. |

```json
{
  "code": "INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR",
  "params": {
    "kind": "global66",
    "providerRef": "1fca61e0b9d24c7a8e35f0a6c2d1b4e95f7a3c8d0e2b6f9a4c1d7e3b5a8f5871",
    "institutionName": null,
    "connectedInThisOrganization": true
  }
}
```

What to do depends on where the account is connected:

- **In this Organization** (`connectedInThisOrganization: true`): use the Integration you already have. Find it in [`GET /integrations`](#get-/integrations).
- **In another Organization** (`connectedInThisOrganization: false`): disconnect it in that Organization first, then connect it here.

In the app, the message names the account the way the **Integraciones** list does, such as *Global66*, the bank of a Fintoc or Plaid link (*Banco de Chile*), or the Provider with its readable reference (*Bsale (Instancia 72433)*). For example: *"Esta cuenta de Global66 ya está conectada en esta empresa. Úsala desde la lista de integraciones."* or *"Esta cuenta de Global66 ya está conectada en otra empresa. Desconéctala allí para conectarla aquí."*

After you connect a bank Provider, list its accounts and feed them into Bank Accounts of any Entity. See [Bank Feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds). After you connect a document Provider, feed a Source of any Entity with it. See [Source Feeds](/api-reference/source-feeds#post-/source-feeds).

## DELETE `/integrations/:integrationId`

`DELETE /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId`

Remove an Integration. A [Mailbox](#a-mailbox-stays) can't be removed. Every feed of the Integration is removed as part of the same operation, in every Entity: its Source Feeds and the feed of each Bank Account it fed. The Sources, the Bank Accounts, and the documents and Movements already landed stay.

- For a `fintoc` Integration, Sintropix also deletes the Link at Fintoc, so the bank stops being read.
- For a `plaid` Integration, Sintropix first removes the bank login at Plaid. If Plaid already removed it, the disconnect still succeeds. If Plaid fails or can't be reached, the disconnect answers `424` or `503` and the Integration stays. Retry later.
- For a `sii_mirror` Integration, the SII mirror first deletes the company Clave and the legal representative's Clave, and stops reading that RUT. If the mirror is busy with that RUT or can't be reached, the disconnect answers `503` with `SII_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR`, and the Integration and both Claves stay. Retry in a few minutes.
- For a `stripe` Integration, Sintropix first uninstalls the Sintropix Stripe App from the account. If the account already uninstalled it, the disconnect still succeeds. If Stripe fails, the disconnect answers `424` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_FAILURE_ERROR` and the Integration stays. Retry later.
- For a `notion` Integration, Sintropix first revokes the grant at Notion. If Notion already removed it, the disconnect still succeeds. If Notion fails, the disconnect answers `424` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_FAILURE_ERROR` and the Integration stays. Retry later.
- A `mailbox` Integration is refused with `409` (`MAILBOX_NOT_REMOVABLE_ERROR`), because removing it would delete its email. See [A Mailbox stays](#a-mailbox-stays).
- For every other kind, Sintropix removes the stored credentials or authorization only. The grant on the provider's side is not revoked. For `google_sheets`, the Sheet keeps the last General Ledger written to it.

**Response**

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `deletedIntegrationId` | uuid | The removed Integration. |
| `unfedBankAccounts` | array | Every Bank Account that lost its feed, in any Entity, in the shape documented on [Bank Accounts](/api-reference/bank-accounts) without the summary fields, and with `feed` set to `null`. Empty when the Integration fed no Bank Account. |

```bash
curl -X DELETE "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/$INTEGRATION_ID" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent"
```

```json
{
  "deletedIntegrationId": "0192a8b4-0000-7000-8000-000000000001",
  "unfedBankAccounts": []
}
```

## SII

The SII Integration (`sii_mirror`) reads the company's DTEs and boletas de honorarios from the SII through Sintropix's SII mirror. Sintropix stores the company RUT as the Integration's `providerRef`, and none of the Clave. The Clave is held by the SII mirror, which signs in to the SII with it.

### Connect the SII

In the app, open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **SII**. Type the company's **RUT** and its **Clave del SII**, then click **Conectar**. While the SII checks the Clave, the form shows **Verificando la Clave con el SII…**. A refused Clave shows **Clave incorrecta** under the field. When the SII answers with a page Sintropix doesn't recognize, the form says so and quotes what the SII says, and nothing is saved. Read the SII's text to see what it asks, sort it out in the SII, then connect again.

Through the API, send `kind: "sii_mirror"` with `rut` and `clave` to [`POST /integrations`](#post-/integrations). The SII checks the Clave before the Integration exists:

| Outcome | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The SII accepts the RUT and Clave | `201` with the Integration |
| The SII refuses the Clave | `422` with `SII_CLAVE_REFUSED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored |
| The SII gives no answer in time, or the mirror is busy with that RUT | `503` with `SII_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR`. Nothing is stored. Retry in a few minutes |
| The SII answers with a page Sintropix doesn't recognize | `424` with `SII_ANSWER_UNRECOGNIZED_ERROR`. `params.siiAnswer` carries the SII's own text, such as a notice that asks you to act in the SII. Nothing is stored |
| Another Integration already reads that RUT | `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR` |

The connect asks for no Entity, and the Entity's tax id doesn't need to match the RUT. Route the documents with a [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds#sii-mirror-feeds) on the Entity they belong to. Each document a feed lands names the connected RUT, with the Entity's name, as the company's side: the issuer of a sale and the receiver of a purchase.

The app doesn't offer the [SII Login](#portal-logins) for new connections. SII Logins stored before keep showing in the list.

### The legal representative's Clave

The company Clave reads the RCV registers. The SII opens the PDF and XML of the DTEs only to the legal representative, so add the legal representative's own RUT and Clave to receive those [files](/api-reference/source-feeds#file-attachments). It's optional: the company Clave alone connects the SII. A legal representative who also represents other taxpayers can add their Clave too: when the SII asks them how to sign in, Sintropix keeps their own session.

In the app, open **Conectar integración** and pick **Clave del representante legal**, under the **Impuestos** tag. The option is dimmed until the company's SII is connected. When the Organization has two or more SII Integrations, the dialog first asks which company, by RUT. The option shows a check once the SII mirror holds a Clave or a certificate of the legal representative of every connected company. While some company still has none, the dialog offers only those companies.

The panel of the SII shows **RUT del representante legal**: the RUT of the legal representative the SII mirror holds, which you can copy. The panel never shows the Clave. With no legal representative there's no such row.

To change the legal representative or their Clave, open the panel of the SII and pick **Cambiar Clave del representante legal** from its actions menu. You can also click **Clave del representante legal** in **Conectar integración** once it shows its check. With two or more SII Integrations, pick the company's RUT first. The dialog starts with the current representative's RUT, which you can edit, and an empty Clave. Type the Clave and click **Cambiar Clave**. When the SII accepts it, the new RUT and Clave replace the old ones and the panel shows the new RUT. When the SII refuses it, the dialog shows **Clave incorrecta** beside the Clave and keeps the previous representative. When the SII answers with a page Sintropix doesn't recognize, the dialog quotes what the SII says and keeps the previous representative. When no representative is declared, the same menu offers **Agregar Clave del representante legal** instead.

Disconnecting the SII deletes the legal representative's Clave together with the company Clave.

#### PUT `/integrations/:integrationId/sii-representative-clave`

`PUT /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId/sii-representative-clave`

Add the legal representative's Clave to an SII Integration. The SII checks it at once, the same way as the company Clave. Sintropix stores neither the RUT nor the Clave. Each SII Integration holds one legal representative. On an Integration that already has one, the same request replaces it: the new `rut` and `clave` take the place of the old ones once the SII accepts the new Clave, even when `rut` names a different person. A refused Clave keeps the representative the SII mirror held.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rut` | string | Yes | The legal representative's own RUT, as a person. Same format and check-digit rule as a [Portal Login](#post-/portal-logins) `rut`. The company's RUT is refused. |
| `clave` | string | Yes | The legal representative's SII password (Clave). Must be non-empty. |

**Response:** the Integration object.

| Status | Code | When it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `422` | `SII_REPRESENTATIVE_RUT_IS_TAXPAYER_RUT_ERROR` | `rut` is the company's own RUT. Nothing is sent to the SII |
| `422` | `SII_CLAVE_REFUSED_ERROR` | The SII refused the Clave |
| `422` | `INTEGRATION_REPRESENTATIVE_CLAVE_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR` | The Integration isn't `sii_mirror` |
| `424` | `SII_ANSWER_UNRECOGNIZED_ERROR` | The SII answered the sign-in with a page Sintropix doesn't recognize. `params.siiAnswer` carries the SII's own text. Nothing is stored |
| `503` | `SII_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR` | The SII gave no answer in time. Retry in a few minutes |

```bash
curl -X PUT "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/$INTEGRATION_ID/sii-representative-clave" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "rut": "12.345.678-5",
    "clave": "representative-sii-clave"
  }'
```

## Gmail and Outlook

A `gmail` or `outlook` Integration connects one email account of the Organization. Sintropix keeps no copy of the account's email. You [search and read it](#read-email) on demand, and you can copy an attachment into an Entity, for example to attach a supplier's PDF to its Invoice. Sintropix only reads: it never sends, changes, or deletes email.

### Connect an email account

You connect an email account from the app, because Google and Microsoft ask for consent in a browser:

1. Open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Gmail** or **Outlook**, under the **Correo** tag.
2. Continue on Composio, which holds the account's access for Sintropix, and allow access on Google or Microsoft with the account you want to connect.

Google asks for full access to the mailbox, but Sintropix only reads it. Outlook asks for read access to the mail only.

For a step-by-step guide written for the account owner, see [Connect Gmail](/guides/connect-gmail).

## Mailbox

A `mailbox` Integration is an email address that Sintropix holds for the Organization, such as `facturas@acme.sintropix.com`. Your clients and suppliers send or forward invoices and other documents to it, and you read them in Sintropix. There's no outside account to connect: Sintropix creates the address and receives its email. Sintropix doesn't turn that email into documents.

- A **Conversation** is a chain of emails on one subject, like a thread in an email client.
- An **email** is one message in a Conversation, received by the Mailbox or sent from it.

An Organization can hold as many Mailboxes as it needs, for example `facturas@` for supplier invoices and `rendiciones@` for expense receipts. Give each one a [purpose](#put-/integrations/integrationid/purpose) so a colleague or Erwin can tell them apart.

### The email domain

Every Mailbox of an Organization takes its address at the Organization's **email domain**: a subdomain of `sintropix.com` that belongs to that Organization alone, such as `acme.sintropix.com`. Sintropix sets it for you. A Superadmin picks it for the Organization in the staff panel, with [`PATCH /organizations/:organizationId/email-domain`](/api-reference/organizations#patch-/organizations/organizationid/email-domain). The Organization's `emailDomain` is `null` until then, and the Organization can't create a Mailbox. Ask Sintropix to set it.

Once a Mailbox lives at the domain, the domain can't change.

### Create a Mailbox

1. Open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Casilla de correo**, under the **Correo** tag.
2. Enter the **Nombre de la casilla**: the part of the address before `@`.
3. Click **Crear casilla**.

The Mailbox's panel opens with its address, its purpose, and **Abrir correo**. When the Organization has no email domain yet, the form says *Esta empresa aún no tiene dominio de correo. Pídelo a Sintropix.*

Choose the name carefully: you can't rename a Mailbox, and you can't remove it.

To have Gmail send your invoices to a Mailbox automatically, see [Forward invoices from Gmail](/guides/gmail-forwarding).

### POST `/integrations/mailbox`

`POST /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/mailbox`

Create a Mailbox at the Organization's email domain. It needs the `books` write permission in the Organization, which a `client` or `staff` caller holds. An API key confined to one Entity gets `403` with `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`.

**Body.** Unknown keys are refused with `400`.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Client-generated id of the new Integration. An id already in use is refused with `409` (`INTEGRATION_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`). |
| `name` | string | Yes | The part of the address before `@`. Up to 64 characters: lowercase letters and digits, in groups joined by a single `.` or `-`, such as `facturas` or `compras.chile`. Anything else is refused with `400`. |

- An address that already exists is refused with `409` (`MAILBOX_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`). `params.address` names it. Choose another name.

**Response** `201`: the Integration object, with `kind: "mailbox"` and the new address as `providerRef`.

In the app, click **Abrir correo** in the Mailbox's panel. Its Conversations open newest first. Click one to read its emails and download the attachments of the emails it received.

### A Mailbox stays

You can't disconnect a Mailbox, because removing it would delete its email. [`DELETE /integrations/:integrationId`](#delete-/integrations/integrationid) on a Mailbox answers `409` (`MAILBOX_NOT_REMOVABLE_ERROR`), and the app shows no disconnect option in its panel. As a result, an Organization that holds a Mailbox can't be [deleted](/api-reference/organizations#delete-/organizations/organizationid).

## Read email

You read the email of a `gmail`, `outlook`, or `mailbox` Integration with the same routes: search it, read an email or its whole Conversation as plain text, and copy an attachment into an Entity. Erwin can read all of them too, so you can ask him to find a supplier's invoice in any of the Organization's email accounts. He also searches them on his own as evidence when he reviews a record or leaves a Follow-up, and their [purpose](#put-/integrations/integrationid/purpose) tells him which account to look in. See [Erwin](/core-concepts). A Mailbox reads with a few differences, listed in [Read a Mailbox's email](#read-a-mailbox’s-email).

### Who can read email

Reading the email of the Organization's accounts is more than reading its books, so it has its own rule:

| Caller | Search and read email | Copy an attachment into an Entity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `staff` or `client` | Yes | Yes |
| `viewer` | No | No |
| An API key confined to one Entity | No | No |

A refused caller gets `403` with `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`.

Sintropix logs each email whose content it hands out and each attachment it copies, under the request's [Audit Envelope](/api-reference/audit-trail). A search isn't logged, because it returns only headers and a short snippet. Reading an email with an API key needs `x-audit-actor` and `x-audit-reason`, as a write does.

### The Email object

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | string | The email's id at Google or Microsoft, or a [Mailbox email's id](#read-a-mailbox’s-email). Use it to read the email or copy an attachment. |
| `conversationId` | string | The id of the email's Conversation: the thread it belongs to. |
| `receivedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | When the email arrived. |
| `from` | object \| null | `{ name, address }` of the sender. `name` can be `null`. |
| `to`, `cc` | array | `{ name, address }` of each recipient. |
| `subject` | string | The subject. |
| `attachments` | array | `{ id, filename, contentType, byteSize }` of each attachment. Use `id` to [copy it](#post-/api/entities/entityid/email-attachments). |
| `snippet` | string | On a search only: the first words of the body. |
| `body` | string | On a read only: the body as plain text. HTML is removed. A body over 20,000 characters is cut and ends with `[The body goes on; it was cut here.]`. |

### GET `/integrations/:integrationId/emails`

`GET /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId/emails`

Search the email of a Gmail, Outlook, or Mailbox account, newest first. The search covers received and sent email. It leaves out drafts, spam, and deleted email.

**Query parameters.** Send at least one criterion. The criteria combine with AND, and each text criterion matches whole words, as the mailbox's own search does. Sintropix keeps only the letters, digits, and `@._-` of each term, so a term can't carry Gmail or Outlook search syntax.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `from` | string | No | Words of the sender's name or address. 1 to 200 characters. |
| `to` | string | No | Words of a recipient's name or address, in To or Cc. 1 to 200 characters. |
| `subject` | string | No | Words of the subject. 1 to 200 characters. |
| `text` | string | No | Words anywhere in the subject or body. 1 to 200 characters. |
| `after` | date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | No | Only email received on or after this day, in Santiago (Chile) time. |
| `before` | date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | No | Only email received before this day, in Santiago (Chile) time. Must be later than `after`. |
| `hasAttachment` | boolean | No | `true` for only email with attachments, `false` for only email without. |
| `attachmentType` | `pdf` \| `xml` \| `image` \| `spreadsheet` \| `any` | No | Only email with an attachment of this type. Can't be combined with `hasAttachment=false`. |
| `limit` | integer | No | Page size, 1 to 50. Default `10`. A Mailbox page holds at most 10. |
| `cursor` | string | No | The `nextCursor` of the previous page. Send the same criteria with it. |

A search with no criterion, an `after` that isn't before `before`, or an `attachmentType` with `hasAttachment=false` answers `400`.

**Response**

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `emails` | array | Email objects with `snippet` and no `body`, newest first. |
| `nextCursor` | string \| null | Pass it as `cursor` for the next, older page. `null` on the last page. A page can hold fewer emails than `limit` and still have a next page. |

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/$INTEGRATION_ID/emails?from=proveedora&attachmentType=pdf&after=2026-10-01" \
```

```json
      "from": { "name": "Proveedora Andina SpA", "address": "facturas@proveedora.cl" },
      "to": [{ "name": null, "address": "pagos@acme.cl" }],
      "cc": [],
      "subject": "Factura 10452",
      "attachments": [
        { "id": "1.2", "filename": "factura-10452.pdf", "contentType": "application/pdf", "byteSize": 84211 }
      ],
      "snippet": "Adjuntamos la factura 10452 por el despacho de octubre."
    }

```

### POST `/api/entities/:entityId/email-attachments`

Copy one attachment of an email into an Entity, then attach it to a record of that Entity. The copy lands where an [upload](/api-reference/attachments#two-phase-upload-flow) lands before it is confirmed:

1. Copy the attachment with this route. It answers an `attachmentId`.
2. Attach it to an Invoice, a Bank Movement, or a Ledger Entry of the same Entity with that host's [confirm route](/api-reference/attachments#confirm-an-upload), sending `attachmentId` and `filename`.

The route lives under the Entity because it writes to that Entity, so it needs the `books` write permission as well as the right to [read email](#who-can-read-email). The Integration must belong to the Entity's Organization.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `integrationId` | uuid | Yes | The Gmail, Outlook, or Mailbox Integration. |
| `emailId` | string | Yes | The `id` of the email. |
| `attachmentId` | string | Yes | The `id` of the attachment, from the email's `attachments`. |

The file must be one of the [accepted content types](/api-reference/attachments#two-phase-upload-flow), up to 25 MB. When the email sends it as a generic binary file, Sintropix takes its type from the file name's extension, such as `.pdf` or `.xml`.

**Response** (`201`)

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `attachmentId` | uuid | Pass it to the confirm route of the record. |
| `filename` | string | The attachment's file name. Pass it to the confirm route too. |
| `contentType` | string | The stored content type. |
| `byteSize` | integer | The file size in bytes. |
| `url` | string | A link that opens the copy for a few minutes. |

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/email-attachments" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Attach the supplier's PDF to invoice 10452" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "integrationId": "1b2c3d4e-5f6a-4b7c-8d9e-0f1a2b3c4d5e",
    "emailId": "18f2a7c3d4e5b6a1",
    "attachmentId": "1.2"
  }'
```

An attachment the email doesn't hold answers `404` with `INTEGRATION_EMAIL_ATTACHMENT_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`.

### Read a Mailbox's email

The routes above read a Mailbox with the same contract, with these differences:

- **Ids.** A Mailbox email's `id` joins two UUIDs with `+`, such as `4f1c2b7e-9a3d-4e5f-8b6a-0c1d2e3f4a5b+9b8a7c6d-5e4f-4a3b-8c2d-1e0f9a8b7c6d`. Use it as you receive it. Its `conversationId` is the id the [Conversation routes](#read-a-mailbox’s-conversations) take.
- **Page size.** A search page holds at most 10 emails. A larger `limit` still reads 10. Each Conversation appears at most once on a page: the email of it that matched.
- **What a search covers.** Received, archived, and sent email. Spam and deleted email are left out. A new email can take about a minute to show up in a search. When more than 10,000 Conversations match, the search covers the 10,000 with the newest match, so narrow the criteria.
- **Recipients.** The `to` of a received email lists the addresses the email was delivered to, not its full To line.
- **Attachment types.** `contentType` comes from the file name's extension. It's empty when Sintropix doesn't accept that type.
- **Copy.** Only the attachments of a received email can be copied. An attachment of an email the Mailbox sent answers `404` (`INTEGRATION_EMAIL_ATTACHMENT_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`).
- **Refusals.** A Mailbox holds no access that can expire, so it never answers `INTEGRATION_GRANT_FAILED_ERROR`. When the Mailbox can't be read, the routes answer `424` (`INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_FAILURE_ERROR`) or `503` (`INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNREACHABLE_ERROR` or `INTEGRATION_NOT_CONFIGURED_ERROR`). Retry later.

### Email errors

| Status | Code | When it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `403` | `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR` | A `viewer`, or an API key confined to one Entity, reads or copies email |
| `404` | `INTEGRATION_NOT_FOUND_ERROR` | The Integration doesn't exist in the Organization, or, on a copy, in the Entity's Organization |
| `404` | `INTEGRATION_EMAIL_NOT_FOUND_ERROR` | The account holds no email with that id. `params.integrationId` and `params.emailId` |
| `404` | `INTEGRATION_EMAIL_ATTACHMENT_NOT_FOUND_ERROR` | The email holds no attachment with that id. `params.integrationId`, `params.emailId`, and `params.attachmentId` |
| `422` | `INTEGRATION_EMAIL_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR` | The Integration isn't `gmail`, `outlook`, or `mailbox`. `params.kind` names its kind |
| `424` | `INTEGRATION_GRANT_FAILED_ERROR` | The account's access expired or was revoked, as `grantHealth: "failed"` shows in the list. Disconnect the Integration and connect the account again |
| `424` | `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_FAILURE_ERROR` | Google or Microsoft refused the request, or the Mailbox can't be read right now |
| `503` | `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNREACHABLE_ERROR` | Google, Microsoft, or the Mailbox can't be reached. Retry later |
| `503` | `INTEGRATION_NOT_CONFIGURED_ERROR` | Sintropix can't read Mailboxes right now. Retry later |

## Google Sheets

A Google Sheets Integration keeps a copy of the Organization's General Ledger in a Google Sheet you own, with every Entity of the Organization in the same tabs. It writes each Entity's books by default, or the group's Consolidated Ledger in one presentation currency when you [export the group](#export-the-group). Use it to work on the ledger with Sheets formulas, or to share it with someone who has no access to Sintropix. Sintropix only writes: nothing you change in the Sheet reaches the books.

Each Integration exports to one Sheet, and each Sheet can be connected only once. One Google account can connect Sheets for several Organizations.

<Note>
  A `client` or `staff` caller can connect a Google Sheet and run an export. An API key confined to one Entity can't run an export, because it writes every Entity of the Organization. See [Access and roles](/api-reference/overview#access-and-roles).
</Note>

### Connect a Google Sheet

You connect Google Sheets from the app, because Google asks for consent in a browser:

1. Open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Google Sheets**.
2. Paste the address of the Sheet, as your browser shows it with the Sheet open. It starts with `https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/`.
3. If an Entity of the Organization keeps [Bases](/api-reference/bases), pick the **Base contable** the export reads for that Entity.
4. Continue on Google with an account that can edit the Sheet, and keep the permission to edit your spreadsheets checked.

When you return, Sintropix checks that the account can edit the Sheet by creating its tab, and connects the Integration. It refuses the connection, and stores nothing, when:

- The address isn't the address of a Google Sheet.
- Google can't find the Sheet with the account you authorized.
- The account can view the Sheet but can't edit it. Share the Sheet with that account as an editor, then connect it again.
- Another Integration already exports to that Sheet.

### What the Sheet holds

Each export rewrites three tabs whole: **Libro mayor**, **Plan de cuentas**, and **Dimensiones**. A `group` export also writes the cells it owns in **Tipos de cambio**. See [Export the group](#export-the-group). Sintropix finds each tab by its title, creates a missing one, and leaves the rest of the Sheet alone. If you rename or delete a tab, the next export creates a new one with that title.

Every Entity of the Organization goes into the same tabs, one Entity after another, in name order. Each Entity's rows are in that Entity's own currency.

The export reads the Basis you picked for the Entity that keeps it. It reads every other Entity with no Basis. See [Pick the Basis](#pick-the-basis).

#### Libro mayor

This section describes **Libro mayor** in a `per_entity` export. A `group` export writes the same tab with more columns and rows. See [Libro mayor in a group export](#libro-mayor-in-a-group-export).

**Libro mayor** holds the General Ledger of each Entity, one [Fiscal Year](/api-reference/reports#the-fiscal-year-in-reports) after another, from the [start you choose](#choose-where-the-export-starts) to the current Fiscal Year, on the calendar of Santiago (Chile). By default it holds the current Fiscal Year only. It reads the live Ledger Entries (`active` and `reversed`), the same lines as the [general ledger report](/api-reference/reports#general-ledger). Each Fiscal Year of each Entity is its own section, oldest first, with one row per line, sorted by account code, then by date:

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Entidad legal** | The Entity's name |
| **Fecha** | The entry date, as a Sheets date |
| **Asiento** | The Ledger Entry, linked to the entry in the app |
| **Fecha de creación** | When the entry was written, as a Sheets date and time in Santiago (Chile) |
| **Código** | The Ledger Account code |
| **Cuenta** | The Ledger Account name |
| **Glosa** | The entry memo |
| **Contacto** | The [Partner](/api-reference/partners) on the line |
| **ID fiscal** | The Partner's tax id |
| **País** | The Partner's country, as an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code |
| **Tipo de contacto** | `Proveedor`, `Cliente`, or `Ambos`: what the row's Entity's books say the Partner is to it. Empty when they say neither |
| One column per [Dimension](/api-reference/dimensions) | The name of the Dimension Value the line carries for that Dimension, or empty. Every Dimension of the Organization takes a column, in name order, even one no line uses |
| **Moneda** | The Entity's currency, which **Débito**, **Crédito**, and **Saldo** are in |
| **Débito** | The debit amount |
| **Crédito** | The credit amount |
| **Saldo** | The account's running balance after this line, signed by the account's normal side |
| **Moneda transacción** | The line's transaction currency. The Entity's currency for a line booked in it |
| **Monto transacción** | The line's amount in its transaction currency |
| **Tipo de cambio** | The line's exchange rate. `1` for a line booked in the Entity's currency |

Amounts and rates are numbers in currency units, not minor units, so Sheets formulas work on them.

Each account of each Entity opens each Fiscal Year with a **Saldo inicial** row dated the first day of that Fiscal Year. It holds what the account carries into the Fiscal Year, in **Saldo** and as its own transaction at a rate of `1`: `0` on an income or expense account, the result of the closed years on the retained earnings account, and the balance before the year on any other account. So a balance-sheet account carries its balance from one year's section into the next, and an income or expense account starts each year at `0`. The row has no entry, creation date, Partner, Dimension Value, debit, or credit. An account with no line in a Fiscal Year shows that row alone when its balance isn't zero, and doesn't show at all when its balance is zero.

#### Plan de cuentas

**Plan de cuentas** holds one row per Ledger Account of each Entity, sorted by code:

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Entidad** | The Entity's name |
| **Código** | The account code |
| **Nombre** | The account name |
| **Tipo** | The account type, in Spanish: `Activo`, `Pasivo`, `Patrimonio`, `Ingreso`, `Costo de ventas`, `Gasto operacional`, `Otros ingresos`, or `Otros gastos` |
| **Grupo** | The account's own group, as its code prefix and its name |
| **Descripción** | The account description |

#### Dimensiones

**Dimensiones** holds one row per Dimension Value of the Organization, sorted by Dimension name, then by code. A Dimension with no value has no row.

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Dimensión** | The Dimension's name |
| **Código** | The value's code |
| **Valor** | The value's name |
| **Código padre** | The code of the value's parent, or empty |

Because every export rewrites the whole tabs, running it again is safe: an entry posted, changed, or reversed since the last export shows up on the next one.

### Pick the Basis

An Entity that keeps [Bases](/api-reference/bases) is read through one of them. The Integration names one Basis, and the export reads it for the Entity that keeps it. While an Entity of the Organization keeps Bases and the Integration names none of its Bases, the Sheet waits for a Basis:

- The Integration's `basisRequiredEntityIds` lists those Entities.
- The Integrations list shows **Falta elegir la base contable** on the Integration's row, and its panel opens with a step that explains why and holds the Basis picker.
- The scheduled export skips the Integration and records no run.
- **Sincronizar ahora**, or [`POST /integrations/:integrationId/general-ledger-exports`](#post-/integrations/integrationid/general-ledger-exports), is refused with `400` and `GENERAL_LEDGER_EXPORT_BASIS_REQUIRED_ERROR`. The panel says *La planilla no se puede sincronizar sin base contable. Elige una y vuelve a sincronizar.*

This happens, for example, when an Entity creates its first Basis after you connected the Sheet. You don't need to reconnect: pick the Basis in the Integration's panel, under the General Ledger export section, where the picker lists the Bases grouped by Entity, or call [`PUT /integrations/:integrationId/basis`](#put-/integrations/integrationid/basis). The next export reads it.

Because the Integration names one Basis only, the export stays refused while more than one Entity of the Organization keeps Bases.

A `group` export reads every Entity with no Basis, so it never waits for one. It is [refused](#export-the-group) instead while any Entity keeps Bases.

### PUT `/integrations/:integrationId/basis`

`PUT /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId/basis`

Set or change the Basis a Google Sheets Integration exports. An export already writing keeps the Basis it started with. The next export reads the new one.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `basisId` | uuid | Yes | A [Basis](/api-reference/bases) of any Entity of the Organization. Unknown keys are refused with `400`. |

- **Response:** the Integration object, with its new `basisId` and `basisRequiredEntityIds`.

An Integration that isn't `google_sheets` answers `422` with `GENERAL_LEDGER_EXPORT_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR`. A `group` Integration answers `422` with `GENERAL_LEDGER_GROUP_EXPORT_TAKES_NO_BASIS_ERROR`: [switch it](#put-/integrations/integrationid/general-ledger-mode) to `per_entity` first. A Basis that isn't in the Organization answers `404` with `BASIS_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`.

```bash
curl -X PUT "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/$INTEGRATION_ID/basis" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Export the tax Basis to the ledger Sheet" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "basisId": "3c4d5e6f-7a8b-4c9d-8e0f-1a2b3c4d5e6f" }'
```

### Choose where the export starts

By default, **Libro mayor** holds the current Fiscal Year only, and it moves to the new Fiscal Year when that one starts. To include earlier years, choose a start in the Integration's panel, in the **Libro mayor desde** field above **Sincronizar ahora**, or call [`PUT /integrations/:integrationId/general-ledger-start`](#put-/integrations/integrationid/general-ledger-start). The panel saves your choice as soon as you pick it, and the next export reads it.

| Start | In the panel | What **Libro mayor** holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `current_fiscal_year` | **Ejercicio en curso** | The current Fiscal Year only. The default for every Google Sheets Integration |
| `since_fiscal_year` | **Desde el ejercicio YYYY** | Every Fiscal Year from the one that starts in `generalLedgerExportSinceYear` to the current one. The panel offers each year from last year down to `generalLedgerExportFirstFiscalYear`. The API takes any four-digit year |
| `all_history` | **Todo el historial** | Every Fiscal Year from the first one with a live Ledger Entry to the current one |

The panel offers only the years that hold entries. It doesn't offer the current year as a **Desde el ejercicio** start, because that writes the same as **Ejercicio en curso**. When no Fiscal Year before the current one holds a live Ledger Entry, the panel shows only **Ejercicio en curso** and **Todo el historial**. A start you saved earlier that falls outside those years stays listed and selected.

The export never writes a year with nothing in it before the books start: a start earlier than the Fiscal Year of the first live Ledger Entry begins at that Fiscal Year instead. A start after the current Fiscal Year writes the current Fiscal Year only.

A longer history makes a larger Sheet. When the General Ledger from the start you chose is too large for one spreadsheet, the export writes nothing, keeps the Sheet as the previous export left it, and ends with the failure `too_large`. The panel says *El libro mayor desde ese inicio no cabe en la planilla, así que se mantuvo la sincronización anterior. Elige un ejercicio más reciente.* Pick a later start and export again.

### Export the group

By default, a Google Sheets export writes each Entity's books in its own currency (`per_entity`). A `group` export writes the group's [Consolidated Ledger](/api-reference/consolidation#consolidated-ledger) instead: every Entity of the Organization on its [Group Accounts](/api-reference/chart-of-accounts), the intercompany amounts of its [Intercompany Links](/api-reference/intercompany-links) eliminated, and presentation amounts in one currency you choose. Use it to filter and pivot the group's consolidation in Sheets instead of rebuilding it by hand.

To choose the mode, open the Integration's panel and pick **Por entidad** or **Grupo** in the **Exportación** field, or call [`PUT /integrations/:integrationId/general-ledger-mode`](#put-/integrations/integrationid/general-ledger-mode). The panel saves your choice as soon as you pick it, and the next export reads it. Existing Integrations stay on **Por entidad**. The field doesn't show while the Organization has a single Entity.

#### When the group can be exported

The group is every Entity of the Organization, and it follows the same rules as a [Consolidation Scope](/api-reference/consolidation#scope-currency-and-rates). The Integration's `groupExportRefusal` states why it can't be exported, and the Entities the reason names:

| `reason` | When | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `single_entity` | The Organization has fewer than two Entities | Add another Entity |
| `entity_keeps_bases` | An Entity keeps [Bases](/api-reference/bases). `entityIds` names those Entities | Export `per_entity` and [pick the Basis](#pick-the-basis) |
| `fiscal_years_differ` | The Entities don't all start their [Fiscal Year](/api-reference/reports#the-fiscal-year-in-reports) in the same month. `entityIds` names the Entities that differ | Export `per_entity` |

While `groupExportRefusal` is set, the panel shows **Grupo** disabled with the reason, and choosing `group` through the API answers `422` with `GENERAL_LEDGER_GROUP_EXPORT_REFUSED_ERROR`.

A `group` Integration can become impossible later, for example when an Entity creates its first Basis. Then the Sheet stops updating until you fix the cause or switch back to **Por entidad**:

- The Integrations list shows **El grupo no se puede exportar** on the Integration's row, and its panel says why.
- The scheduled export skips the Integration and records no run.
- **Sincronizar ahora**, or [`POST /integrations/:integrationId/general-ledger-exports`](#post-/integrations/integrationid/general-ledger-exports), is refused with `422` and `GENERAL_LEDGER_GROUP_EXPORT_REFUSED_ERROR`.

A `group` export reads every Entity with no Basis, so a `group` Integration names no Basis, and `basisRequiredEntityIds` stays empty.

#### Libro mayor in a group export

A `group` export writes the same **Libro mayor** tab, so formulas and pivot tables that read it keep working when you change the mode. The first row is the header, and no row sums the tab. The tab has every column of the [`per_entity` tab](#libro-mayor), in the same order, plus these:

| Column | Where | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tipo de línea** | After **Entidad legal** | What the row is: `Saldo inicial`, `Entidad`, `Eliminación`, `Diferencia`, or `Redondeo` |
| **Código grupo** | After **Cuenta** | The code of the row's Group Account |
| **Cuenta grupo** | After **Código grupo** | The name of the row's Group Account |
| **Tipo de cuenta** | After **Cuenta grupo** | The Group Account's type, with the same labels as **Plan de cuentas** |
| **Vínculo** | After **Glosa** | The id of the Intercompany Link, on **Eliminación** and **Diferencia** rows |
| **Débito presentación** | At the end | **Débito**, in the presentation currency |
| **Crédito presentación** | At the end | **Crédito**, in the presentation currency |
| **Saldo presentación** | At the end | The Group Account's running balance, in the presentation currency |

Each [Fiscal Year](#choose-where-the-export-starts) from the start you chose is consolidated on its own, oldest first. Within a Fiscal Year, each Group Account, in code order, opens with its Entities' **Saldo inicial** rows, then lists its Entities' lines and its eliminations by date. The rows of each kind are:

- **Saldo inicial** and **Entidad**: the Entity's rows, as the `per_entity` tab writes them, with **Saldo** as the running balance of the Entity's own account. Each Entity's result of closed years that no account carries opens the year as a **Saldo inicial** row with **Cuenta grupo** set to *Resultado de ejercicios anteriores sin cerrar*.
- **Eliminación**: reverses a linked line in full on the later date of its Link, with the line's Entity, account, Contact, and Dimensions, on the other side. It has no **Saldo**, because it isn't in any Entity's books. A Link eliminated before the Fiscal Year is carried in on its first day, with *Saldo inicial* in the **Glosa**.
- **Diferencia**: one row per Link whose two sides may not cancel in the presentation currency, or that has only one line booked. The **Glosa** states the cause: *sin revaluar*, *conversión*, or *en tránsito*. An *en tránsito* row has no amount. See [Differences](/api-reference/consolidation#differences).
- **Redondeo**: one row per Fiscal Year that holds what rounding leaves.

**Diferencia** and **Redondeo** rows have *Diferencias TC de grupo* as their **Cuenta grupo** and no **Código grupo**. With them, each Fiscal Year's presentation amounts net to zero at any rate.

The presentation columns are Sheets formulas, not values. Each one divides the row's amount by the **Tasa en uso** of the row's **Moneda** in **Tipos de cambio**, rounded to the decimals of the presentation currency. So when you change a rate, every presentation amount moves at once, with no new export. The formulas separate their arguments with `;`, so they work in a Sheet of any locale.

A `group` tab has more cells than a `per_entity` one, so it reaches the size limit sooner. When it is too large, the export ends with the failure [`too_large`](#the-general-ledger-export-object), as a `per_entity` export does.

#### Tipos de cambio

A `group` export writes **Tipos de cambio** before **Libro mayor**. Unlike the other tabs, Sintropix doesn't rewrite it whole: it writes only the cells it owns, and keeps the ones you type.

| Cell | Content | Who writes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **B1** | The presentation currency, such as `USD` | You. The first export sets it to `USD` when an Entity keeps its books in USD, or to `CLP` otherwise, and never writes it again |
| **B2** | The decimals of the presentation currency | Sintropix |
| **B3** | The currency the suggested rates were computed for. When it differs from **B1**, **C3** asks you to sync again | Sintropix |

From row 6, the tab holds one row for each functional currency of the group. A currency keeps its row from one export to the next, and a new currency takes the row after the last:

| Column | Content | Who writes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Moneda funcional** | The functional currency | Sintropix |
| **Sugerido** | The published [FX rate](/api-reference/fx-rates) on the day of the export. `1` for the presentation currency, and empty when no published rate reaches the pair | Sintropix |
| **Usado** | The rate you want to use instead | You. Sintropix never overwrites it |
| **Tasa en uso** | **Usado** when you typed one, otherwise **Sugerido**. The presentation formulas read this column | Sintropix |
| **Fuente** | Where the suggestion comes from, or a reminder to type the rate in **Usado** when none is published | Sintropix |

A rate is the number of units of the functional currency that one unit of the presentation currency is worth, as in the [consolidated reports](/api-reference/consolidation#scope-currency-and-rates). To present a CLP Entity in USD at 1 USD = 950 CLP, type `950` in **Usado** on the `CLP` row.

To change the presentation currency, type it in **B1** and sync again, so that **Sugerido** is computed for the new currency. Check your **Usado** rates too, because they are still quoted in the old currency. If you delete or rename the tab, the presentation columns show `#REF!` until the next export creates it again, with the default presentation currency and an empty **Usado**.

Switching back to **Por entidad** leaves **Tipos de cambio** as it is, so the rates you typed are back in use if you return to **Grupo**. **Plan de cuentas** and **Dimensiones** are the same in both modes.

### When the export runs

Sintropix exports every Google Sheets Integration every four hours, except one that [waits for its Basis](#pick-the-basis) and a `group` Integration whose group [can't be exported](#export-the-group). It records no run for those. To export right away, click **Sincronizar ahora** on the Integration's panel, or call [`POST /integrations/:integrationId/general-ledger-exports`](#post-/integrations/integrationid/general-ledger-exports). The panel shows when the last export ran and how many lines it wrote, or why it failed.

### The General Ledger Export object

A General Ledger Export is one run that writes the General Ledger of every Entity of the Organization to the Sheet. It states either the lines it wrote or why it wrote nothing: exactly one of `lineCount` and `failure` is set.

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Export id. |
| `organizationId` | uuid | Owning Organization. |
| `integrationId` | uuid | The Google Sheets Integration. |
| `occurredAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | When the export ran. |
| `lineCount` | integer \| null | Ledger lines written to **Libro mayor**, across every Entity. The **Saldo inicial** rows don't count, and neither do the **Eliminación**, **Diferencia**, and **Redondeo** rows of a `group` export. `null` when the export failed. |
| `failure` | string \| null | Why the export wrote nothing (see below). `null` when the export succeeded. |

| `failure` | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `authorization_expired` | The Google authorization expired or was revoked | Renew the authorization, or disconnect the Integration and connect the Sheet again |
| `sheet_not_found` | Google can't find the Sheet: it was deleted, or the account lost access | Check the Sheet, then connect it again |
| `sheet_not_writable` | The account can no longer edit the Sheet | Give the account edit access, then export again |
| `provider_unreachable` | Google couldn't be reached | Export again in a few minutes |
| `provider_failure` | Google failed | Export again in a few minutes |
| `too_large` | The General Ledger from the Integration's start is too large for one spreadsheet. The export wrote nothing and the Sheet keeps the previous export | [Choose a later start](#choose-where-the-export-starts), then export again |

## Files for Erwin and the MCP

Some Integrations share files with Erwin and with the assistants you connect to the [MCP server](/guides/mcp-server). You choose the files, and they read each one as it is now at its Provider, as an Excel workbook. Use it for what lives outside your books, such as a collections tracker your team keeps in Sheets or a sales report in Metabase. Sintropix stores none of a file's content, and it never writes to the Provider.

Two kinds of Integration hold files. Each Integration in [`GET /integrations`](#get-/integrations) says whether it holds files in `holdsFiles`:

| Kind | Its files | How you choose them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `google_drive` | One Google Sheet, read with every tab | You pick the Sheet when you connect. See [Connect a Sheet for Erwin and the MCP](#connect-a-sheet-for-erwin-and-the-mcp) |
| `metabase` | The Metabase questions you choose. Sintropix runs a question in Metabase each time someone reads it, with the filters the reader sends | In the Integration's panel, with **Elegir preguntas**, or through [`POST /integrations/:integrationId/files`](#post-/integrations/integrationid/files). See [Metabase](#metabase) |

The panel's **Archivos para Erwin y el MCP** section lists the chosen files, each with its purpose. Write a purpose that says what the file holds and when to use it, for example "Cobranza mensual por cliente". Erwin and the assistants use it to pick the right file. While a file has no purpose, they read the description the file has at its Provider instead: the Sheet's description in Google Drive, or the question's description in Metabase.

### Connect a Sheet for Erwin and the MCP

A `google_drive` Integration shares one Google Sheet. Each read asks Google for the Sheet as it is now, with every tab. This is a different use of a Sheet than the [General Ledger export](#google-sheets), and one Sheet can be in both.

You connect it from the app, because Google asks for consent in a browser:

1. Open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Google Drive**.
2. Continue on Google and allow access. Google opens its file picker, which lists only spreadsheets. Pick the Sheet.

Sintropix gets access to the Sheet you pick and to nothing else in your Drive. When you return, it checks the Sheet and opens the Integration's panel. It refuses the connection, and stores nothing, when:

- You left the picker without picking a Sheet.
- The file you picked isn't a spreadsheet, or Google can't reach it with the account you authorized.
- The Sheet's owner doesn't let readers download it. Ask the owner to allow downloads, then connect it again.
- The Sheet is already connected, in this or another Organization.

Each Integration holds the one Sheet you picked. To share another Sheet, connect it as another Integration.

### Who can read the files

Reading a shared file is more than reading your books, so it follows the same rule as [reading email](#who-can-read-email):

| Caller | List and read files |
| --- | --- |
| `staff` or `client` | Yes |
| `viewer` | No |
| An API key confined to one Entity | No |

The same rule covers the list of the files you can choose. A refused caller gets `403` with `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`. Choosing a file, unchoosing it, and setting its purpose are writes, so a `viewer` can't do them either. An OAuth connection, such as the MCP server's, reads files with the Role of the person who approved it.

Sintropix logs each file it hands out under the request's [Audit Envelope](/api-reference/audit-trail). Reading a file with an API key needs `x-audit-actor` and `x-audit-reason`, as a write does. Listing the files isn't logged.

## Xpendit

An `xpendit` Integration connects one Xpendit enterprise with its API key. Sintropix reads Xpendit and never writes to it. An enterprise holds one or more **Subsidiaries**, Xpendit's word for the accounts its expenses and funds belong to. Sintropix calls each one a **Provider Account**.

With an Xpendit Integration you can:

- [Read what Xpendit holds](#read-what-xpendit-holds): each Subsidiary's recent expenses and its open funds.
- Route each Subsidiary to a Source of [Expense Renditions](/api-reference/sources#sources-that-hold-expense-renditions) of an Entity, with an [expense Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds#expense-feeds). Each [sync](/api-reference/source-feeds#sync-an-expense-feed) lands the expenses Xpendit approved as approved Expense Claims, booked as Expense Renditions with their receipts attached.

### Connect Xpendit

In the app, open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Xpendit**, under the **Rendiciones** tag. Paste the API key of your Xpendit enterprise. Through the API, send it to [`POST /integrations`](#post-/integrations) with `kind: "xpendit"` and `accessToken`.

Sintropix checks the key with Xpendit before it stores it:

| Outcome | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Xpendit accepts the key | `201` with the Integration. `providerRef` is the id of the enterprise |
| Xpendit refuses the key | `422` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored |
| Another Integration already connects that enterprise | `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR`. `params.providerRef` names the enterprise |

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Connect the Xpendit enterprise to read its expenses" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "7c8d9e0f-1a2b-4c3d-8e4f-5a6b7c8d9e0f",
    "kind": "xpendit",
    "accessToken": "xpendit-api-key"
  }'
```

For Buk, Sintropix checks the new key at the same tenant. For Metabase, Sintropix checks the new key at the same site, with the same rules as at connect, and the chosen questions and their purposes stay. For Rho, the new token must open the same business. ## Metabase

A `metabase` Integration connects one [Metabase Cloud](https://www.metabase.com/cloud) site, where you keep the reports of your business, with an API key. You choose which of its questions Erwin and the assistants you connect to the [MCP server](/guides/mcp-server) can read. Each read runs the question in Metabase at that moment and hands out its rows as an Excel workbook. Sintropix stores none of the results and never changes anything in Metabase. The Integration feeds nothing.

Only Metabase Cloud connects, at an address that ends in `metabaseapp.com`. A self-hosted Metabase can't connect.

### Create the key in Metabase

Choosing questions in Sintropix limits what Erwin and the MCP read. It doesn't limit the key: what the key can read is set in Metabase, by the collections its group can view. So give the key a group of its own:

1. In Metabase, an administrator opens **Admin → People → Groups** and creates a group for Sintropix only, for example "Sintropix".
2. In **Admin → Permissions → Collections**, give that group **View** access to the collections Sintropix should read, and nothing else.
3. In **Admin → Settings → Authentication → API keys**, create a key in that group. Don't pick the Administrators group: Sintropix refuses an administrator's key.
4. Copy the key. Metabase shows it only once.

### Connect Metabase

In the app, open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Metabase**. Type the address of your Metabase in **Dirección de Metabase**, such as `tuempresa.metabaseapp.com`, paste the key in **Clave de API**, and connect. You can paste any address of your Metabase: Sintropix keeps its host. Through the API, send [`POST /integrations`](#post-/integrations) with `kind: "metabase"`, `host`, and `accessToken`.

Sintropix checks the key with Metabase before it stores it. The key is stored encrypted and sent only to the host of your site.

| Outcome | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Metabase accepts the key | `201` with the Integration. `providerRef` is the host, such as `acme.metabaseapp.com` |
| The address isn't a `metabaseapp.com` address | `400`. Nothing leaves Sintropix. The app says *Escribe la dirección de tu Metabase Cloud, por ejemplo tuempresa.metabaseapp.com.* |
| Metabase refuses the key | `422` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored |
| No site lives at the address, the key is in the Administrators group, or the key reaches no question | `422` with `INTEGRATION_CONNECT_REJECTED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored. The app says *Metabase no tiene esa dirección, la clave es de un administrador o no llega a ninguna pregunta.* Check the address and the group of the key in Metabase |
| Another Integration already connects that site | `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR` |

Each Metabase site can be connected only once.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Connect Metabase so Erwin can read the sales reports" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "5d6e7f80-91a2-4b3c-8d4e-5f6a7b8c9d0e",
    "kind": "metabase",
    "host": "acme.metabaseapp.com",
    "accessToken": "mb_your_api_key"
  }'
```

### Choose the questions

A new Metabase Integration has no question chosen, so Erwin and the MCP server read nothing from it yet. In the Integration's panel, click **Elegir preguntas**. The dialog lists every question the key can view, except archived ones, with a search by name or description. Switch on each question Erwin and the MCP server may read, and click **Listo**.

The panel's **Archivos para Erwin y el MCP** section then lists each chosen question with its purpose, or with its description in Metabase while it has no purpose, and its filters, such as *Filtros: Canal · Año*. Write a purpose for a question whose name and description don't say when to use it.

Through the API, [list the questions the key reaches](#get-/integrations/integrationid/files/reachable), then [choose](#post-/integrations/integrationid/files) or [unchoose](#delete-/integrations/integrationid/files/fileid) each one. To read a question with its filters, send them in the query of [the read](#get-/integrations/integrationid/files/fileid), such as `?canal=online`. See [Files for Erwin and the MCP](#files-for-erwin-and-the-mcp).

A question that's archived or deleted in Metabase, or that the key's group can no longer view, stays chosen but drops out of the list, and reading it answers `424` with `INTEGRATION_FILE_GONE_ERROR`.

### Replace the Metabase key

To rotate the key, regenerate it in Metabase, then [replace it](#put-/integrations/integrationid/access-token): in the app, open the Integration's panel and click **Reemplazar clave de API**. Sintropix checks the new key at the same site, with the same rules as at connect. The chosen questions and their purposes stay. A key Metabase no longer accepts makes every read answer `424` with `INTEGRATION_GRANT_FAILED_ERROR` until you replace it.

### Disconnect Metabase

[`DELETE /integrations/:integrationId`](#delete-/integrations/integrationid), or disconnecting from the Integration's panel, removes the key Sintropix stores. Metabase keeps the key working until you delete it there too, under **Admin → Settings → Authentication → API keys**.

## Rho

A `rho` Integration connects one business on [Rho](https://www.rho.co), the US business banking platform, with a read-only API access token. Sintropix only reads Rho: it never moves money. You can [preview every account](#get-/integrations/integrationid/rho-overview) of the business with its current balance, including Treasury and rewards accounts. The Integration feeds no Bank Account yet.

### Create the token in Rho

Only an Owner or Admin of the Rho business can create an API access token, and Rho asks for a two-factor code to do it.

1. In Rho, open **Settings → API → Access Tokens** and click **Create token**.
2. Name it for Sintropix, and use the token for Sintropix only.
3. Pick these scopes, all read-only: `accounts:read`, `transactions:read`, `statements:read`, and `businesses:read`. Don't pick any `:write` scope: Sintropix only reads.
4. Pick when it expires. Rho allows one year at most, and also expires a token that goes unused for 45 days. Note the date: you replace the token before then.
5. Leave the list of allowed IP addresses empty.
6. Confirm with your two-factor code and copy the token. Rho shows it only once.

### Connect Rho

In the app, open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Rho**, under the **Banco** tag. Paste the token in **Clave de API** and connect. Through the API, send [`POST /integrations`](#post-/integrations) with `kind: "rho"` and `accessToken`.

Sintropix checks the token with Rho before it stores it: it reads the business and every one of its accounts. The token is stored encrypted.

| Outcome | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Rho accepts the token | `201` with the Integration. `providerRef` is the id of the Rho business |
| Rho refuses the token, for example because it expired or was revoked | `422` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored |
| The token lacks `businesses:read` or `accounts:read` | `422` with `INTEGRATION_CONNECT_REJECTED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored. The app says *Rho aceptó el token, pero no deja leer las cuentas.* |
| Another Integration already connects that business | `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR`. `params.providerRef` names the business |

Each Rho business can be connected only once.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Connect the Rho business to see its accounts" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "3b4c5d6e-7f80-4a91-b2c3-d4e5f6a7b8c9",
    "kind": "rho",
    "accessToken": "rhobat_your_token"
  }'
```

When the connection is stored, the Integration's panel lists the business's accounts under **Cuentas en Rho**.

### Replace the Rho token

Before the token expires, or after you revoke it, create a new token in Rho with the same scopes. Then [replace it](#put-/integrations/integrationid/access-token): in the app, open the Integration's panel and click **Reemplazar clave de API**. The new token must belong to the same business, or the replacement answers `409` with `INTEGRATION_GRANT_MISMATCH_ERROR` and the stored token stays. Then revoke the old token in Rho.

### Disconnect Rho

[`DELETE /integrations/:integrationId`](#delete-/integrations/integrationid), or disconnecting from the Integration's panel, removes the token Sintropix stores. Rho keeps the token working until you revoke it there too, under **Settings → API → Access Tokens**.

## Stripe

A `stripe` Integration connects one Stripe account with read-only access. Sintropix only reads: it never charges, refunds, or withdraws money. It can feed a Bank Account with each currency of the account's Stripe payments balance, through a [bank feed](/api-reference/bank-feeds).

### Connect Stripe

You connect Stripe from the app, because Stripe asks for consent in a browser:

1. Open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Stripe**, under the **Pagos** tag.
2. Continue on Stripe, pick the account to connect, and accept the read permissions of the Sintropix Stripe App. This installs the App in that account.

When you return, the Integration's panel shows what the connection reaches: the account's business name, **País**, **Moneda por defecto**, and **Modo** (**En vivo** or **Prueba**). The Integration object carries the same facts in `accountName`, `country`, `defaultCurrency`, and `mode`.

If you cancel on Stripe, nothing is connected. Each Stripe account can be connected only once.

### Disconnect Stripe

Disconnecting uninstalls the Sintropix Stripe App from the account, then removes the Integration. See [`DELETE /integrations/:integrationId`](#delete-/integrations/integrationid).

## Notion

A `notion` Integration connects one Notion workspace with read-only access to the pages and databases you share with Sintropix. Sintropix never writes to Notion. It stores nothing of what a database holds and feeds nothing yet: you can list the databases and preview each one.

### Connect Notion

You connect Notion from the app, because Notion asks for consent in a browser:

1. Open **Configuración → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Notion**.
2. Continue on Notion, pick the workspace, and pick the pages or databases you share with Sintropix.

When you return, the Integration's panel lists the databases the connection reaches under **Bases en Notion**. To reach another database later, open it in Notion and share it with the Sintropix connection from its **•••** menu, under **Conexiones**. Through the API, [list the Provider Accounts](#get-/integrations/integrationid/provider-accounts): each one is a database, and its `id` is the data source id the preview takes.

## Portal Logins

A Portal Login is the login of a portal that no Provider reads. Unlike an Integration, a Portal Login belongs to one Entity, and its routes live under `/api/entities/:entityId/portal-logins`. Sintropix stores the login and the password encrypted and uses them only to read the portal. Erwin can sign in to the portal with them, for example to [load a card's unbilled movements](/api-reference/bank-movements#from-unbilled-movements) from a bank's site. No read ever returns them, so not even you can see them again in Sintropix. A Portal Login has one of four kinds:

| Kind | Portal | What you store |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bank_login` | A bank's portal | The bank, picked from the [Banks catalog](/api-reference/reference-data#get-/banks), plus the RUT and the password you sign in with |
| `getnet_login` | The Getnet merchant portal | The username or email, and the password you sign in with |
| `sii_login` | The SII, as a company or as a person. To connect the SII, use the [SII Integration](#sii) instead | The taxpayer type, plus the RUT and the SII password (clave) you sign in with |
| `clay_login` | Clay, the Chilean ERP you migrate your books from, so the Sintropix team can migrate them | A name you choose, plus either an API key or the username and password you sign in with |

Sintropix doesn't sign in to the portal when you store a Portal Login, so a wrong login or password isn't refused. A Portal Login has no health status: it's stored, never "working" or "failed". To change the password, delete the Portal Login and store it again.

In the app, connect one from **Configuración → Integraciones**: pick your bank by its name, or pick **Getnet** or **Clay** (under the **ERP** tag). Pick the **Entidad legal** the login belongs to. It's preset when the Organization has only one Entity. For **Clay**, give it a **Nombre** (prefilled as "Clay") and pick how it signs in: **API key** or **Usuario y clave**. Name each Clay Login after the company it holds, so you can store one per company you migrate. A Portal Login shows in the list with the status **Clave guardada**.

<Note>
  A `client` or `staff` caller can store and delete Portal Logins. A `viewer` can only list them. See [Access and roles](/api-reference/overview#access-and-roles).
</Note>

### The Portal Login object

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Portal Login id. |
| `entityId` | uuid | Owning Entity. |
| `kind` | `bank_login` \| `getnet_login` \| `sii_login` \| `clay_login` | The kind of portal. |
| `label` | string | The portal's name. For a `bank_login`, the bank's name in the [Banks catalog](/api-reference/reference-data#get-/banks). For a `getnet_login`, always `Getnet`. For a `sii_login`, `SII Empresa` or `SII Persona`, by its `holderType`. For a `clay_login`, the name you gave it. |
| `bankCode` | string \| null | For a `bank_login`, the bank's three-digit CMF code. `null` for every other kind. |
| `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | When the Portal Login was stored and last changed. |

### POST `/portal-logins`

Store a Portal Login for the Entity.

**Body.** The shape depends on `kind`. Every kind takes an `id`:

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Client-generated id. Retrying with the same id is answered `409` (`CREDENTIAL_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`). |
| `kind` | `bank_login` \| `getnet_login` \| `sii_login` \| `clay_login` | Yes | The kind of portal. |

Each kind adds its own fields:

| Kind | Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bank_login` | `bankCode` | string | The bank's three-digit CMF code, from [`GET /banks`](/api-reference/reference-data#get-/banks), for example `001` for Banco de Chile. A code the catalog doesn't hold is refused with `400` (`CREDENTIAL_BANK_UNKNOWN_ERROR`). |
| `bank_login` | `rut` | string | The RUT you sign in to the bank with. Dots, spaces, and the hyphen are optional, and a lowercase `k` is accepted. A RUT whose check digit doesn't match is refused with `400` (`RUT_INVALID_ERROR`). |
| `bank_login` | `password` | string | The bank password. Must be non-empty. |
| `getnet_login` | `username` | string | The username or email you sign in to the Getnet portal with. Must be non-empty. |
| `getnet_login` | `password` | string | The Getnet password. Must be non-empty. |
| `sii_login` | `holderType` | `business` \| `individual` | Whose SII login it is: `business` for the company, `individual` for a person, such as its legal representative. |
| `sii_login` | `rut` | string | The RUT you sign in to the SII with. Same format and check-digit rule as the `bank_login` `rut`. |
| `sii_login` | `password` | string | The SII password (clave). Must be non-empty. |
| `clay_login` | `label` | string | The name you give this Clay Login, for example the company it holds. Whitespace is trimmed and must be non-empty. |
| `clay_login` | `method` | `api_key` \| `password` | How Clay is opened: with an API key, or with the username and password of the Clay web app. |
| `clay_login` | `apiKey` | string | The Clay API key. Required, and must be non-empty, when `method` is `api_key`. |
| `clay_login` | `username` | string | The username you sign in to Clay with. Required, and must be non-empty, when `method` is `password`. |
| `clay_login` | `password` | string | The Clay password. Required, and must be non-empty, when `method` is `password`. |

Each name can be used once per Entity. Sintropix names a Bank Login after its bank, so an Entity holds at most one Bank Login per bank. A second one for the same bank is refused with `409` (`CREDENTIAL_LABEL_ALREADY_USED_ERROR`). A `bank_login` body takes no `label`. Sintropix names every Getnet Login `Getnet`, so an Entity holds at most one. A second Getnet Login is refused with the same error. To store another Getnet login, delete the first one. Sintropix names an SII Login by its `holderType`: `SII Empresa` for `business` and `SII Persona` for `individual`. An Entity holds at most one of each, and a second one of the same `holderType` is refused with the same error. A `sii_login` body takes no `label`. You name each Clay Login, so an Entity can hold as many as it needs. A Clay Login whose name the Entity already uses is refused with the same error.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `200` | Success on `GET`, `PUT`, `DELETE`, and a General Ledger Export, including one that failed. |
| `201` | Integration, Mailbox, or Portal Login created, or an email attachment copied into an Entity. |
| `400` | Validation error (including a Portal Login RUT that fails its check digit, `RUT_INVALID_ERROR`, or a Bank Login `bankCode` the Banks catalog doesn't hold, `CREDENTIAL_BANK_UNKNOWN_ERROR`), missing `x-audit-actor` on a key-authenticated mutation, or a General Ledger Export while the Integration names no Basis of an Entity that keeps Bases (`GENERAL_LEDGER_EXPORT_BASIS_REQUIRED_ERROR`). |

| `404` | Organization, Entity, Integration (`INTEGRATION_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), Portal Login (`CREDENTIAL_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), Mailbox (`MAILBOX_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), Conversation (`CONVERSATION_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), email (`INTEGRATION_EMAIL_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), email attachment (`INTEGRATION_EMAIL_ATTACHMENT_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), a Notion database the connection doesn't reach (`INTEGRATION_DATA_SOURCE_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), or a Basis outside the Organization (`BASIS_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`) not found. |
