An Integration is a linked external system: the credentials, tokens, and configuration Sintropix stores to reach that system on the Organization's behalf. Every grant to an external account is an Integration, including the banks and payment providers that feed Bank Accounts. Integrations are the credential side. The feeds are the recurring pull jobs that import data through them.

What an Integration can feed depends on its kind:

| Kind | Feeds |
| --- | --- |
| `sii_mirror` | Up to two [Sources](/api-reference/sources), through [Source Feeds](/api-reference/source-feeds): one for DTEs (`dte`) and one for boletas de honorarios (`honorarium`) |
| `bsale`, `wasabil` | A [Source](/api-reference/sources), through a [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds) of DTEs (`dte`) |
| `fintoc`, `plaid`, `global66`, `cardda`, `dlocal`, `mercadopago`, `polar` | One or more [Bank Accounts](/api-reference/bank-accounts), through [bank feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds) |
| `xpendit` | A [Source](/api-reference/sources) that holds Expense Renditions for each of its Subsidiaries, through a [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds#expense-feeds) of expenses (`expense`). Each sync lands the expenses Xpendit approved as approved [Expense Claims](/api-reference/expense-claims). You can also [read its expenses](#read-what-xpendit-holds) on demand |
| `mercadolibre`, `stripe`, `buk` | Nothing yet |
| `gmail`, `outlook`, `mailbox` | Nothing. You search and read their email on demand instead. See [Read email](#read-email) |
| `google_sheets` | Nothing. It receives the General Ledger instead. See [Google Sheets](#google-sheets) |

An Organization can hold more than one Integration at a time (for example, a Bsale document feed alongside a Fintoc bank and a Mercado Pago authorization). Each external account can be connected only once.

The `fintoc` kind connects a bank login through the [Fintoc](https://fintoc.com) widget. Only `staff` can connect or reconnect a bank through Fintoc. A `client` connects a bank by storing its [Bank Login](#portal-logins). The `global66` kind connects Global66 with a client id, a client secret, and the accounts you name. The `cardda` kind connects a Cardda company with its API key, and the `dlocal` kind connects a dLocal Go merchant with its API key and secret key. Sintropix reads the Provider before it stores the credentials, so credentials that cannot read are refused at connect.

The `plaid` kind connects a US bank through [Plaid](https://plaid.com) Link. Plaid Link shows US banks only, in English, and asks for access to the bank's transactions, with up to 730 days of history. Your bank password goes to Plaid and never reaches Sintropix. It can feed Bank Accounts with the bank's posted transactions. See [`POST /integrations/plaid/link-tokens`](#post-integrations-plaid-link-tokens) and [Bank Feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds#plaid).

The `mercadopago` kind connects a Mercado Pago account through OAuth. It can feed a Bank Account with the account's available money. See [Bank Feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds#mercado-pago).

The `gmail` and `outlook` kinds connect an email account: a Gmail account or a Microsoft Outlook account. You connect it from the app. Sintropix only reads: it never sends, changes, or deletes email. An Organization can connect several email accounts, but each account can be connected only once. You can [search and read](#read-email) the email of a connected account through the API.

The `mailbox` kind is an email address that Sintropix holds for the Organization, such as `facturas@acme.sintropix.com`. You create it in Sintropix: there's no outside account to connect. An Organization can hold several Mailboxes, all at its own email domain. See [Mailbox](#mailbox).

The `google_sheets` kind connects one Google Sheet through Google OAuth. Sintropix writes the General Ledger of the current Fiscal Year of every Entity of the Organization to that Sheet, every four hours and whenever you ask. See [Google Sheets](#google-sheets).

The `xpendit` kind connects the enterprise of an Xpendit account, the expense tool, with its API key. You can read the expenses and funds of each of its Subsidiaries, and route each Subsidiary to a Source of Expense Renditions. See [Xpendit](#xpendit).

The `buk` kind connects a [Buk](https://www.buk.cl) account, the payroll and HR system, with the address of your Buk and an API key. It lists the Empresas the key reaches, with their RUT, and feeds nothing yet. See [Buk](#buk).

The `stripe` kind connects a [Stripe](https://stripe.com) account through OAuth, by installing the Sintropix Stripe App in that account with read-only access. It feeds nothing yet. See [Stripe](#stripe).

The `polar` kind connects a [Polar](https://polar.sh) account through OAuth. You grant Sintropix read-only access to the account's organizations, orders, payments, refunds, disputes, transactions, and payouts. If you clear the transactions or payouts permission on Polar's consent screen, Sintropix refuses the connection and stores nothing. Sign in to Polar as a user with the owner, admin, or finance role in the organization: Polar shows an organization's money to those roles only. It can feed a USD Bank Account with each organization's Polar balance. Sintropix only reads: it never charges, refunds, or withdraws money. See [Bank Feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds#polar).

To connect a bank that no Provider reads, such as a credit card Fintoc can't fetch, store its login as a [Bank Login](#portal-logins). To connect the Getnet merchant portal, store its login as a [Getnet Login](#portal-logins). To give Sintropix access to Clay, the Chilean ERP you migrate your books from, store it as a [Clay Login](#portal-logins). None of these is an Integration kind, but the app lists them with the Integrations.

The `sii_mirror` kind connects the SII, the Chilean tax authority, and is the only SII document Provider. You connect it with the company's RUT and its SII password (Clave), and the SII checks them at once. It then reads the RCV registers of that RUT from Sintropix's Chilean SII mirror. The mirror reads the SII registers every 4 hours, so a document reaches it a few hours after the SII records it. See [SII](#sii).

In the app, open **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones** and click **Conectar integración**. The catalog lists every option with its logo, and you can search it or filter it by tag: **Banco**, **Pagos**, **Ventas**, **Facturador**, **Impuestos**, **Rendiciones**, **Remuneraciones**, **Correo**, or **ERP**. Each Chilean bank is its own option, and you can find it by its short name too, such as "bci". Pick an option and fill in its form. For a Provider that uses OAuth, such as Google or Mercado Pago, you continue on the Provider's page and return to Sintropix afterwards.

Connecting asks for no Entity. When the connection is stored, its panel opens on the step it still lacks, where you route it to the Entities (**Entidades legales**) of the Organization. For a document Provider, pick the Source each Document Family lands in, from the Sources of every Entity grouped by Entity, and the date to read from. You can also create a Source in any Entity. For a bank Provider, link each of its accounts to a Bank Account of any Entity, or create one in the Entity you pick. Until you finish that step, the list shows the connection as **Falta elegir fuentes** or **Falta elegir cuentas**.

The list shows every connection of the Organization. Each Integration's row names the Entities its feeds reach, or **Sin asignar** until a feed routes it. A Portal Login's row names its own Entity.

## Integrations belong to the Organization

An Integration belongs to the [Organization](/api-reference/organizations), not to one Entity. Its feeds route it: each [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds) or [bank feed](/api-reference/bank-feeds) names the Entity whose Source or Bank Account it fills. One Integration can feed several Entities of its Organization. For example, one bank login can feed a Bank Account in each of two Entities. No tax id decides or limits where a feed lands: any feed can land in any Entity of the Organization.

The routes follow the same split:

- Every Integration route lives under `/api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations`: connect, list, the Fintoc Link Intent, the Plaid Link token, reconnect, the replacement of an API key, the purpose, the SII legal representative's Clave, the discovered accounts, the Provider Accounts, the General Ledger Export, a Mailbox and its Conversations, the email of a Gmail, Outlook, or Mailbox account, the expenses of an Xpendit account, and disconnect.
- Every feed route lives under the Entity it lands in, `/api/entities/:entityId/...`. So does the copy of an [email attachment](#post-api-entities-entityid-email-attachments) into an Entity. They take an Integration of that Entity's Organization. An Integration of another Organization answers `404` with `INTEGRATION_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`.

Connecting, reconnecting, disconnecting, and setting the purpose of an Integration need the `books` write permission in the Organization, which a `client` or `staff` caller holds. A `viewer` can only list Integrations, and can't read the email of a Gmail, Outlook, or Mailbox account or the expenses of an Xpendit account. An API key confined to one Entity:

- Lists every Integration of its Organization, but sees only the Sources and Bank Accounts of its own Entity.
- Can't connect, reconnect, renew, or disconnect an Integration, replace its API key, run a General Ledger Export, read or copy the email of a Gmail, Outlook, or Mailbox account, or read the expenses of an Xpendit account. It gets `403` with `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`.
- Adds and removes only the feeds that land in its own Entity.

An Entity whose feed routes an Integration of its Organization, or whose Book a Google Sheets Integration exports, can't move to another Organization. See [`PATCH /api/entities/:entityId/organization`](/api-reference/entities#patch-api-entities-entityid-organization).

## The Integration object

The access token is stored encrypted and is never returned on any read.

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Integration id. |
| `organizationId` | uuid | Owning Organization. |
| `kind` | `bsale` \| `wasabil` \| `sii_mirror` \| `fintoc` \| `global66` \| `cardda` \| `dlocal` \| `plaid` \| `mercadopago` \| `mercadolibre` \| `gmail` \| `outlook` \| `mailbox` \| `polar` \| `google_sheets` \| `xpendit` \| `buk` \| `stripe` | The external system. |
| `providerRef` | string \| null | The provider's own handle for the connected account (for Bsale, the instance number; for `sii_mirror`, the company RUT the SII accepted at connect, unformatted; for `fintoc`, the Fintoc Link; for `plaid`, the Plaid Item id; for Mercado Pago and Mercado Libre, the account ID; for `gmail` and `outlook`, the account's email address; for `mailbox`, its address; for `polar`, the email address of the Polar user who granted access; for `google_sheets`, the id of the Google Sheet; for `xpendit`, the id of the Xpendit enterprise; for `buk`, the tenant, the subdomain of your Buk, such as `acme` for `acme.buk.cl`; for `stripe`, the Stripe account id, such as `acct_1Q2w3E4r5T6y7U8i`). `null` when a document provider could not determine it at connect time. |
| `documentCount` | integer \| null | Number of documents the provider reported when the credential was verified at connect time. `null` for every kind that is not a document integration: the bank kinds and the OAuth authorizations. |
| `institutionName` | string \| null | The bank's display name, as the Provider reports it. Only `fintoc` and `plaid` state it; `null` for every other kind. |
| `holderId` | string \| null | The account holder's tax id (RUT), unformatted, as the Provider reports it. Only `fintoc` and `cardda` state it; `null` for every other kind. |
| `mode` | `test` \| `live` \| null | Whether a `fintoc`, `plaid`, or `stripe` Integration runs against the Provider's test or live environment. `null` for every other kind. |
| `accountName` | string \| null | For `stripe`, the business name of the Stripe account, or its Dashboard name when it has no business name, as read at connect. It can be `null` for a Stripe account that states neither. `null` for every other kind. |
| `country` | string \| null | For `stripe`, the country of the Stripe account, as an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code such as `US`, read at connect. `null` for every other kind. |
| `defaultCurrency` | string \| null | For `stripe`, the default currency of the Stripe account, as an uppercase ISO 4217 code such as `USD`, read at connect. `null` for every other kind. |
| `feedableFamilies` | array | The [Document Families](/api-reference/source-feeds#document-families) this Integration's provider can feed: `["dte", "honorarium"]` for `sii_mirror`, `["dte"]` for `bsale` and `wasabil`, `["expense"]` for `xpendit`. Empty for every kind that feeds no documents. |
| `sourceFeeds` | array | The Source Feeds that read through this Integration, in the shape documented on [Source Feeds](/api-reference/source-feeds). Each Entity holds at most one Feed per family. Empty when the Integration feeds no Source. |
| `fedBankAccounts` | array | `{ id, name, entityId }` of each Bank Account this Integration feeds, in any Entity, in name order. Empty when it feeds none. |
| `routedEntities` | array | `{ id, name }` of each Entity the Integration's feeds reach, in name order. Empty while no feed routes it. |
| `bookId` | uuid \| null | For `google_sheets`, the [Book](/api-reference/books) the export reads for the Entity that keeps it. `null` for every other kind, and when no Book was picked. |
| `lastGeneralLedgerExport` | object \| null | For `google_sheets`, the last [General Ledger Export](#the-general-ledger-export-object). `null` until the first export runs, and always `null` for every other kind. |
| `purpose` | string \| null | What the account is for, in your own words, such as "Facturas de proveedores de Acme". It helps a person or an agent pick the right account when the Organization holds several. Up to 500 characters. `null` until you [set it](#put-integrations-integrationid-purpose). |
| `grantHealth` | `working` \| `failed` \| `unknown` \| null | For `gmail` and `outlook`, whether the account's access still works, checked on each list. `failed` means the access expired or was revoked: disconnect the Integration and connect the account again. `unknown` means Sintropix couldn't check it right now. For `mailbox`, `working` when Sintropix reaches its inbox and `unknown` otherwise; a Mailbox holds no access that can expire, so it's never `failed`. `null` for every other kind. |
| `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | Row timestamps. |

## POST `/integrations`

`POST /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations`

Create an Integration of the Organization by handing Sintropix a provider-issued access token. The token is verified against the provider once, then stored encrypted. Connecting asks for no Entity: route the Integration to Entities afterwards, through its feeds.

This route connects the document Providers, the bank Providers, Xpendit, and Buk. Mercado Pago, Mercado Libre, Gmail, Outlook, Polar, Google Sheets, and Stripe use OAuth and are connected from **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones** in the Sintropix app. You cannot create them through this endpoint. A Mailbox has its own route: [`POST /integrations/mailbox`](#post-integrations-mailbox).

**Body.** The shape depends on `kind`. Every kind takes an `id`:

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Client-generated id. Retrying the same `POST` with the same id is rejected instead of creating a duplicate. |
| `kind` | `bsale` \| `wasabil` \| `xpendit` \| `buk` \| `sii_mirror` \| `fintoc` \| `plaid` \| `global66` \| `cardda` \| `dlocal` | Yes | The external system to link. `mercadopago`, `mercadolibre`, `gmail`, `outlook`, `mailbox`, `polar`, `google_sheets`, and `stripe` are not accepted here. |

Each kind adds its own fields:

| Kind | Fields | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bsale`, `wasabil`, `xpendit` | `accessToken` (string) | The provider's API access token. For `xpendit`, the API key of the enterprise. Whitespace is trimmed and must be non-empty. |
| `buk` | `tenant` (string), `accessToken` (string) | The address of your Buk and its API key. See [Connect Buk](#connect-buk). |
| `sii_mirror` | `rut` (string), `clave` (string) | The company's RUT and its SII password (Clave). The RUT takes the same format and check-digit rule as a [Portal Login](#post-portal-logins) `rut`. The Clave must be non-empty. See [SII](#sii). |
| `fintoc` | `exchangeToken` (string) | The token the Fintoc widget mints after you [create a link intent](#post-integrations-fintoc-link-intents) and the user completes the widget. |
| `plaid` | `publicToken` (string) | The public token Plaid Link mints after you [create a Link token](#post-integrations-plaid-link-tokens) and the user signs in to the bank. Connecting the same Plaid Item twice is refused with `409`. |
| `global66` | `clientId` (string), `clientSecret` (string), `accounts` (array) | Your Global66 client credentials and at least one account to read. Each account is `{ accountId, currency }`, where `currency` is an ISO 4217 code of three uppercase letters. A currency Sintropix does not know is refused with `INTEGRATION_CURRENCY_UNKNOWN_ERROR` before Global66 is called. |
| `cardda` | `apiKey` (string) | The Cardda API key. It selects the company, and the Integration discovers that company's one CLP wallet. |
| `dlocal` | `apiKey` (string), `secretKey` (string) | The dLocal Go API key and its paired secret key. They name the merchant, and the Integration discovers its one balance. |

For `sii_mirror`, the SII checks the RUT and Clave before Sintropix creates the Integration, so the request can take up to about a minute. A Clave the SII refuses answers `422` with `SII_CLAVE_REFUSED_ERROR`. When the SII gives no answer in time, the request answers `503` with `SII_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR` and creates nothing. Retry in a few minutes.

Credentials the Provider refuses answer `422` with `INTEGRATION_CONNECT_REJECTED_ERROR`. An API key Xpendit or Buk refuses answers `422` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR`. An external account another Integration already holds answers `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR`.

- **Response** (`201`): the Integration object (its `sourceFeeds` and `fedBankAccounts` are empty on creation).

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "d3e4f5a6-b7c8-4d9e-8f0a-1b2c3d4e5f6a",
    "kind": "bsale",
    "accessToken": "b7f3c9e1a2d4f6081e3c5a7b9d1f2e4c"
  }'
```

```json
{
  "id": "d3e4f5a6-b7c8-4d9e-8f0a-1b2c3d4e5f6a",
  "organizationId": "5f1c2a3b-4d5e-4f6a-8b7c-9d0e1f2a3b4c",
  "kind": "bsale",
```

Connect the SII with the company's RUT and Clave:

```bash

  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "e7a1b2c3-d4e5-4f6a-8b9c-0d1e2f3a4b5c",
    "kind": "sii_mirror",
    "rut": "76.123.456-0",
    "clave": "my-sii-clave"
  }'
```

Connect a dLocal Go merchant:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "0192a8b4-0000-7000-8000-000000000001",
    "kind": "dlocal",
    "apiKey": "dlocal_api_key",
    "secretKey": "dlocal_secret_key"

```

After you connect a bank Provider, list its accounts and feed them into Bank Accounts of any Entity. See [Bank Feeds](/api-reference/bank-feeds). After you connect a document Provider, feed a Source of any Entity with it. See [Source Feeds](/api-reference/source-feeds#post-source-feeds).

## POST `/integrations/fintoc/link-intents`

`POST /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/fintoc/link-intents`

Start the Fintoc connect flow. Sintropix creates a Link Intent at Fintoc and answers what the frontend needs to open the Fintoc widget. When the user completes the widget, it mints an exchange token. Send that token to [`POST /integrations`](#post-integrations) with `kind: "fintoc"`.

The widget needs a browser, so you can't complete a Fintoc connect from the API alone.

<Note>
  Only a `staff` caller can create a Link Intent. Any other caller gets `403` (`PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`). A `client` connects a bank by storing a [Bank Login](#portal-logins) instead.
</Note>

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `holderType` | `business` \| `individual` | Yes | Who holds the account at the bank. |

**Response**

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `widgetToken` | string | Token the Fintoc widget needs. |
| `publicKey` | string | Public key for the widget session. |

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/fintoc/link-intents" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"holderType":"business"}'
```

## POST `/integrations/plaid/link-tokens`

`POST /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/plaid/link-tokens`

Start the Plaid connect flow. Sintropix creates a Link token at Plaid and answers it. Open Plaid Link with it. When the user signs in to the bank, Plaid Link mints a public token. Send that token to [`POST /integrations`](#post-integrations) with `kind: "plaid"`.

Plaid Link needs a browser, so you can't complete a Plaid connect from the API alone. No request body.

**Response**

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `linkToken` | string | Token that opens Plaid Link. |

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/plaid/link-tokens" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent"
```

Then connect with the public token Plaid Link minted:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "4a5b6c7d-8e9f-4a0b-9c1d-2e3f4a5b6c7d",
    "kind": "plaid",
    "publicToken": "public-production-1a2b3c4d-5e6f-7a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d"
  }'
```

The response is the Integration object, with the bank's name in `institutionName`. Next, [list the bank's accounts](/api-reference/bank-feeds#get-discovered-accounts) and feed them into Bank Accounts.

## POST `/integrations/:integrationId/reconnect`

`POST /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId/reconnect`

Restore a Fintoc Integration whose bank stopped trusting the stored grant. You know it needs this when [its discovered accounts](/api-reference/bank-feeds#get-discovered-accounts) answer `status: "login_required"`. Run the Fintoc widget again and send the fresh exchange token. The Integration keeps its id and every feed it holds.

Only `fintoc` Integrations reconnect, and only a `staff` caller can reconnect one. Any other caller gets `403` (`PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`). To repair a Global66, Cardda, dLocal Go, or Plaid Integration whose credentials stopped working, disconnect it and connect again. For Plaid, this includes a bank login that expired. For Xpendit or Buk, [replace its API key](#put-integrations-integrationid-access-token) instead.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `exchangeToken` | string | Yes | Fresh token from the Fintoc widget. |

A grant from another bank or another holder, or an Integration that is not `fintoc`, is refused with `409` and `INTEGRATION_GRANT_MISMATCH_ERROR`.

- **Response:** the restored Integration object.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/$INTEGRATION_ID/reconnect" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"exchangeToken":"ex_tok_456"}'
```

## GET `/integrations`

`GET /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations`

List every Integration of the Organization, in the order they were connected, including any Mercado Pago, Mercado Libre, Gmail, Outlook, Polar, Google Sheets, or Stripe authorization connected from the app, and every Mailbox. Each item carries its Source Feeds, the Bank Accounts it feeds, and the Entities its feeds reach. A Google Sheets Integration carries its last General Ledger Export.

An API key confined to one Entity gets every Integration of the Organization, with only the Source Feeds and Bank Accounts of its own Entity. `routedEntities` still names every Entity a feed reaches.

- **Response:** array of Integration objects.

## PUT `/integrations/:integrationId/purpose`

`PUT /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId/purpose`

Set or clear the purpose of an Integration: what the account is for, in your own words. Write it so a colleague or an agent can tell your accounts apart, for example "Casilla de facturas de proveedores" for one Gmail account and "Correo personal de la gerenta" for another. Any kind of Integration takes a purpose.

In the app, open the Integration's panel. **Propósito** is the first row of its details, and you edit it in place.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `purpose` | string \| null | Yes | 1 to 500 characters after trimming. `null` clears it. A blank string is refused with `400`. Unknown keys are refused with `400`. |

- **Response:** the Integration object, with its new `purpose`.

```bash
curl -X PUT "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/$INTEGRATION_ID/purpose" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Name the supplier invoice mailbox" \
```

## DELETE `/integrations/:integrationId`

`DELETE /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId`

Remove an Integration. A [Mailbox](#a-mailbox-stays) can't be removed. Every feed of the Integration is removed as part of the same operation, in every Entity: its Source Feeds and the feed of each Bank Account it fed. The Sources, the Bank Accounts, and the documents and Movements already landed stay.

- For a `fintoc` Integration, Sintropix also deletes the Link at Fintoc, so the bank stops being read.
- For a `plaid` Integration, Sintropix first removes the bank login at Plaid. If Plaid already removed it, the disconnect still succeeds. If Plaid fails or can't be reached, the disconnect answers `424` or `503` and the Integration stays. Retry later.
- For a `sii_mirror` Integration, the SII mirror first deletes the company Clave and the legal representative's Clave, and stops reading that RUT. If the mirror is busy with that RUT or can't be reached, the disconnect answers `503` with `SII_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR`, and the Integration and both Claves stay. Retry in a few minutes.
- For a `stripe` Integration, Sintropix first uninstalls the Sintropix Stripe App from the account. If the account already uninstalled it, the disconnect still succeeds. If Stripe fails, the disconnect answers `424` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_FAILURE_ERROR` and the Integration stays. Retry later.
- A `mailbox` Integration is refused with `409` (`MAILBOX_NOT_REMOVABLE_ERROR`), because removing it would delete its email. See [A Mailbox stays](#a-mailbox-stays).
- For every other kind, Sintropix removes the stored credentials or authorization only. The grant on the provider's side is not revoked. For `google_sheets`, the Sheet keeps the last General Ledger written to it.

**Response**

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `deletedIntegrationId` | uuid | The removed Integration. |
| `unfedBankAccounts` | array | Every Bank Account that lost its feed, in any Entity, in the shape documented on [Bank Accounts](/api-reference/bank-accounts) without the summary fields, and with `feed` set to `null`. Empty when the Integration fed no Bank Account. |

```bash
curl -X DELETE "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/$INTEGRATION_ID" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent"
```

```json
{
  "deletedIntegrationId": "0192a8b4-0000-7000-8000-000000000001",
  "unfedBankAccounts": []
}
```

## SII

The SII Integration (`sii_mirror`) reads the company's DTEs and boletas de honorarios from the SII through Sintropix's SII mirror. Sintropix stores the company RUT as the Integration's `providerRef`, and none of the Clave. The Clave is held by the SII mirror, which signs in to the SII with it.

### Connect the SII

In the app, open **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **SII**. Type the company's **RUT** and its **Clave del SII**, then click **Conectar**. While the SII checks the Clave, the form shows **Verificando la Clave con el SII…**. A refused Clave shows **Clave incorrecta** under the field.

Through the API, send `kind: "sii_mirror"` with `rut` and `clave` to [`POST /integrations`](#post-integrations). The SII checks the Clave before the Integration exists:

| Outcome | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| The SII accepts the RUT and Clave | `201` with the Integration |
| The SII refuses the Clave | `422` with `SII_CLAVE_REFUSED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored |
| The SII gives no answer in time, or the mirror is busy with that RUT | `503` with `SII_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR`. Nothing is stored. Retry in a few minutes |
| Another Integration already reads that RUT | `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR` |

The connect asks for no Entity, and the Entity's tax id doesn't need to match the RUT. Route the documents with a [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds#sii-mirror-feeds) on the Entity they belong to. Each document a feed lands names the connected RUT, with the Entity's name, as the company's side: the issuer of a sale and the receiver of a purchase.

The app doesn't offer the [SII Login](#portal-logins) for new connections. SII Logins stored before keep showing in the list.

### The legal representative's Clave

The company Clave reads the RCV registers. The SII opens the PDF and XML of the DTEs only to the legal representative, so add the legal representative's own RUT and Clave to receive those [files](/api-reference/source-feeds#file-attachments). It's optional: the company Clave alone connects the SII.

In the app, open **Conectar integración** and pick **Clave del representante legal**, under the **Impuestos** tag. The option is dimmed until the company's SII is connected. When the Organization has two or more SII Integrations, the dialog first asks which company, by RUT. The option shows a check once the SII mirror holds a Clave or a certificate of the legal representative of every connected company.

Disconnecting the SII deletes the legal representative's Clave together with the company Clave.

#### PUT `/integrations/:integrationId/sii-representative-clave`

`PUT /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId/sii-representative-clave`

Add the legal representative's Clave to an SII Integration. The SII checks it at once, the same way as the company Clave. Sintropix stores neither the RUT nor the Clave. Sending it again replaces the Clave the SII mirror holds for that person.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rut` | string | Yes | The legal representative's own RUT, as a person. Same format and check-digit rule as a [Portal Login](#post-portal-logins) `rut`. The company's RUT is refused. |
| `clave` | string | Yes | The legal representative's SII password (Clave). Must be non-empty. |

**Response:** the Integration object.

| Status | Code | When it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `422` | `SII_REPRESENTATIVE_RUT_IS_COMPANY_RUT_ERROR` | `rut` is the company's own RUT. Nothing is sent to the SII |
| `422` | `SII_CLAVE_REFUSED_ERROR` | The SII refused the Clave |
| `422` | `INTEGRATION_REPRESENTATIVE_CLAVE_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR` | The Integration isn't `sii_mirror` |
| `503` | `SII_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR` | The SII gave no answer in time. Retry in a few minutes |

```bash
    "rut": "12.345.678-5",
```

## Gmail and Outlook

A `gmail` or `outlook` Integration connects one email account of the Organization. Sintropix keeps no copy of the account's email. You [search and read it](#read-email) on demand, and you can copy an attachment into an Entity, for example to attach a supplier's PDF to its Invoice. Sintropix only reads: it never sends, changes, or deletes email.

### Connect an email account

You connect an email account from the app, because Google and Microsoft ask for consent in a browser:

1. Open **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Gmail** or **Outlook**, under the **Correo** tag.
2. Continue on Composio, which holds the account's access for Sintropix, and allow access on Google or Microsoft with the account you want to connect.

Google asks for full access to the mailbox, but Sintropix only reads it. Outlook asks for read access to the mail only.

## Mailbox

A `mailbox` Integration is an email address that Sintropix holds for the Organization, such as `facturas@acme.sintropix.com`. Your clients and suppliers send or forward invoices and other documents to it, and you read them in Sintropix. There's no outside account to connect: Sintropix creates the address and receives its email. Sintropix doesn't turn that email into documents.

- A **Conversation** is a chain of emails on one subject, like a thread in an email client.
- An **email** is one message in a Conversation, received by the Mailbox or sent from it.

An Organization can hold as many Mailboxes as it needs, for example `facturas@` for supplier invoices and `rendiciones@` for expense receipts. Give each one a [purpose](#put-integrations-integrationid-purpose) so a colleague or Erwin can tell them apart.

### The email domain

Every Mailbox of an Organization takes its address at the Organization's **email domain**: a subdomain of `sintropix.com` that belongs to that Organization alone, such as `acme.sintropix.com`. Sintropix sets it for you. A Superadmin picks it for the Organization in the staff panel, with [`PATCH /organizations/:organizationId/email-domain`](/api-reference/organizations#patch-organizations-organizationid-email-domain). The Organization's `emailDomain` is `null` until then, and the Organization can't create a Mailbox. Ask Sintropix to set it.

Once a Mailbox lives at the domain, the domain can't change.

### Create a Mailbox

1. Open **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Casilla de correo**, under the **Correo** tag.
2. Enter the **Nombre de la casilla**: the part of the address before `@`.
3. Click **Crear casilla**.

The Mailbox's panel opens with its address, its purpose, and **Abrir correo**. When the Organization has no email domain yet, the form says *Esta empresa aún no tiene dominio de correo. Pídelo a Sintropix.*

Choose the name carefully: you can't rename a Mailbox, and you can't remove it.

### POST `/integrations/mailbox`

`POST /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/mailbox`

Create a Mailbox at the Organization's email domain. It needs the `books` write permission in the Organization, which a `client` or `staff` caller holds. An API key confined to one Entity gets `403` with `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`.

**Body.** Unknown keys are refused with `400`.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Client-generated id of the new Integration. An id already in use is refused with `409` (`INTEGRATION_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`). |
| `name` | string | Yes | The part of the address before `@`. Up to 64 characters: lowercase letters and digits, in groups joined by a single `.` or `-`, such as `facturas` or `compras.chile`. Anything else is refused with `400`. |

- An Organization with no email domain is refused with `409` (`MAILBOX_EMAIL_DOMAIN_NOT_SET_ERROR`).
- An address that already exists is refused with `409` (`MAILBOX_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`). `params.address` names it. Choose another name.
- An email domain that no longer receives email is refused with `409` (`EMAIL_DOMAIN_NOT_OPEN_ERROR`). `params.emailDomain` names it. Ask Sintropix to check it.
- When the Organization's email plan already holds every Mailbox it includes, the create is refused with `424` (`MAILBOX_LIMIT_REACHED_ERROR`). It answers the same until an administrator raises the plan, so don't retry it. The app says *El plan de correo llegó a su límite de casillas. Un administrador debe ampliarlo.*

**Response** `201`: the Integration object, with `kind: "mailbox"` and the new address as `providerRef`.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations/mailbox" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Mailbox for Acme's supplier invoices" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "id": "5c6d7e8f-9a0b-4c1d-8e2f-3a4b5c6d7e8f", "name": "facturas" }'
```

### A Mailbox stays

You can't disconnect a Mailbox, because removing it would delete its email. [`DELETE /integrations/:integrationId`](#delete-integrations-integrationid) on a Mailbox answers `409` (`MAILBOX_NOT_REMOVABLE_ERROR`), and the app shows no disconnect option in its panel. As a result, an Organization that holds a Mailbox can't be [deleted](/api-reference/organizations#delete-organizations-organizationid).

## Read email

You read the email of a `gmail`, `outlook`, or `mailbox` Integration with the same routes: search it, read an email or its whole Conversation as plain text, and copy an attachment into an Entity. Erwin can read all of them too, so you can ask him to find a supplier's invoice in any of the Organization's email accounts. He also searches them on his own as evidence when he reviews a record or leaves a Follow-up, and their [purpose](#put-integrations-integrationid-purpose) tells him which account to look in. See [Erwin](/core-concepts). A Mailbox reads with a few differences, listed in [Read a Mailbox's email](#read-a-mailboxs-email).

### Who can read email

Reading the email of the Organization's accounts is more than reading its books, so it has its own rule:

| Caller | Search and read email | Copy an attachment into an Entity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `staff` or `client` | Yes | Yes |
| `viewer` | No | No |
| An API key confined to one Entity | No | No |

A refused caller gets `403` with `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`.

Sintropix logs each email whose content it hands out and each attachment it copies, under the request's [Audit Envelope](/api-reference/audit-trail). A search isn't logged, because it returns only headers and a short snippet. Reading an email with an API key needs `x-audit-actor` and `x-audit-reason`, as a write does.

### The Email object

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | string | The email's id at Google or Microsoft, or a [Mailbox email's id](#read-a-mailboxs-email). Use it to read the email or copy an attachment. |
| `conversationId` | string | The id of the email's Conversation: the thread it belongs to. |
| `receivedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | When the email arrived. |
| `from` | object \| null | `{ name, address }` of the sender. `name` can be `null`. |
| `to`, `cc` | array | `{ name, address }` of each recipient. |
| `subject` | string | The subject. |
| `attachments` | array | `{ id, filename, contentType, byteSize }` of each attachment. Use `id` to [copy it](#post-api-entities-entityid-email-attachments). |
| `snippet` | string | On a search only: the first words of the body. |
| `body` | string | On a read only: the body as plain text. HTML is removed. A body over 20,000 characters is cut and ends with `[The body goes on; it was cut here.]`. |

### GET `/integrations/:integrationId/emails`

`GET /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId/emails`

**Query parameters.** Send at least one criterion. The criteria combine with AND, and each text criterion matches whole words, as the mailbox's own search does. Sintropix keeps only the letters, digits, and `@._-` of each term, so a term can't carry Gmail or Outlook search syntax.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `from` | string | No | Words of the sender's name or address. 1 to 200 characters. |
| `to` | string | No | Words of a recipient's name or address, in To or Cc. 1 to 200 characters. |
| `subject` | string | No | Words of the subject. 1 to 200 characters. |
| `text` | string | No | Words anywhere in the subject or body. 1 to 200 characters. |
| `after` | date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | No | Only email received on or after this day, in Santiago (Chile) time. |
| `before` | date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | No | Only email received before this day, in Santiago (Chile) time. Must be later than `after`. |
| `hasAttachment` | boolean | No | `true` for only email with attachments, `false` for only email without. |
| `attachmentType` | `pdf` \| `xml` \| `image` \| `spreadsheet` \| `any` | No | Only email with an attachment of this type. Can't be combined with `hasAttachment=false`. |
| `limit` | integer | No | Page size, 1 to 50. Default `10`. A Mailbox page holds at most 10. |
| `cursor` | string | No | The `nextCursor` of the previous page. Send the same criteria with it. |

A search with no criterion, an `after` that isn't before `before`, or an `attachmentType` with `hasAttachment=false` answers `400`.

| Field | Type | Description |

```bash
  -H "x-audit-reason: Read the supplier's reply about invoice 10452"
```

An email the account doesn't hold answers `404` with `INTEGRATION_EMAIL_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`.

### POST `/api/entities/:entityId/email-attachments`

Copy one attachment of an email into an Entity, then attach it to a record of that Entity. The copy lands where an [upload](/api-reference/attachments#two-phase-upload-flow) lands before it is confirmed:

1. Copy the attachment with this route. It answers an `attachmentId`.
2. Attach it to an Invoice, a Bank Movement, or a Ledger Entry of the same Entity with that host's [confirm route](/api-reference/attachments#confirm-an-upload), sending `attachmentId` and `filename`.

The route lives under the Entity because it writes to that Entity, so it needs the `books` write permission as well as the right to [read email](#who-can-read-email). The Integration must belong to the Entity's Organization.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `integrationId` | uuid | Yes | The Gmail, Outlook, or Mailbox Integration. |
| `emailId` | string | Yes | The `id` of the email. |
| `attachmentId` | string | Yes | The `id` of the attachment, from the email's `attachments`. |

The file must be one of the [accepted content types](/api-reference/attachments#two-phase-upload-flow), up to 25 MB. When the email sends it as a generic binary file, Sintropix takes its type from the file name's extension, such as `.pdf` or `.xml`.

**Response** (`201`)

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `attachmentId` | uuid | Pass it to the confirm route of the record. |
| `filename` | string | The attachment's file name. Pass it to the confirm route too. |
| `contentType` | string | The stored content type. |
| `byteSize` | integer | The file size in bytes. |
| `url` | string | A link that opens the copy for a few minutes. |

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/email-attachments" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Attach the supplier's PDF to invoice 10452" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "integrationId": "1b2c3d4e-5f6a-4b7c-8d9e-0f1a2b3c4d5e",
    "emailId": "18f2a7c3d4e5b6a1",
    "attachmentId": "1.2"
  }'
```

An attachment the email doesn't hold answers `404` with `INTEGRATION_EMAIL_ATTACHMENT_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`.

### Read a Mailbox's email

The routes above read a Mailbox with the same contract, with these differences:

- **Ids.** A Mailbox email's `id` joins two UUIDs with `+`, such as `4f1c2b7e-9a3d-4e5f-8b6a-0c1d2e3f4a5b+9b8a7c6d-5e4f-4a3b-8c2d-1e0f9a8b7c6d`. Use it as you receive it. Its `conversationId` is the id the [Conversation routes](#read-a-mailboxs-conversations) take.
- **Page size.** A search page holds at most 10 emails. A larger `limit` still reads 10. Each Conversation appears at most once on a page: the email of it that matched.
- **What a search covers.** Received, archived, and sent email. Spam and deleted email are left out. A new email can take about a minute to show up in a search. When more than 10,000 Conversations match, the search covers the 10,000 with the newest match, so narrow the criteria.
- **Recipients.** The `to` of a received email lists the addresses the email was delivered to, not its full To line.
- **Attachment types.** `contentType` comes from the file name's extension. It's empty when Sintropix doesn't accept that type.
- **Copy.** Only the attachments of a received email can be copied. An attachment of an email the Mailbox sent answers `404` (`INTEGRATION_EMAIL_ATTACHMENT_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`).
- **Refusals.** A Mailbox holds no access that can expire, so it never answers `INTEGRATION_GRANT_FAILED_ERROR`. When the Mailbox can't be read, the routes answer `424` (`INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_FAILURE_ERROR`) or `503` (`INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNREACHABLE_ERROR` or `INTEGRATION_NOT_CONFIGURED_ERROR`). Retry later.

### Email errors

| Status | Code | When it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `403` | `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR` | A `viewer`, or an API key confined to one Entity, reads or copies email |
| `404` | `INTEGRATION_NOT_FOUND_ERROR` | The Integration doesn't exist in the Organization, or, on a copy, in the Entity's Organization |
| `404` | `INTEGRATION_EMAIL_NOT_FOUND_ERROR` | The account holds no email with that id. `params.integrationId` and `params.emailId` |
| `404` | `INTEGRATION_EMAIL_ATTACHMENT_NOT_FOUND_ERROR` | The email holds no attachment with that id. `params.integrationId`, `params.emailId`, and `params.attachmentId` |
| `422` | `INTEGRATION_EMAIL_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR` | The Integration isn't `gmail`, `outlook`, or `mailbox`. `params.kind` names its kind |
| `424` | `INTEGRATION_GRANT_FAILED_ERROR` | The account's access expired or was revoked, as `grantHealth: "failed"` shows in the list. Disconnect the Integration and connect the account again |
| `424` | `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_FAILURE_ERROR` | Google or Microsoft refused the request, or the Mailbox can't be read right now |
| `503` | `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNREACHABLE_ERROR` | Google, Microsoft, or the Mailbox can't be reached. Retry later |
| `503` | `INTEGRATION_NOT_CONFIGURED_ERROR` | Sintropix can't read Mailboxes right now. Retry later |

## Google Sheets

A Google Sheets Integration keeps a copy of the Organization's General Ledger in a Google Sheet you own, with every Entity of the Organization in the same tabs. Use it to work on the ledger with Sheets formulas, or to share it with someone who has no access to Sintropix. Sintropix only writes: nothing you change in the Sheet reaches the books.

Each Integration exports to one Sheet, and each Sheet can be connected only once. One Google account can connect Sheets for several Organizations.

<Note>
  A `client` or `staff` caller can connect a Google Sheet and run an export. An API key confined to one Entity can't run an export, because it writes every Entity of the Organization. See [Access and roles](/api-reference/overview#access-and-roles).
</Note>

### Connect a Google Sheet

You connect Google Sheets from the app, because Google asks for consent in a browser:

1. Open **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Google Sheets**.
2. Paste the address of the Sheet, as your browser shows it with the Sheet open. It starts with `https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/`.
3. If an Entity of the Organization keeps [Books](/api-reference/books), pick the **Libro** the export reads for that Entity.
4. Continue on Google with an account that can edit the Sheet, and keep the permission to edit your spreadsheets checked.

When you return, Sintropix checks that the account can edit the Sheet by creating its tab, and connects the Integration. It refuses the connection, and stores nothing, when:

- The address isn't the address of a Google Sheet.
- Google can't find the Sheet with the account you authorized.
- The account can view the Sheet but can't edit it. Share the Sheet with that account as an editor, then connect it again.
- Another Integration already exports to that Sheet.

### What the Sheet holds

Each export rewrites three tabs whole: **Libro mayor**, **Plan de cuentas**, and **Dimensiones**. Sintropix finds each tab by its title, creates a missing one, and leaves the rest of the Sheet alone. If you rename or delete a tab, the next export creates a new one with that title.

Every Entity of the Organization goes into the same tabs, one Entity after another, in name order. Each Entity's rows are in that Entity's own currency.

The export reads the Book you picked for the Entity that keeps it. It reads every other Entity with no Book. If another Entity of the Organization keeps Books, the export fails with `400` and `BOOK_REQUIRED_ERROR`, and `params.entityId` names that Entity. That export records no run.

#### Libro mayor

**Libro mayor** holds the General Ledger of each Entity's current [Fiscal Year](/api-reference/reports#the-fiscal-year-in-reports), on the calendar of Santiago (Chile). It reads the live Ledger Entries (`active` and `reversed`), the same lines as the [general ledger report](/api-reference/reports#general-ledger). Each Entity's rows have one row per line, sorted by account code, then by date:

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Entidad** | The Entity's name |
| **Fecha** | The entry date, as a Sheets date |
| **Asiento** | The Ledger Entry, linked to the entry in the app |
| **Fecha de creación** | When the entry was written, as a Sheets date and time in Santiago (Chile) |
| **Código** | The Ledger Account code |
| **Cuenta** | The Ledger Account name |
| **Glosa** | The entry memo |
| **Contacto** | The [Partner](/api-reference/partners) on the line |
| **RUT** | The Partner's tax id |
| **País** | The Partner's country, as an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code |
| **Tipo de contacto** | `Proveedor`, `Cliente`, or `Ambos`: what the row's Entity's books say the Partner is to it. Empty when they say neither |
| One column per [Dimension](/api-reference/dimensions) | The name of the Dimension Value the line carries for that Dimension, or empty. Every Dimension of the Organization takes a column, in name order, even one no line uses |
| **Moneda** | The Entity's currency, which **Débito**, **Crédito**, and **Saldo** are in |
| **Débito** | The debit amount |
| **Crédito** | The credit amount |
| **Saldo** | The account's running balance after this line, signed by the account's normal side |
| **Moneda transacción** | The line's transaction currency. The Entity's currency for a line booked in it |
| **Monto transacción** | The line's amount in its transaction currency |
| **Tipo de cambio** | The line's exchange rate. `1` for a line booked in the Entity's currency |

Amounts and rates are numbers in currency units, not minor units, so Sheets formulas work on them.

Each account of each Entity opens with a **Saldo inicial** row dated the first day of the Entity's Fiscal Year. It holds what the account carries into the Fiscal Year, in **Saldo** and as its own transaction at a rate of `1`: `0` on an income or expense account, the result of the closed years on the retained earnings account, and the balance before the year on any other account. It has no entry, creation date, Partner, Dimension Value, debit, or credit. An account with no line this Fiscal Year shows that row alone when its balance isn't zero, and doesn't show at all when its balance is zero.

#### Plan de cuentas

**Plan de cuentas** holds one row per Ledger Account of each Entity, sorted by code:

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Entidad** | The Entity's name |
| **Código** | The account code |
| **Nombre** | The account name |
| **Tipo** | The account type, in Spanish: `Activo`, `Pasivo`, `Patrimonio`, `Ingreso`, `Costo de ventas`, `Gasto operacional`, `Otros ingresos`, or `Otros gastos` |
| **Grupo** | The account's own group, as its code prefix and its name |
| **Descripción** | The account description |

#### Dimensiones

**Dimensiones** holds one row per Dimension Value of the Organization, sorted by Dimension name, then by code. A Dimension with no value has no row.

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Dimensión** | The Dimension's name |
| **Código** | The value's code |
| **Valor** | The value's name |
| **Código padre** | The code of the value's parent, or empty |

Because every export rewrites the whole tabs, running it again is safe: an entry posted, changed, or reversed since the last export shows up on the next one.

### When the export runs

Sintropix exports every Google Sheets Integration every four hours. To export right away, click **Sincronizar ahora** on the Integration's panel, or call [`POST /integrations/:integrationId/general-ledger-exports`](#post-integrations-integrationid-general-ledger-exports). The panel shows when the last export ran and how many lines it wrote, or why it failed.

### The General Ledger Export object

A General Ledger Export is one run that writes the General Ledger of every Entity of the Organization to the Sheet. It states either the lines it wrote or why it wrote nothing: exactly one of `lineCount` and `failure` is set.

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Export id. |
| `organizationId` | uuid | Owning Organization. |
| `integrationId` | uuid | The Google Sheets Integration. |
| `occurredAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | When the export ran. |
| `lineCount` | integer \| null | Ledger lines written to **Libro mayor**, across every Entity. The **Saldo inicial** rows don't count. `null` when the export failed. |
| `failure` | string \| null | Why Google refused the export (see below). `null` when the export succeeded. |

| `failure` | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `authorization_expired` | The Google authorization expired or was revoked | Renew the authorization, or disconnect the Integration and connect the Sheet again |
| `sheet_not_found` | Google can't find the Sheet: it was deleted, or the account lost access | Check the Sheet, then connect it again |
| `sheet_not_writable` | The account can no longer edit the Sheet | Give the account edit access, then export again |
| `provider_unreachable` | Google couldn't be reached | Export again in a few minutes |
| `provider_failure` | Google failed | Export again in a few minutes |

## Xpendit

An `xpendit` Integration connects one Xpendit enterprise with its API key. Sintropix reads Xpendit and never writes to it. An enterprise holds one or more **Subsidiaries**, Xpendit's word for the accounts its expenses and funds belong to. Sintropix calls each one a **Provider Account**.

With an Xpendit Integration you can:

- [Read what Xpendit holds](#read-what-xpendit-holds): each Subsidiary's recent expenses and its open funds.
- Route each Subsidiary to a Source of [Expense Renditions](/api-reference/sources#sources-that-hold-expense-renditions) of an Entity, with an [expense Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds#expense-feeds). Each [sync](/api-reference/source-feeds#sync-an-expense-feed) lands the expenses Xpendit approved as approved Expense Claims, booked as Expense Renditions with their receipts attached.

### Connect Xpendit

In the app, open **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Xpendit**, under the **Rendiciones** tag. Paste the API key of your Xpendit enterprise. Through the API, send it to [`POST /integrations`](#post-integrations) with `kind: "xpendit"` and `accessToken`.

Sintropix checks the key with Xpendit before it stores it:

| Outcome | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Xpendit accepts the key | `201` with the Integration. `providerRef` is the id of the enterprise |
| Xpendit refuses the key | `422` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored |
| Another Integration already connects that enterprise | `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR`. `params.providerRef` names the enterprise |

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Connect the Xpendit enterprise to read its expenses" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "7c8d9e0f-1a2b-4c3d-8e4f-5a6b7c8d9e0f",
    "kind": "xpendit",
    "accessToken": "xpendit-api-key"
  }'
```

### PUT `/integrations/:integrationId/access-token`

`PUT /api/organizations/:organizationId/integrations/:integrationId/access-token`

Replace the API key of an Xpendit or Buk Integration, for example after you rotate the key in the Provider. For Xpendit, the new key must open the same enterprise. For Buk, Sintropix checks the new key at the tenant the Integration already names. The Integration keeps its id and its Feeds. In the app, open the Integration's panel and click **Reemplazar clave de API**.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `accessToken` | string | Yes | The new API key. Whitespace is trimmed and must be non-empty. Unknown keys are refused with `400`. |

Sintropix checks the new key with the Provider first. If it fails any check, the stored key stays:

| Status | Code | When it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `409` | `INTEGRATION_GRANT_MISMATCH_ERROR` | The key opens another enterprise. `params.integrationId` |
| `422` | `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR` | Xpendit or Buk refuses the key |
| `422` | `INTEGRATION_CONNECT_REJECTED_ERROR` | For Buk, the key reaches no Empresa of the tenant |
| `422` | `INTEGRATION_TOKEN_REPLACEMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR` | The Integration isn't `xpendit` or `buk`. `params.integrationId` and `params.kind` |

- **Response** (`200`): the Integration object.

```bash

  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
```

## Buk

A `buk` Integration connects your [Buk](https://www.buk.cl), the payroll and HR system, with an API key. Sintropix only reads Buk and never writes to it. Your Buk lives at its own address, such as `acme.buk.cl`: Sintropix calls the subdomain, `acme`, the **tenant**. A tenant holds one or more **Empresas**, and each Empresa the key reaches is a **Provider Account**.

A Buk Integration lists the Empresas the key reaches, each with its RUT. It doesn't read payroll and feeds nothing yet.

### Create the API key in Buk

The Buk API comes with the **Plataforma Pro** plan. If **Configuración → Accesos API** is greyed out in Buk, your plan doesn't include it: ask your Buk account manager. A Buk administrator creates the key:

1. In Buk, open **Configuración → Accesos API** and click **Crear nueva API Key**.
2. Name it for what it's for, such as "Sintropix". Create a key for Sintropix alone, and don't share it with another system.
3. Pick these permissions:
   - **Nivel:** Lectura. Sintropix only reads.
   - **Permisos de empleados:** Lectura.
   - **Permisos de contabilidad:** Lectura.
   - **Datos sensibles:** Sí.
   - **Empresas:** the Empresas Sintropix keeps the books of. Leave out any Empresa that isn't yours.
4. Click **Actualizar API Key**, then **Guardar**.
5. In the list of keys, click the eye icon of the new key and copy the token.

If your Buk restricts access by IP in **Whitelist de acceso**, contact Sintropix before you connect.

### Connect Buk

In the app, open **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Buk**, under the **Remuneraciones** tag. Type the address of your Buk in **Dirección de Buk**, such as `tuempresa.buk.cl`, paste the token in **Clave de API**, and click **Conectar**. The panel then lists the Empresas the key reaches under **Empresas en Buk**, each with its RUT.

Through the API, send [`POST /integrations`](#post-integrations) with `kind: "buk"`:

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tenant` | string | Yes | The address of your Buk. You can send the tenant alone (`acme`), the host (`acme.buk.cl`), or a pasted address (`https://acme.buk.cl/apidocs`): Sintropix keeps the tenant, in lowercase. The tenant must be one subdomain of `buk.cl`: letters, digits, and inner hyphens, up to 63 characters. Any other address, such as `acme_corp` or `acme.buk.cl.example.com`, is refused with `400` before the key is sent anywhere. |
| `accessToken` | string | Yes | The token of the Buk API key. Whitespace is trimmed and must be non-empty. |

Sintropix sends the key only to `https://<tenant>.buk.cl`, and checks it there before it stores it:

| Outcome | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Buk accepts the key, and it reaches at least one Empresa | `201` with the Integration. `providerRef` is the tenant |
| Buk refuses the key | `422` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored |
| No Buk lives at that address, or the key reaches no Empresa | `422` with `INTEGRATION_CONNECT_REJECTED_ERROR`. Nothing is stored. The app says *Buk no tiene esa dirección, o la clave no llega a ninguna empresa.* Check the address and the Empresas of the key in Buk |
| Another Integration already connects that tenant | `409` with `INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR`. `params.providerRef` names the tenant |
| Buk can't be reached, or the address sends Sintropix elsewhere | `503` with `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNREACHABLE_ERROR`. Nothing is stored, and the key goes nowhere else |

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/integrations" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "x-audit-reason: Connect the client's Buk" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "2e3f4a5b-6c7d-4e8f-9a0b-1c2d3e4f5a6b",
    "kind": "buk",
    "tenant": "acme.buk.cl",
    "accessToken": "buk-api-key"
  }'
```

To list the Empresas, call [`GET /integrations/:integrationId/provider-accounts`](#get-integrations-integrationid-provider-accounts):

```json
[
  { "id": "101", "name": "Comercial Andes SpA", "taxId": "76.123.456-0" },
  { "id": "102", "name": "Servicios Andes Ltda.", "taxId": "77.234.567-4" }
]
```

### Replace or remove the Buk key

- **Replace it.** Create a new key in Buk. Then, in the Integration's panel, click **Reemplazar clave de API**, or call [`PUT /integrations/:integrationId/access-token`](#put-integrations-integrationid-access-token). Sintropix checks the new key at the same tenant, and the panel lists the Empresas of the new key. Then delete the old key in Buk.
- **Remove it.** [`DELETE /integrations/:integrationId`](#delete-integrations-integrationid), or **Desconectar** in the panel, removes the key Sintropix stores. Buk keeps the key working until you delete it too, in **Configuración → Accesos API**.

## Stripe

A `stripe` Integration connects one Stripe account with read-only access. Sintropix only reads: it never charges, refunds, or withdraws money. It feeds nothing yet.

### Connect Stripe

You connect Stripe from the app, because Stripe asks for consent in a browser:

1. Open **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones**, click **Conectar integración**, and pick **Stripe**, under the **Pagos** tag.
2. Continue on Stripe, pick the account to connect, and accept the read permissions of the Sintropix Stripe App. This installs the App in that account.

When you return, the Integration's panel shows what the connection reaches: the account's business name, **País**, **Moneda por defecto**, and **Modo** (**En vivo** or **Prueba**). The Integration object carries the same facts in `accountName`, `country`, `defaultCurrency`, and `mode`.

If you cancel on Stripe, nothing is connected. Each Stripe account can be connected only once.

### Disconnect Stripe

Disconnecting uninstalls the Sintropix Stripe App from the account, then removes the Integration. See [`DELETE /integrations/:integrationId`](#delete-integrations-integrationid).

## Portal Logins

A Portal Login is the login of a portal that no Provider reads. Unlike an Integration, a Portal Login belongs to one Entity, and its routes live under `/api/entities/:entityId/portal-logins`. Sintropix stores the login and the password encrypted and uses them only to read the portal. No read ever returns them, so not even you can see them again in Sintropix. A Portal Login has one of four kinds:

| Kind | Portal | What you store |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bank_login` | A bank's portal | The bank, picked from the [Banks catalog](/api-reference/reference-data#get-banks), plus the RUT and the password you sign in with |
| `getnet_login` | The Getnet merchant portal | The username or email, and the password you sign in with |
| `sii_login` | The SII, as a company or as a person. To connect the SII, use the [SII Integration](#sii) instead | The taxpayer type, plus the RUT and the SII password (clave) you sign in with |
| `clay_login` | Clay, the Chilean ERP you migrate your books from, so the Sintropix team can migrate them | A name you choose, plus either an API key or the username and password you sign in with |

Sintropix doesn't sign in to the portal when you store a Portal Login, so a wrong login or password isn't refused. A Portal Login has no health status: it's stored, never "working" or "failed". To change the password, delete the Portal Login and store it again.

In the app, connect one from **Configuración de empresa → Integraciones**: pick your bank by its name, or pick **Getnet** or **Clay** (under the **ERP** tag). Pick the **Entidad legal** the login belongs to. It's preset when the Organization has only one Entity. For **Clay**, give it a **Nombre** (prefilled as "Clay") and pick how it signs in: **API key** or **Usuario y clave**. Name each Clay Login after the company it holds, so you can store one per company you migrate. A Portal Login shows in the list with the status **Clave guardada**.

<Note>
  A `client` or `staff` caller can store and delete Portal Logins. A `viewer` can only list them. See [Access and roles](/api-reference/overview#access-and-roles).
</Note>

### The Portal Login object

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Portal Login id. |
| `entityId` | uuid | Owning Entity. |
| `kind` | `bank_login` \| `getnet_login` \| `sii_login` \| `clay_login` | The kind of portal. |
| `label` | string | The portal's name. For a `bank_login`, the bank's name in the [Banks catalog](/api-reference/reference-data#get-banks). For a `getnet_login`, always `Getnet`. For a `sii_login`, `SII Empresa` or `SII Persona`, by its `holderType`. For a `clay_login`, the name you gave it. |
| `bankCode` | string \| null | For a `bank_login`, the bank's three-digit CMF code. `null` for every other kind. |
| `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | When the Portal Login was stored and last changed. |

### POST `/portal-logins`

Store a Portal Login for the Entity.

**Body.** The shape depends on `kind`. Every kind takes an `id`:

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Client-generated id. Retrying with the same id is answered `409` (`CREDENTIAL_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`). |
| `kind` | `bank_login` \| `getnet_login` \| `sii_login` \| `clay_login` | Yes | The kind of portal. |

Each kind adds its own fields:

| Kind | Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bank_login` | `bankCode` | string | The bank's three-digit CMF code, from [`GET /banks`](/api-reference/reference-data#get-banks), for example `001` for Banco de Chile. A code the catalog doesn't hold is refused with `400` (`CREDENTIAL_BANK_UNKNOWN_ERROR`). |
| `bank_login` | `rut` | string | The RUT you sign in to the bank with. Dots, spaces, and the hyphen are optional, and a lowercase `k` is accepted. A RUT whose check digit doesn't match is refused with `400` (`RUT_INVALID_ERROR`). |
| `bank_login` | `password` | string | The bank password. Must be non-empty. |
| `getnet_login` | `username` | string | The username or email you sign in to the Getnet portal with. Must be non-empty. |
| `getnet_login` | `password` | string | The Getnet password. Must be non-empty. |
| `sii_login` | `holderType` | `business` \| `individual` | Whose SII login it is: `business` for the company, `individual` for a person, such as its legal representative. |
| `sii_login` | `rut` | string | The RUT you sign in to the SII with. Same format and check-digit rule as the `bank_login` `rut`. |
| `sii_login` | `password` | string | The SII password (clave). Must be non-empty. |
| `clay_login` | `label` | string | The name you give this Clay Login, for example the company it holds. Whitespace is trimmed and must be non-empty. |
| `clay_login` | `method` | `api_key` \| `password` | How Clay is opened: with an API key, or with the username and password of the Clay web app. |
| `clay_login` | `apiKey` | string | The Clay API key. Required, and must be non-empty, when `method` is `api_key`. |
| `clay_login` | `username` | string | The username you sign in to Clay with. Required, and must be non-empty, when `method` is `password`. |
| `clay_login` | `password` | string | The Clay password. Required, and must be non-empty, when `method` is `password`. |

Each name can be used once per Entity. Sintropix names a Bank Login after its bank, so an Entity holds at most one Bank Login per bank. A second one for the same bank is refused with `409` (`CREDENTIAL_LABEL_ALREADY_USED_ERROR`). A `bank_login` body takes no `label`. Sintropix names every Getnet Login `Getnet`, so an Entity holds at most one. A second Getnet Login is refused with the same error. To store another Getnet login, delete the first one. Sintropix names an SII Login by its `holderType`: `SII Empresa` for `business` and `SII Persona` for `individual`. An Entity holds at most one of each, and a second one of the same `holderType` is refused with the same error. A `sii_login` body takes no `label`. You name each Clay Login, so an Entity can hold as many as it needs. A Clay Login whose name the Entity already uses is refused with the same error.

- **Response** (`201`): the Portal Login object.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/portal-logins" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "5c6d7e8f-9a0b-4c1d-8e2f-3a4b5c6d7e8f",
    "kind": "bank_login",
    "bankCode": "001",
    "rut": "12.345.678-5",
    "password": "my-bank-password"
  }'
```

```json
{
  "id": "5c6d7e8f-9a0b-4c1d-8e2f-3a4b5c6d7e8f",
  "entityId": "8b0a7d5e-6a3c-4f2b-9e1d-2c4b6a8f0e21",
```

Delete a Portal Login and its stored login and password. Sintropix stops reading the portal with it. Your login at the portal is unchanged.

## Status codes

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `200` | Success on `GET`, `PUT`, `DELETE`, and a General Ledger Export, including one Google refused. |
| `201` | Integration, Mailbox, or Portal Login created, or an email attachment copied into an Entity. |
| `400` | Validation error (including a Portal Login RUT that fails its check digit, `RUT_INVALID_ERROR`, or a Bank Login `bankCode` the Banks catalog doesn't hold, `CREDENTIAL_BANK_UNKNOWN_ERROR`), missing `x-audit-actor` on a key-authenticated mutation, or a General Ledger Export while an Entity other than the one of the picked Book keeps Books (`BOOK_REQUIRED_ERROR`). |
| `403` | A caller other than `staff` creates a Fintoc Link Intent or reconnects a Fintoc Integration, or an API key confined to one Entity connects, renews, or disconnects an Integration or runs a General Ledger Export, or a `viewer` or an API key confined to one Entity reads or copies email, reads a Mailbox's Conversations, or reads the expenses of an Xpendit account, or an API key confined to one Entity creates a Mailbox (`PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`). |
| `404` | Organization, Entity, Integration (`INTEGRATION_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), Portal Login (`CREDENTIAL_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), Mailbox (`MAILBOX_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), Conversation (`CONVERSATION_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), email (`INTEGRATION_EMAIL_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), or email attachment (`INTEGRATION_EMAIL_ATTACHMENT_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`) not found. |
| `409` | Integration id already exists, the Organization has no email domain for a Mailbox (`MAILBOX_EMAIL_DOMAIN_NOT_SET_ERROR`), a Mailbox address already exists (`MAILBOX_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`), a disconnect names a Mailbox (`MAILBOX_NOT_REMOVABLE_ERROR`), the external account, such as an SII RUT, is already connected (`INTEGRATION_ACCOUNT_ALREADY_CONNECTED_ERROR`), a reconnect names another grant or a non-Fintoc Integration, or a replaced Xpendit key opens another enterprise (`INTEGRATION_GRANT_MISMATCH_ERROR`), a Mailbox create names an email domain that no longer receives email (`EMAIL_DOMAIN_NOT_OPEN_ERROR`), a Portal Login id already exists (`CREDENTIAL_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`), or a Portal Login names a portal the Entity already uses, including a second Bank Login for the same bank, a second Getnet Login, a second SII Login of the same `holderType`, or a Clay Login whose name is in use (`CREDENTIAL_LABEL_ALREADY_USED_ERROR`), or another General Ledger Export of the Integration is still running (`GENERAL_LEDGER_EXPORT_RUNNING_ERROR`). |
| `410` | The Provider no longer holds the grant (`INTEGRATION_GRANT_GONE_ERROR`). Disconnect the Integration and connect it again. |
| `422` | The Provider refused the credentials (`INTEGRATION_CONNECT_REJECTED_ERROR`), Xpendit or Buk refused the API key (`INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR`), an API key replacement names an Integration that isn't `xpendit` or `buk` (`INTEGRATION_TOKEN_REPLACEMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR`), an Xpendit expense or Provider Account read names an Integration that has none (`INTEGRATION_EXPENSES_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR`), a Global66 account names a currency Sintropix does not know (`INTEGRATION_CURRENCY_UNKNOWN_ERROR`), a General Ledger Export names an Integration that isn't `google_sheets` (`GENERAL_LEDGER_EXPORT_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR`), the SII refused a Clave (`SII_CLAVE_REFUSED_ERROR`), a legal representative's Clave names the company's own RUT (`SII_REPRESENTATIVE_RUT_IS_COMPANY_RUT_ERROR`), the legal representative's Clave names an Integration that isn't `sii_mirror` (`INTEGRATION_REPRESENTATIVE_CLAVE_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR`), or an email route names an Integration that isn't `gmail`, `outlook`, or `mailbox` (`INTEGRATION_EMAIL_NOT_SUPPORTED_ERROR`). |
| `424` | The Provider refused the request (`BANK_FEED_PROVIDER_FAILURE_ERROR`, `BANK_FEED_PROVIDER_UNAUTHORIZED_ERROR`, `BANK_FEED_PROVIDER_ROLE_REFUSED_ERROR`, or `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_FAILURE_ERROR`), the access of an email account failed (`INTEGRATION_GRANT_FAILED_ERROR`), or the email plan holds every Mailbox it includes (`MAILBOX_LIMIT_REACHED_ERROR`). See [Provider refusals](/api-reference/overview#provider-refusals). |
| `503` | The Provider can't be reached right now (`BANK_FEED_PROVIDER_UNREACHABLE_ERROR` or `INTEGRATION_PROVIDER_UNREACHABLE_ERROR`), Sintropix can't connect that Provider right now (`INTEGRATION_NOT_CONFIGURED_ERROR`), Sintropix can't store a Portal Login right now (`CREDENTIAL_NOT_CONFIGURED_ERROR`), the SII gave no answer in time or the SII mirror is busy with that RUT (`SII_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR`), or a Mailbox can't be created or read right now (`MAILBOX_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR`). Retry later. |
