Invoices are Sintropix's model for both AR (issued to customers) and AP (received from suppliers). Each Invoice can be booked to a Ledger Entry and, in Chile, linked to a Documento Tributario Electrónico (DTE) issued through the SII. This page covers listing, creating, editing, booking, and DTE-linking Invoices, individually or in bulk.

## Status

Invoices carry two derived, read-only status values on the listing rows:

| Field | Values | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking status | `unbooked`, `booked`, `excluded` | An Invoice is `booked` when a Ledger Entry backs it (`bookingEntryId` is set), `excluded` when someone [excluded it from the books](#exclude-an-invoice-from-the-books), and `unbooked` otherwise. Only an `unbooked` Invoice is pending work. |
| `paymentStatus` | `paid`, `partial`, `unpaid`, `null` | Derived from the open amount of the Control Line (ADR 0018) in the Entity's functional currency. `null` means the Invoice is not booked and settles nothing yet. A [`tax_only`](#tax-only-documents) document owes nothing, so its `paymentStatus` is always `null`, even when booked. |

The `GET /invoices/pages` listing accepts a combined filter `statusFilter` with values `all`, `notPaid`, `paid`, `unbooked`, `booked`, `excluded`, and `notExcluded`, and supports `groupBy=bookingStatus` and `groupBy=paymentStatus`. `all` lists every document, excluded ones included. `notExcluded` lists every document still headed for the books, and `excluded` lists only the excluded ones. `notPaid` is the filter a payment run works from.

Amounts are integers in the minor units of their currency (`1190000` is CLP 1,190,000; `125050` is USD 1,250.50). Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, tax periods are `YYYY-MM`, and timestamps are ISO 8601.

## Document kinds

Every Invoice has a `documentKind`. It says what the booking does with the Invoice's total:

| `documentKind` | What its booking does | What it owes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `invoice` | Books the claim or obligation on its [Control Line](#post-/invoices/invoiceid/book): a debit on a receivable account, or a credit on a payable account. | Its total, until the Control Line is settled. |
| `credit_note` | Books the opposite side of an `invoice`, such as a Chilean DTE `61`. You apply it to an invoice with an ordinary Match. | It settles an invoice once matched. |
| `tax_only` | Books tax only, with no Control Line and no claim or obligation. | Nothing. |

### Tax-only documents

Use `tax_only` for a document that only declares tax. The usual case is a factura de compra (DTE `46`) for a purchase abroad. The foreign vendor's own Invoice already holds the payable, so the 46 books only the IVA: a debit to IVA Crédito Fiscal and a credit to IVA Retenido.

A `tax_only` document:

- Books a balanced Ledger Entry with no Control Line. Send `controlLineId: null` when you [book it](#post-/invoices/invoiceid/book). Its `controlLineId` and `controlAccountId` stay `null`, so read `bookingEntryId` to tell whether it's booked.
- Can't have a line on an account whose sub-type is `payable`, `expense_rendition_payable`, or `receivable`. A claim there would be an open item that no Invoice owns. The booking is refused with `409` and `TAX_ONLY_LINE_ON_CONTROL_ACCOUNT_ERROR`, and [Reclassify](/api-reference/ledger-entries) refuses to move a line of its booking onto such an account the same way.
- Owes nothing, booked or not. Its `paymentStatus`, `paymentDate`, and `openAmount` are `null`. It never appears in [Payables to Plan](/api-reference/reports#payables-to-plan), a [Payment Order](/api-reference/payment-batches) can't name it, and it adds nothing to the amounts of [customer status](/api-reference/reports#customer-status).
- Can't be [deferred](#deferrals).
- Is never a match candidate for a [Bank Movement](/api-reference/bank-movements).
- Is pending work while it's unbooked, like any other document, and you can [exclude it](#exclude-an-invoice-from-the-books).

Both directions accept `tax_only`. A vendor's [Booking Template](/api-reference/partners#booking-templates) can also book its new 46s as `tax_only` automatically.

### Switch the kind

To make an unbooked Invoice `tax_only`, send `{ "documentKind": "tax_only" }` to [`PATCH /invoices/:invoiceId`](#patch-/invoices/invoiceid). Send `"invoice"` to switch it back. In the app, open the document and pick **Solo impuesto** in **Clase de documento**. The listing then shows the document as, for example, **46 · solo impuesto**, with no payment status.

The switch is refused when:

| Case | Status | Code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Invoice is booked. Unbook it, switch the kind, and book it again. | `409` | `INVOICE_BOOKED_FIELDS_LOCKED_ERROR` |
| The Invoice is a credit note. A credit note never switches, and nothing switches to `credit_note`. | `409` | `INVOICE_DOCUMENT_KIND_FIXED_ERROR` |
| A [Payment Order](/api-reference/payment-batches) names the Invoice. Remove the orders first. | `409` | `INVOICE_PAYMENT_ORDERS_PIN_ERROR` |
| The Invoice lands a [Sales Summary](/api-reference/sii#sales-summaries). The SII sets its kind. | `409` | `DTE_HELD_BY_SALES_SUMMARY_ERROR` |

A Source Feed sync never changes the kind of an Invoice that already landed. Documents booked as `invoice` before `tax_only` existed keep their kind. To correct one, unbook it, switch it to `tax_only`, and book it again.

## Ontop invoices

[Ontop](https://www.getontop.com) pays a company's foreign contractors from a prepaid USD wallet, and bills the company for each payment. Erwin can load those invoices, book them, and match each payment to its invoices. Ask him in a Slack channel bound to the Entity's Organization through an [Agent Channel](/api-reference/agent-channels), for example *"carga las facturas de Ontop"*. To load the wallet's movements first, ask *"carga Ontop"*. See [Ontop wallet](/api-reference/bank-movements#ontop-wallet).

Ontop issues three kinds of invoice:

| Prefix | What it bills | Paid from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ONTW` | One contractor in one payment | The wallet or a card |
| `ONTF` | The fee of a payment charged to a card | A card |
| `OMF` | The monthly membership | A card |

### Before the first load

Erwin reads the facts below from the Ontop page of his [wiki](/core-concepts). Ask him to note them there before the first load. If the page is missing or lacks a fact, he stops and asks. He never creates a Partner, a Source, a Bank Account, or an account.

- The Partner of Ontop, the [Source](/api-reference/sources) of its invoices, and the Bank Account of the wallet.
- The control account of the invoices, and the account for the exchange difference.
- The expense account of each category of contractor, and the category of each contractor.
- The expense account for Ontop's fees (`ONTF` and `OMF`).

The Entity also needs a login Credential for Ontop.

### What a load does

Each load reads from 3 days before the newest issue date among the Partner of Ontop's Invoices to today. On the first load, Erwin asks from which date to start.

1. **Create.** Erwin creates each invoice whose number no Invoice of the Partner of Ontop has yet, as an AP Invoice in USD:

   | Field | Value |
   | --- | --- |
   | `partnerId`, `sourceId` | The Partner and the Source of Ontop |
   | `direction`, `documentKind`, `currency` | `payable`, `invoice`, `USD` |
   | `documentNumber` | The Ontop number, for example `ONTW650770` |
   | `total` | The invoice's total in USD, in cents |
   | `issueDate` | The date of the payment the invoice belongs to. For an `OMF`, its issue date |
   | `memo` | `Ontop <number> · <contractor>`, or `Ontop <number> · Membresía` for an `OMF` |
   | `metadata` | In this order, when Ontop shows them: `Tipo Ontop`, `Worker ID`, `Contratista`, `Fecha pago contratista`, and each part of the pay that isn't zero (`Compensation`, `Bonus`, `Commissions`, `Reimbursements`, `Total compensation`, `Ontop fees`) |

2. **Book.** Erwin books each new `ONTW` to the account of its contractor's category, and each `ONTF` and `OMF` to the fee account. The entry credits the control account and debits the expense account, on the issue date, at the [FX rate](/api-reference/fx-rates) of USD for that date, or of the last earlier date with a rate. When the wiki has no category for a contractor, Erwin leaves the invoice unbooked with a `question` [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups) that offers one option per category. He never guesses a category from the contractor's role.
3. **Match.** Erwin matches each payment to its invoices once all of them are booked and open:
   - A `PAYMENT` of the wallet pairs with the Ontop payment made from the wallet, with the same amount and a date at most 3 days away.
   - A card charge whose description names Ontop pairs with the Ontop payment made by card, or with an `OMF`, with the same amount and a date at most 3 days away.

   He matches the Movement to the first invoice, [assigns](/api-reference/bank-movements#post-/movements/movementid/assign) the others from the Suspense Account, and books what's left to the exchange difference account on the last invoice. If an invoice of the payment isn't booked yet, the Movement waits unbooked with a `waiting` Follow-up that names the invoices. If two payments fit, he pairs none and leaves a `question` Follow-up.

The other wallet movements get their own treatment:

- A **top-up by wire** is recorded as an [internal transfer](/api-reference/bank-movements#record-an-internal-transfer) from the company's bank account.
- A **top-up by card** isn't booked. Erwin leaves a `question` Follow-up.
- A **cashback** gets a `proposal` Follow-up that books it to the account the wiki gives, or a `question` one when the wiki gives none.

Erwin never changes or books again an Invoice that was already in Sintropix, and never deletes an Invoice or a Movement. He reports the invoices he created and booked, the payments he matched, each Follow-up he left, the contractors the wiki doesn't have yet, and the wallet's balance in Ontop next to its Bank Account's balance.

<Note>
  Erwin loads the invoices as Ontop issues them. The Chilean tax treatment of these payments, such as Impuesto Adicional or a factura de compra, is out of scope.
</Note>

## Expense renditions

A **rendition** (rendición) is an expense that a person paid with their own money for the company, which the company pays back. Sintropix records each rendition as a `payable` Invoice whose Partner is the person who paid, never the merchant. Erwin loads renditions from [Rindegastos](https://www.rindegastos.com) and books them.

People can also submit a rendition in Sintropix itself, as an [Expense Claim](/api-reference/expense-claims), or report it to Erwin, who [submits the claim for them](/api-reference/expense-claims#report-a-rendition-to-erwin). When the Approver approves it, Sintropix creates its Invoice already booked.

Mark the Source of the renditions, such as `Rindegastos`, as one that [holds Expense Renditions](/api-reference/sources#sources-that-hold-expense-renditions). Its Invoices are then listed in **Rendiciones** instead of **Compras**, and `GET /invoices/pages` tells them apart with `isExpenseRendition`.

### Load Rindegastos

Ask Erwin in a Slack channel bound to the Entity's Organization through an [Agent Channel](/api-reference/agent-channels), for example *"carga Rindegastos"*. Before the first load:

- Add a login Credential for Rindegastos to the Entity in **Integraciones**. Erwin only reads Rindegastos. He never approves, rejects, closes, or edits anything there.
- If a person's name in Rindegastos differs from the name of their Partner, note the pair on the Rindegastos page of Erwin's [wiki](/core-concepts).

The Rindegastos company must keep its accounts in the Entity's functional currency. If it doesn't, Erwin stops. Every Invoice of a load is in that currency.

Each load reads every report, because a report can close months after it was sent. Erwin loads each **approved** expense of a **closed** report that isn't in Sintropix yet. He skips rejected expenses and the expenses of reports still in process.

For each new expense, Erwin creates one Invoice and attaches its receipt:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `partnerId` | The person who reported the expense, found by RUT, then by the wiki, then by name. Erwin creates the Partner when none fits, and asks when two fit. |
| `sourceId` | The [Source](/api-reference/sources) named `Rindegastos`. Erwin creates it on the first load if the Entity has none. |
| `direction`, `documentKind` | `payable`, `invoice` |
| `documentNumber` | `RG-<expense id>`, the key that keeps a load from adding an expense twice |
| `currency`, `total` | The currency of the Rindegastos company, and the amount the person is paid back, as Rindegastos states it. An expense paid in another currency is loaded at that amount too. |
| `issueDate` | The date of the expense |
| `memo` | `Informe <folio> · <report title> · <merchant>` |
| `metadata` | In this order, when Rindegastos shows them: `Informe`, `Título informe`, `ID informe`, `ID gasto`, `Comercio`, `Categoría`, `Cuenta contable Rindegastos`, `Nota`, `Nota informe`, `Moneda original`, `Total original`, and `Tasa de cambio` for an expense paid in another currency, `Reembolsable: No`, `Fondo`, `Tipo de documento (OCR)`, `RUT comercio (OCR)`, `Folio (OCR)`, then each custom field of the expense, and each custom field of the report as `<name> (informe)`. A value longer than 500 characters is split into numbered labels, such as `Nota (1)` and `Nota (2)`. |

For an expense paid in another currency, such as a USD hotel, the Invoice holds the amount the person is paid back. The currency they paid in, the amount in that currency, and the rate Rindegastos applied are only in `Moneda original`, `Total original`, and `Tasa de cambio`, as Rindegastos shows them.

A load books nothing. An expense without a receipt, or whose receipt fails to download, is still loaded, and Erwin reports it. `Cuenta contable Rindegastos` is information only: it doesn't follow your chart of accounts, so Erwin never books to it. Erwin also ignores the **Pagado** status of Rindegastos, because Sintropix learns what's paid from the bank.

Erwin never changes or deletes an Invoice that's already in Sintropix. If Rindegastos changes an expense after it was loaded, so that it's no longer approved, its report reopens, or its currency or amount changes, Erwin leaves a `question` [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups) on the Invoice that states what changed.

He reports the Invoices he created by report with their totals, the receipts that are missing or failed, the Partners he created, and each Follow-up he left.

### Book renditions

Ask Erwin, for example *"contabiliza las rendiciones"*. He books each unbooked Invoice of the `Rindegastos` Source. He skips an Invoice that has a `question` Follow-up until a person answers it.

The Entity needs a control account for renditions, such as *Rendiciones por pagar*. If it has none, Erwin stops and asks. Each entry is dated on the Invoice's issue date, and its Control Line credits that account with the person as Partner. The debit depends on the expense's document:

| Document | Debit | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A boleta, or no document | The expense account | The expense is only in the rendition. |
| A factura issued to the company | The supplier's payable account, with the supplier as Partner | The expense is already in the supplier's factura, which comes from the SII. The person paid that factura for the company, so debiting an expense account would book it twice. |

- **Expense account.** Erwin uses the account the wiki gives for the expense's category. Otherwise, he uses the one expense account that clearly fits. When none clearly fits, he leaves the Invoice unbooked with a `question` Follow-up that offers one option per account that fits.
- **Factura.** Erwin reads the receipt to tell a factura from a boleta. A factura of a supplier with the same RUT and total, within 10 days of the expense, also confirms it. He finds the supplier by the RUT on the receipt, and creates its Partner when there is none. When the receipt shows no RUT, he leaves a `question` Follow-up that asks for the supplier. He never creates the supplier's factura from a receipt.
- **Other currency.** An Invoice of a Rindegastos load is already in the functional currency, so Erwin books its `total` as it is.
- **Not reimbursable, or a fund.** When the expense is marked `Reembolsable: No` or spends a `Fondo`, Erwin leaves it unbooked with a `question` Follow-up that asks how to book it.

The debit to a supplier's payable is an advance until the supplier's factura is booked. On each run, Erwin [matches](/api-reference/allocations#post-/api/entities/entityid/ledger/allocations/match) each open advance to the Control Line of a booked factura of the same supplier, with the same total, within 10 days of the expense. When two facturas fit, he matches neither and reports both. When the amounts differ, the smaller one settles and the rest stays open. He reports each advance still without a factura 30 days after the expense.

Erwin never changes or books again an Invoice that was already booked. He reports how many renditions he booked to an expense account and how many against a factura, the advances he matched and any rest that stayed open, the advances without a factura after 30 days, and each Follow-up he left.

## GET `/invoices`

List every Invoice for the Entity, filtered by direction and document kind. Pass `ids` instead to read the full detail of up to 100 specific Invoices in one request.

**Query parameters**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ids` | uuid[] | No | 1 to 100 Invoice ids. Repeat the parameter for each id (`?ids=A&ids=B`). Cannot be combined with any other query parameter. See [Reading several Invoices by id](#reading-several-invoices-by-id). |
| `direction` | string | No | `receivable` (AR, issued to a customer) or `payable` (AP, received from a supplier). Omit to list both. |
| `documentKind` | string | No | `invoice`, `credit_note`, or `tax_only`. Omit to list every kind. |

**Response:** an array of Invoice objects.

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Invoice id. |
| `entityId` | uuid | Owning Entity. |
| `partnerId` | uuid | Counterparty [Partner](/api-reference/partners). |
| `direction` | string | `receivable` or `payable`. |
| `documentKind` | string | `invoice`, `credit_note`, or `tax_only`. See [Document kinds](#document-kinds). |
| `documentNumber` | string | The number printed on the document. |
| `currency` | string | ISO 4217 code the document is denominated in. |
| `total` | integer | Gross total in minor units of `currency`. |
| `issueDate` | string | `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| `dueDate` | string or null | `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| `memo` | string or null | Free text. |
| `metadata` | array | Additional Information lines, each `{ "label": string, "value": string }`. Empty array when none. |
| `sourceId` | uuid or null | The [Source](/api-reference/sources) the document came from. Null when it was entered directly. |
| `bookingEntryId` | uuid or null | The Ledger Entry that books the Invoice. Null means the Invoice is not booked. |
| `controlLineId` | uuid or null | The Ledger Entry Line that carries the receivable or payable. Null while the Invoice is not booked, and always null on a `tax_only` document. |
| `controlAccountId` | uuid or null | The Ledger Account the control line sits on. Null exactly when `controlLineId` is null. |
| `followUp` | object or null | The [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups) on this Invoice, which marks its pending work. Null when there is none. |
| `excludedAt` | timestamp or null | When the Invoice was [excluded from the books](#exclude-an-invoice-from-the-books). Null when it isn't excluded. |
| `exclusionReason` | string or null | Why it was excluded. Null exactly when `excludedAt` is null. |
| `createdAt` | timestamp | Creation time. |
| `updatedAt` | timestamp | Last update time. |

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/invoices?direction=receivable" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

### Reading several Invoices by id

When you pass `ids`, the response is an array of Invoice detail objects instead of plain Invoice objects. Each element has the same shape as the response of [`GET /invoices/:invoiceId`](#get-/invoices/invoiceid), including `dte`, `attachmentCount`, and `bookingEntry`. Use this when you already hold a set of Invoice ids and need their full detail without one request per Invoice.

- Results follow the order of `ids`. A repeated id is returned once, at its first position.
- Ids the Entity does not hold are left out of the array rather than refused. Compare the returned `id` values against the ids you sent.
- Combining `ids` with `direction`, `documentKind`, or any other query parameter returns 400.
- Sintropix reads every element from one consistent snapshot.

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/invoices?ids=$INVOICE_ID_1&ids=$INVOICE_ID_2" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

## GET `/invoices/pages`

Paginated Invoice listing with richer filters. Use this instead of `GET /invoices` for UI-style pagination.

**Query parameters**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `direction` | string | No | `receivable` or `payable`. Omit to page over both. |
| `documentKind` | string | No | `invoice`, `credit_note`, or `tax_only`. Keep only documents of this [kind](#document-kinds). Omit to page over every kind. |
| `cursor` | uuid | No | The `nextCursor` returned by the previous page. Omit for the first page. |
| `limit` | integer | No | Rows per page, 1 to 100. Default `50`. |
| `statusFilter` | string | No | `all`, `notPaid`, `paid`, `unbooked`, `booked`, `excluded`, or `notExcluded`. Default `all`, which lists excluded documents too. `notExcluded` leaves them out. |
| `dteType` | string | No | Keep only documents whose DTE has this type code (`33`, `61`, ...). Repeat the parameter to allow several codes. Use `none` for documents with no DTE. |
| `sourceId` | string | No | A Source id, or `none` for documents that came from no Source. Also scopes the totals in the response. |
| `isExpenseRendition` | boolean | No | `true` keeps only [Expense Renditions](/api-reference/sources#sources-that-hold-expense-renditions), the payable documents of a Source that holds them, as **Rendiciones** lists. `false` leaves them out, as **Compras** does. Omit to return both. A `receivable` document is never an Expense Rendition. Also scopes the totals in the response. |
| `partnerId` | uuid | No | Keep only documents of this Partner. |
| `currency` | string | No | ISO 4217 code. |
| `paymentStatus` | string | No | `paid`, `partial`, or `unpaid`. |
| `standing` | string | No | `accepted`, `pending`, `claimed`, `annulment_requested`, `annulled`, or `observed` keeps documents whose DTE the SII holds in that [Standing](/api-reference/source-feeds#standing-and-tax-period). `incompatible` keeps the booked documents with an [Incompatible Standing](/api-reference/source-feeds#incompatible-standing). |
| `followUpKind` | string | No | `proposal`, `question`, `waiting`, or `none`. Filters by the kind of the document's [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups). `none` keeps documents with no Follow-up. |
| `issueDateFrom` | string | No | `YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive. |
| `issueDateTo` | string | No | `YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive. |
| `dueDateFrom` | string | No | `YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive. Documents without a due date are excluded. |
| `dueDateTo` | string | No | `YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive. Documents without a due date are excluded. |
| `taxPeriodFrom` | string | No | `YYYY-MM`, inclusive. Read from the DTE; documents without one are excluded. |
| `taxPeriodTo` | string | No | `YYYY-MM`, inclusive. |
| `totalMin` | integer | No | Minimum `total`, in minor units. |
| `totalMax` | integer | No | Maximum `total`, in minor units. |
| `groupBy` | string | No | `none`, `partner`, `currency`, `dteType`, `bookingStatus`, `paymentStatus`, `issueMonth`, `dueMonth`, or `taxPeriod`. Default `none`. |
| `search` | string | No | Case-insensitive contains-match on `documentNumber`, `partnerName`, and `memo`. A search that reads as an amount also matches `total` and the Open Amount; see [Search by amount](/api-reference/overview#search-by-amount). At most 100 characters; blank is ignored. |
| `erpThread` | string | No | `with` keeps only documents that have an [ERP Thread](/api-reference/notifications#talk-about-a-record-in-its-erp-thread), the conversation about the record in its **Thread** tab; `without` keeps only documents with none. Omit to return both. Group counts follow this filter; `totalCount`, `unbookedCount`, and `outstandingTotal` do not. |
| `sort` | string | No | `documentNumber`, `partnerName`, `currency`, `total`, `issueDate`, `dueDate`, `memo`, `dteTypeCode`, `bookingStatus`, `paymentStatus`, `openAmount`, or `taxPeriod`. Default `issueDate`. Sorting by `openAmount` places unbooked documents (null `openAmount`) before every value in both directions. |
| `sortDirection` | string | No | `asc` or `desc`. Default `desc`. |

**Response**

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `invoices` | array | The Invoice rows on this page (see below). |
| `nextCursor` | uuid or null | Pass as `cursor` to fetch the next page. Null on the last page. |
| `totalCount` | integer | Documents in scope: the direction, Source, and `isExpenseRendition` requested, ignoring every other filter. |
| `unbookedCount` | integer | How many of those are not yet booked. Excluded documents don't count. |
| `outstandingTotal` | integer | Sum of the unbooked documents' totals, in the Entity's functional currency. Excluded documents don't count. |

Each element of `invoices` is an **Invoice row**: every field of the Invoice object above, plus:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dte` | object or null | The DTE behind the document (fields listed under `PUT /invoices/:invoiceId/dte`). Null when none. |
| `partnerName` | string | Name of the Partner. |
| `partnerWebsite` | string or null | Website of the Partner, as a bare domain (`acme.cl`); null when it has none. The Partner's [logo](/api-reference/partners#get-/api/organizations/organizationid/partners/partnerid/logo) is read from it. |
| `sourceName` | string or null | Name of the Source, null when the document came from none. |
| `isExpenseRendition` | boolean | Whether the document is an [Expense Rendition](/api-reference/sources#sources-that-hold-expense-renditions): a `payable` Invoice whose Source holds Expense Renditions. Read from the Source, so marking or unmarking the Source changes it on every row at once. |
| `paymentStatus` | string or null | `paid`, `partial`, or `unpaid`. Null while the document is not booked, and always null on a `tax_only` document. |
| `paymentDate` | string or null | `YYYY-MM-DD`. For a `paid` document, the latest entry date of the entries that settled its Control Line, whatever settled it: a payment, a credit note, or a manual offset. **Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `dteTypeCode` | string | Yes | SII document type code, from `GET /sii/document-types` (`33`, `34`, `61`, ...). |

| `issuerRut` | string | Yes | Issuer RUT in canonical form, digits then hyphen then check digit (`76123456-0`). |
| `receiverRut` | string or null | Yes | Receiver RUT, same form. |
| `issuerName` | string or null | Yes | Issuer name as printed. |
| `receiverName` | string or null | Yes | Receiver name as printed. |
| `issueDate` | string | Yes | `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| `taxPeriod` | string or null | Yes | The F29 / IVA month, `YYYY-MM`. Null when not yet known; it never defaults to the issue month. |
| `currency` | string | Yes | ISO 4217 code. |
| `total` | integer | Yes | Gross total printed on the DTE, minor units, zero or more. |
| `netAmount` | integer or null | No | Net base, minor units. Omit or null when the DTE states none. |
| `exemptAmount` | integer or null | No | Exempt part of the base, minor units. |
| `taxAmount` | integer or null | No | Tax amount, minor units. The whole IVA, including any part the buyer withholds. |
| `withheldIvaAmount` | integer or null | No | The part of `taxAmount` the buyer withholds, minor units. Omit or null when the DTE withholds no IVA. |

| `metadata` | array | Yes | Additional Information lines, `{ "label": string, "value": string }`. Send `[]` for none. |

**Withheld IVA.** On a factura de compra (type `46`), the buyer withholds the IVA and pays it to the SII directly, so `total` leaves the withheld part out. State the whole IVA in `taxAmount` and the withheld part in `withheldIvaAmount`. A total withholding has `withheldIvaAmount` equal to `taxAmount`. For example, a 46 with a net of 1.000.000 CLP and a total withholding states `"netAmount": 1000000`, `"taxAmount": 190000`, `"withheldIvaAmount": 190000`, and `"total": 1000000`. A field the block leaves unstated keeps its stored value and counts as no change. Any other structural difference — `dteTypeCode`, `folio`, `issuerRut`, `receiverRut`, `issuerName`, `receiverName`, `issueDate`, `currency`, `total`, `netAmount`, `exemptAmount`, `taxAmount`, `withheldIvaAmount`, or `externalRef` — is answered `409` with code `INVOICE_BOOKED_FIELDS_LOCKED_ERROR`; unbook the Invoice first if you need to move one of them. ## POST `/invoices/:invoiceId/book`

**Body**

Each element of `newLedgerEntryLines`:

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Caller-generated line id. Must not already exist. See [Ledger Entries](/api-reference/ledger-entries#post-/api/entities/entityid/ledger/entries). |
| `accountId` | uuid | Yes | Ledger Account of the Entity. |
| `functionalAmount` | integer | Yes | Magnitude in the functional currency, minor units, zero or more. |
| `side` | string | Yes | `debit` or `credit`. |
| `partnerId` | uuid or null | No | Counterparty. Lines other than the control line default to the Invoice's Partner when omitted. |
| `transactionCurrency` | string | No | ISO 4217 code. Send together with the next two fields, or none of the three. |

| `transactionFxRate` | string | No | Decimal string such as `"945.12"`. No exponent or sign. |

| `deferral` | object | No | Spread this line's whole amount over whole months instead of recognizing it on the entry date: `{ recognitionAccountId, startMonth, monthCount }`. See [Deferrals](#deferrals). |

The Control Line (the line whose `id` is `controlLineId`) must sit on an open-item receivable account for a `receivable` Invoice or payable account for a `payable` one, name the Invoice's Partner, and match the Invoice's denomination. A regular invoice debits a receivable or credits a payable; a credit note posts the opposite side. Its account becomes the Invoice's `controlAccountId`.

A `tax_only` document has no Control Line. Its entry must still balance, and none of its lines may sit on an account whose sub-type is `payable`, `expense_rendition_payable`, or `receivable`. For example, a factura de compra 46 of CLP 190,000 IVA for a purchase abroad:

```json

    "entryDate": "2026-08-20",
    "memo": "Factura de compra 46 1234 Proveedor Extranjero",
    "newLedgerEntryLines": [
      { "id": "3b4c5d6e-7f8a-4b9c-8d0e-2f3a4b5c6d7e", "accountId": "b2c3d4e5-f6a7-4b8c-9d0e-1f2a3b4c5d6e", "functionalAmount": 190000, "side": "debit" },
      { "id": "4c5d6e7f-8a9b-4c0d-9e1f-3a4b5c6d7e8f", "accountId": "e5f6a7b8-c9d0-4e1f-8a2b-3c4d5e6f7a8b", "functionalAmount": 190000, "side": "credit" }
    ]
  }
}
```

Other lines of the entry can post to the same account as the Control Line. For example, net an advance or a retention of the same Partner in the Invoice's own entry, or split a receivable into one line per due date. Only the Control Line is the Invoice's: the payment status derives from it alone. Any other line on that account is an ordinary open item of the Partner until an [Allocation](/api-reference/allocations) settles it.

Example body for a CLP purchase invoice of 1,190,000 (1,000,000 net plus 19% VAT):

```json

  "entry": {
    "id": "9c4d5e6f-7a8b-4c9d-8e0f-1a2b3c4d5e6f",
    "entryDate": "2026-08-14",
    "memo": "Factura 45210 Servicios Cloud SpA",
    "newLedgerEntryLines": [
      { "id": "4d5e6f7a-8b9c-4d0e-8f1a-2b3c4d5e6f7a", "accountId": "a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a7b-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d", "functionalAmount": 1000000, "side": "debit" },
```

### Detecting concurrent changes with `expectedBookingVersion`

Send `expectedBookingVersion` when the booking decision is derived from state you read earlier and any change to the Invoice or its DTE between the read and the write should abort the booking. Typical cases: a scheduled workflow that batches unbooked Invoices, an integration that classifies an Invoice from its stored DTE amounts, or a UI that stages a booking for review.

1. `GET /invoices/:invoiceId` and remember `bookingVersion`.
2. Build the entry from the values you just read.
3. `POST /invoices/:invoiceId/book` (or `POST /invoices/book`) with `expectedBookingVersion` set to that token.
4. On `409 INVOICE_BOOKING_CHANGED_ERROR`, re-read the Invoice and decide whether to rebuild the entry.

The check runs after the server acquires the Invoice lock, so a booking that races a concurrent DTE edit either sees the edit and refuses, or wins the lock and books the pre-edit state without either write being lost. Omit the field when no earlier read exists and any current state is acceptable.

## Deferrals

Some invoices cover several months: an annual software subscription paid up front, or a four-month service billed on one sales invoice. A **Deferral** spreads one line of the booking over whole months, so the result is recognized month by month instead of all in the invoice month.

- On a **payable** Invoice, the deferred line debits a **prepaid expense** account (an `asset` with sub-type `prepaid_expense`).
- On a **receivable** Invoice, the deferred line credits a **deferred revenue** account (a `liability` with sub-type `deferred_revenue`).
- Each month, a **Release** moves that month's share from the deferral account to the **Recognition Account**: the expense or income account the line would have posted to had it not been deferred.

Create the two accounts in the [Chart of Accounts](/api-reference/chart-of-accounts#prepaid-expenses-and-deferred-revenue) before you defer a line. In the app, turn on **Diferir** on a line when you classify the Invoice.

### Defer a line when you book

You defer at booking time only. Add `deferral` to the line in `entry.newLedgerEntryLines` on [`POST /invoices/:invoiceId/book`](#post-/invoices/invoiceid/book). The same field works in `POST /invoices/book` and in `bookingEntryWithLines` on `POST /invoices`. There is no separate endpoint to defer an Invoice that is already booked; unbook it and book it again with the terms.

Fields of `deferral`:

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `recognitionAccountId` | uuid | Yes | The account each Release lands in. For a payable Invoice: `cost_of_goods_sold`, `operating_expense`, or `other_expense`. For a receivable Invoice: `income` or `other_income`. |
| `startMonth` | string | Yes | First month of service, `YYYY-MM`. |
| `monthCount` | integer | Yes | Number of months, 1 to 600. The last Release must fall in December 9999 at the latest. |

These rules apply:

- The whole line is deferred. You can't defer part of a line.
- The line's account must have the sub-type of the Invoice's direction, on the matching side: a `debit` on `prepaid_expense` for a payable Invoice, a `credit` on `deferred_revenue` for a receivable one.
- Credit notes and `tax_only` documents can't be deferred.
- Only the functional amount is deferred. A foreign-currency line is released at the amount booked on the Invoice, and the Releases carry no transaction currency.
- Defer the net line only. The IVA line and the control line follow the Invoice as usual.
- Several lines of the same booking can each carry their own `deferral`.

### The Release schedule

Booking a deferred line creates one Release per month:

- **Straight-line amounts.** Each Release carries an equal share of the line's functional amount. The last month absorbs the rounding.
- **Month-end dates.** Each Release is dated the last day of its month.
- **Past and future months.** A Release dated today or earlier is booked right away with status `active`. A later one is a [Scheduled Entry](/api-reference/ledger-entries#scheduled-entries) with status `scheduled`, and Sintropix books it on its date.
- **Closed months.** If a Release would fall on or before the ledger lock date, the whole booking is refused with `LEDGER_ENTRY_DATE_LOCKED_ERROR`.
- **Partner and Dimensions.** Both lines of each Release carry the Invoice's Partner. The deferred line's `dimensionValueIds` go on the recognition line of each Release, not on the deferral account line of the booking.

Each Release has two lines: the Recognition Account and the deferral account. For a prepaid expense the Release debits the Recognition Account and credits the prepaid account. For deferred revenue it debits the deferred revenue account and credits the Recognition Account.

A Release keeps the plan it was created with. Changing its date, through `PATCH /ledger/entries/:entryId` or by rescheduling it, is refused with `DEFERRAL_RELEASE_LOCKED_ERROR`. Moving one of its lines to another account is refused with `LEDGER_ENTRY_LINE_ACCOUNT_PINNED_ERROR`.

Example: a bill of CLP 1,428,000 (1,200,000 net plus 19% VAT) for a 12-month software subscription starting in July 2026. The net line debits the prepaid expense account and releases 100,000 a month into the software expense account.

```json
{
  "controlLineId": "0e1f2a3b-4c5d-4e6f-8a7b-8c9d0e1f2a3b",
  "entry": {
    "id": "7b8c9d0e-1f2a-4b3c-8d4e-5f6a7b8c9d0e",
    "entryDate": "2026-07-01",
    "memo": "Factura 88120 Software Anual SpA",
    "newLedgerEntryLines": [
      {
        "id": "8c9d0e1f-2a3b-4c4d-8e5f-6a7b8c9d0e1f",
        "accountId": "c3d4e5f6-a7b8-4c9d-8e0f-1a2b3c4d5e6f",
        "functionalAmount": 1200000,
        "side": "debit",
        "deferral": {
          "recognitionAccountId": "d4e5f6a7-b8c9-4d0e-8f1a-2b3c4d5e6f7a",
          "startMonth": "2026-07",
          "monthCount": 12
        }
      },
      { "id": "9d0e1f2a-3b4c-4d5e-8f6a-7b8c9d0e1f2a", "accountId": "b2c3d4e5-f6a7-4b8c-9d0e-1f2a3b4c5d6e", "functionalAmount": 228000, "side": "debit" },
      { "id": "0e1f2a3b-4c5d-4e6f-8a7b-8c9d0e1f2a3b", "accountId": "3e7a9c1b-2d4f-4a6e-8b0c-1d2e3f4a5b6c", "functionalAmount": 1428000, "side": "credit", "partnerId": "0b6c0f2e-5a4d-4e8f-b1c3-7d9e2a4f6c88" }
```

The booking response doesn't list the Releases. Read them from `deferrals` on [`GET /invoices/:invoiceId`](#get-/invoices/invoiceid).

### Reading a Deferral

Each element of `deferrals` on the Invoice detail:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Deferral id. |
| `deferredLedgerEntryLineId` | uuid | The booking line that was deferred. |
| `deferredAccount` | object | The prepaid expense or deferred revenue account: `{ id, code, name }`. |
| `recognitionAccount` | object | The Recognition Account: `{ id, code, name }`. |
| `startMonth` | string | First month, `YYYY-MM`. |
| `monthCount` | integer | Number of months. |
| `releases` | array | The Releases, ordered by date (below). |
| `recognizedAmount` | integer | Sum of the `active` Releases, in functional minor units. |
| `pendingAmount` | integer | The deferred line's amount minus `recognizedAmount`. |

Each element of `releases`:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ledgerEntryId` | uuid | The Release's Ledger Entry. |
| `entryDate` | string | `YYYY-MM-DD`, the last day of its month. |
| `amount` | integer | Functional amount, minor units. |
| `status` | string | `active` (booked), `scheduled` (pending), `cancelled`, `deleted` (unbooked), or `reversed`. |

A Deferral has no status of its own. Read its state from its Releases.

### Undoing a Deferral

A Deferral follows its booking:

- **Unbook** the booking entry ([`DELETE /ledger/entries/:entryId`](/api-reference/ledger-entries#delete-/api/entities/entityid/ledger/entries/entryid)): the `active` Releases are unbooked and the `scheduled` ones are cancelled.
- **Reverse** the booking entry ([`POST /ledger/entries/:entryId/reverse`](/api-reference/ledger-entries#post-/api/entities/entityid/ledger/entries/entryid/reverse)): the `active` Releases are reversed on the same reversal date and the `scheduled` ones are cancelled.
- If an `active` Release sits on or before the ledger lock date, the whole Unbook is refused with `LEDGER_ENTRY_UNBOOK_FROZEN_ERROR` and the whole Reverse with `LEDGER_ENTRY_FROZEN_ERROR`.

In the app, the Unbook and Reverse dialogs on the Invoice and on the entry page list these effects before you confirm.

To stop a Deferral but keep what's already recognized, cancel its pending Releases.

### POST `/deferrals/:deferralId/cancel`

Cancel every `scheduled` Release of the Deferral. Releases already booked stay as they are. In the app, this is **Cancelar lo pendiente** on the Invoice. The call has no body.

**Response** (`200`): the Deferral, in the shape of an element of `deferrals`, with the cancelled Releases now `cancelled`.

**Domain errors**

| Code | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DEFERRAL_NOT_FOUND_ERROR` | `404` | The Deferral does not exist under this Entity. The response carries `params: { deferralId }`. |

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/deferrals/$DEFERRAL_ID/cancel" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent"
```

## Exclude an invoice from the books

Some documents land but should never be booked: for example, an annulled boleta or a factura you claimed at the SII. Deleting one doesn't help, because the next [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds) sync lands it again. Exclude it instead. An **Exclusion** records, with a reason, that the Invoice will never be booked. It takes the Invoice out of the pending work, and you can lift it at any time.

An excluded Invoice:

- Is still listed by `statusFilter=all`, the default, and the app crosses those rows out. It's left out of `statusFilter=notExcluded` and `statusFilter=unbooked`, of `unbookedCount` and `outstandingTotal`, of the home dashboard's `salesToBook`, `purchasesToBook`, and `expenseRenditionsToBook`, and of match candidates.
- Can't be booked, single or bulk, and can't hold a [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups). Excluding the Invoice clears its Follow-up.
- Owes nothing to plan in a [Payment Batch](/api-reference/payment-batches).
- Stays landed. A sync skips it, but still writes the Standing and Tax Period of its DTE.

A booked Invoice can't be excluded: unbook it first. Anyone who can book an Invoice can exclude or include it. A `viewer` is refused with `403`. Each Exclusion and Inclusion appears in the Invoice's [audit trail](/api-reference/audit-trail).

### PUT `/invoices/:invoiceId/exclusion`

Exclude an unbooked Invoice from the books.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `reason` | string | Yes | Why the Invoice will never be booked. Trimmed, non-empty, up to 1,000 characters. |

**Response:** `{ "invoice": <Invoice row>, "totalCount": integer, "unbookedCount": integer, "outstandingTotal": integer }`, with `excludedAt` and `exclusionReason` set.

```bash
curl -X PUT "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/invoices/$INVOICE_ID/exclusion" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "reason": "Boleta anulada en el SII." }'
```

### DELETE `/invoices/:invoiceId/exclusion`

Include an excluded Invoice in the books again. It goes back to `unbooked`.

**Response:** `{ "invoice": <Invoice row>, "totalCount": integer, "unbookedCount": integer, "outstandingTotal": integer }`, with `excludedAt` and `exclusionReason` null.

### Exclusion errors

| Code | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `INVOICE_ALREADY_BOOKED_ERROR` | `409` | The Invoice is booked. Unbook it before you exclude it. |
| `INVOICE_EXCLUDED_ERROR` | `409` | The Invoice is already excluded. |
| `INVOICE_NOT_EXCLUDED_ERROR` | `409` | The Invoice isn't excluded, so there's nothing to include. |

A blank `reason`, or one longer than 1,000 characters, is refused with `400`.
