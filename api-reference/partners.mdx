A Partner is any counterparty on your books: a customer, a supplier, an employee, or a tax authority. Partners are referenced from Invoices, Ledger Lines with Open Items, and Bank Movement matches. This page covers Partner CRUD, the paginated Partner listing, [the Organization's Partners](#organization-partners), [Aliases](#aliases), [merging duplicate Partners](#merge-partners), [Bank Details](#bank-details), and Booking Templates.

Amounts are integers in the minor units of the Entity's functional currency. Timestamps are ISO 8601.

<Note>
  **Entities with Books.** When the Entity has at least one [Book](/api-reference/books), `GET /partners/pages` requires a `bookId` query parameter — the `openReceivable` and `openPayable` totals on the page rows are then computed inside that Book. `GET /partners` and the Partner rows returned by create, update, and delete stay outside the rule and surface open totals across every Book.
</Note>

## Partners belong to the Organization

A Partner belongs to the [Organization](/api-reference/organizations), not to one Entity. One real-world party is one Partner, whichever Entities of the Organization deal with it. An Entity starts **using** a Partner when something names it in that Entity: a create, an Invoice, a Ledger Line, a Bank Movement, a Payment Order, or a sync. You can also [use an existing Partner](#put-partners-partnerid) explicitly.

The routes under `/api/entities/:entityId/partners` serve the Partners that Entity uses. A Partner the Entity doesn't use answers `404`. What a change reaches depends on the field:

| Shared by every Entity of the Organization | Kept by this Entity alone |
| --- | --- |
| `name`, `legalName`, `country`, `taxId`, `website`, [Aliases](#aliases), and [Bank Details](#bank-details) | `defaultPurchaseAccountId`, `booksAutomatically`, and the [Booking Template](#booking-templates) |

For example, when one Entity corrects a supplier's legal name, every other Entity sees the new name. When it sets a Default Purchase Account for that supplier, the other Entities keep their own.

### Roles

A Partner has no type. Whether an Entity buys from a Partner or sells to it is read from that Entity's books, so the same Partner can be a supplier of one Entity and a customer of another. The `roles` field of a Partner row lists what this Entity's books say:

| Role | When the Partner has it |
| --- | --- |
| `vendor` | The Entity holds a payable Invoice from the Partner, or keeps a Default Purchase Account, a Booking Template, or `booksAutomatically` for it. |
| `customer` | The Entity holds a receivable Invoice to the Partner. |

A Partner can have both roles, or none. In the app, **Contactos** filters by **Relación** (**Proveedor** or **Cliente**).

### Representing Partners

Each Entity is represented by one Partner of its Organization. That Partner carries the Entity's tax identity (`country` and `taxId`), and its `representsEntityId` is the Entity's id. Sintropix creates it with the Entity. The other Entities of the Organization use it to name that Entity as their counterparty, for example for what they owe each other.

- An Entity never uses the Partner that represents it. A write that names it in that Entity is refused with `422` and `PARTNER_REPRESENTS_ENTITY_ERROR`, with `params.partnerId` and `params.entityId`. This applies to creating or using the Partner, and to naming it on a Ledger Line, an Invoice, a Bank Movement, or a Payment Order.
- A bank feed lands a Movement whose counterparty is the Entity itself with no Partner. A [Source Feed](/api-reference/source-feeds) document addressed to the Entity itself is rejected alone, with `PARTNER_REPRESENTS_ENTITY_ERROR`, and the rest of the sync lands.
- Its `country` and `taxId` are the Entity's. Correct them on the [Entity](/api-reference/entities#patch-api-entities-entityid). Changing them on the Partner is refused with `422` and `PARTNER_REPRESENTS_ENTITY_TAX_IDENTITY_ERROR`.

## GET `/partners`

List every Partner the Entity uses, each row enriched with this Entity's Open Item totals (unsettled AR and AP balances). The list includes Merged Partners. To keep only active Partners, drop rows where `mergedIntoPartnerId` is not null.

**Response:** an array of Partner objects.

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Partner id. |
| `organizationId` | uuid | Organization that owns the Partner. |
| `representsEntityId` | uuid or null | The Entity this Partner [represents](#representing-partners). Null for an ordinary Partner. |
| `entityId` | uuid | The Entity of the route. `defaultPurchaseAccountId`, `booksAutomatically`, and the open totals are this Entity's. |
| `name` | string | Display name. |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountId` | uuid or null | Ledger Account this Entity proposes when booking the Partner's purchase invoices. |
| `booksAutomatically` | boolean | Whether Sintropix books the Partner's new purchase invoices in this Entity from its [Booking Template](#booking-templates). Always `false` for a Partner without a template in this Entity. |
| `legalName` | string or null | Registered legal name. |
| `country` | string or null | ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code of the tax registration. |
| `taxId` | string or null | Tax registration number. Chilean RUTs are stored as digits, hyphen, check digit (`76123456-0`). |
| `website` | string or null | The Partner's website, stored as a bare lowercase domain (`acme.cl`). Sintropix reads the Partner's [logo](#get-partners-partnerid-logo) from it. |
| `aliases` | string[] | Other names that find this Partner, in name order. See [Aliases](#aliases). |
| `mergedIntoPartnerId` | uuid or null | The Partner this one was [merged](#merge-partners) into. Null for an active Partner. |
| `openReceivable` | integer | Unsettled amount this Partner owes the Entity. |
| `openPayable` | integer | Unsettled amount the Entity owes this Partner. |
| `createdAt` | timestamp | Creation time. |
| `updatedAt` | timestamp | Last update time. |

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

## GET `/partners/pages`

Paginated listing of the Partners the Entity uses, for UI use. Merged Partners are never listed.

**Query parameters.** Unknown parameters are rejected with `400`.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cursor` | uuid | No | The `nextCursor` of the previous page. Omit for the first page. |
| `limit` | integer | No | Rows per page, 1 to 100. Default `50`. |
| `search` | string | No | Case-insensitive contains-match on `name`, `legalName`, `taxId`, and the default purchase account's code and name. Also matches [Aliases](#aliases), ignoring case, accents, punctuation, and spacing. A search that reads as an amount also matches `openReceivable` and `openPayable`; see [Search by amount](/api-reference/overview#search-by-amount). At most 100 characters. |
| `role` | string | No | `vendor` or `customer`. Keeps the Partners that have this [role](#roles) in the Entity. |
| `country` | string | No | ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code. |
| `openReceivableMin` | integer | No | Inclusive lower bound on `openReceivable`. |
| `openReceivableMax` | integer | No | Inclusive upper bound; must not be below the minimum. |
| `openPayableMin` | integer | No | Inclusive lower bound on `openPayable`. |
| `openPayableMax` | integer | No | Inclusive upper bound; must not be below the minimum. |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountFilter` | string | No | `all`, `assigned`, or `missing`. Default `all`. Requires `role=vendor` unless `all`. |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountId` | uuid | No | Keep only vendors with this default purchase account. Requires `role=vendor`. |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountClassification` | string | No | `expense`, `fixed_asset`, or `missing`. Requires `role=vendor`. |
| `groupBy` | string | No | `none`, `country`, `defaultPurchaseAccount`, or `defaultPurchaseAccountClassification`. Default `none`. The last two require `role=vendor`. |
| `sort` | string | No | `name`, `legalName`, `taxId`, `country`, `openReceivable`, `openPayable`, or `defaultPurchaseAccount`. Default `name`. |
| `sortDirection` | string | No | `asc` or `desc`. Default `asc`. |
| `bookId` | uuid | Conditional | Required when the Entity has at least one Book; must be omitted otherwise. |

**Response**

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `partners` | array | The Partner rows on this page (see below). |
| `nextCursor` | uuid or null | Pass as `cursor` to fetch the next page. Null on the last page. |
| `totalCount` | integer | Partners matching the filters, across all pages. |
| `withoutDefaultPurchaseAccountCount` | integer | Of those, how many have no default purchase account. |

Each element of `partners` is a **Partner row**: every field of the Partner object above, plus:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `roles` | string[] | The Partner's [roles](#roles) in this Entity: `vendor`, `customer`, both, or none. |
| `countryName` | string or null | Name of `country`. |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountCode` | string or null | Code of the default purchase account. |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountName` | string or null | Name of the default purchase account. |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountClassification` | string | `expense`, `fixed_asset`, or `missing`. |
| `bankDetails` | object or null | Where the Partner receives a transfer. See [Bank Details](#bank-details). Null when none are set. |
| `group` | object or null | Null unless `groupBy` was requested. Contains `by`, `value`, `key` (string), `partnerCount` (integer), `openReceivable` (integer), `openPayable` (integer), and `startsGroup` (boolean, true on the first row of each group). |

The type of `group.value` depends on `group.by`:

| `by` | `value` |
| --- | --- |
| `country` | string or null |
| `defaultPurchaseAccount` | string or null |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountClassification` | `expense`, `fixed_asset`, or `missing` |

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners/pages?role=vendor&defaultPurchaseAccountFilter=missing&limit=25" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

## POST `/partners`

Create a Partner in the Organization and start using it in this Entity.

**Body.** Unknown keys are rejected with `400`.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Caller-generated id. Retrying with the same id is answered `409`, so a retried request never creates a second Partner. |
| `name` | string | Yes | Non-empty display name, at most 200 characters. |
| `legalName` | string or null | No | Non-empty if sent, at most 200 characters. |
| `country` | string or null | No | ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code known to Sintropix. |
| `taxId` | string or null | No | Tax registration number, at most 50 characters. When `country` is `CL` the value must be a valid RUT; separators and dots are accepted and it is stored in canonical form (`76.123.456-0` becomes `76123456-0`). |
| `website` | string or null | No | A domain or a URL, at most 2048 characters. Sintropix keeps only the host, lowercased and without `www.`: `https://www.Acme.cl/contacto` is stored as `acme.cl`. A value that is not a domain is refused with `400`, with the validation issue `WEBSITE_INVALID_ERROR`. |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountId` | uuid or null | No | The account this Entity proposes for the Partner's purchase invoices. It must be an expense-type Ledger Account or a fixed-asset account. Setting it gives the Partner the `vendor` role here. |

A `country` and `taxId` pair belongs to one Partner of the Organization:

- When another Partner of the Organization already holds it, the create is refused with `409` and `PARTNER_TAX_IDENTITY_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`. `params.partnerId` names that Partner. [Use it](#put-partners-partnerid) in this Entity instead. In the app, the refusal offers **Usar** with the existing Partner's name.
- The Entity's own tax identity belongs to its [representing Partner](#representing-partners), so it is refused with `422` and `PARTNER_REPRESENTS_ENTITY_ERROR`.

**Response** (`201`): the created Partner row, as `GET /partners/pages` would list it.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "0b6c0f2e-5a4d-4e8f-b1c3-7d9e2a4f6c88",
    "name": "Servicios Cloud SpA",
    "legalName": "Servicios Cloud SpA",
    "country": "CL",
    "taxId": "76123456-0",
    "defaultPurchaseAccountId": "a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a7b-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d"
  }'
```

## PUT `/partners/:partnerId`

Use a Partner of the Organization in this Entity. Call it when a create is refused with `PARTNER_TAX_IDENTITY_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`, or to pick a Partner another Entity already uses. Using a Partner the Entity already uses changes nothing, so you can retry safely. No request body.

**Response:** the Partner row, as `GET /partners/pages` lists it.

| Code | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PARTNER_NOT_FOUND_ERROR` | `404` | No Partner with this id belongs to the Entity's Organization. |
| `PARTNER_MERGED_ERROR` | `409` | The Partner is a [Merged Partner](#after-a-merge). Use the Partner in `params.mergedIntoPartnerId` instead. |
| `PARTNER_REPRESENTS_ENTITY_ERROR` | `422` | The Partner [represents](#representing-partners) this Entity. |

```bash
curl -X PUT "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners/$PARTNER_ID" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent"
```

## PATCH `/partners/:partnerId`

Update Partner attributes. A change to `name`, `legalName`, `country`, `taxId`, `website`, or `aliases` reaches every Entity of the Organization. A change to `defaultPurchaseAccountId` or `booksAutomatically` reaches this Entity only.

**Body:** any subset of the fields below. At least one is required; unknown keys are rejected with `400`. Send `null` to clear a nullable field.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | string | No | Non-empty, at most 200 characters. |
| `legalName` | string or null | No | Non-empty if sent, at most 200 characters. |
| `country` | string or null | No | ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code. |
| `taxId` | string or null | No | Same rules as on create, including the RUT rule when the resulting country is `CL`. |
| `website` | string or null | No | Same rule as on create. |
| `defaultPurchaseAccountId` | uuid or null | No | Expense-type or fixed-asset account. |
| `booksAutomatically` | boolean | No | Turn automatic booking from the [Booking Template](#booking-templates) on or off in this Entity. `true` requires a saved template (`409`). Not accepted on create: a new Partner starts with `false`. |
| `aliases` | string[] | No | The full list of [Aliases](#aliases). Each Alias is at most 200 characters. An existing Alias you leave out is removed. Send `[]` to remove all. Not accepted on create. |

A Merged Partner cannot be updated (`409`, `PARTNER_MERGED_ERROR`). A tax identity another Partner of the Organization holds is refused as on create. On a [representing Partner](#representing-partners), `country` and `taxId` can't change (`422`, `PARTNER_REPRESENTS_ENTITY_TAX_IDENTITY_ERROR`).

**Response:** `{ "partner": <Partner row>, "previousPartner": <Partner row> }`, the row after and before the update.

```bash
curl -X PATCH "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners/$PARTNER_ID" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "legalName": "Servicios Cloud Limitada", "defaultPurchaseAccountId": null }'
```

## DELETE `/partners/:partnerId`

Stop using the Partner in this Entity. Sintropix also deletes the Partner itself when no other Entity uses it and it represents no Entity. Otherwise the Partner stays for the Entities that use it.

A Partner this Entity's records still reference, such as Ledger Lines with Open Items, is refused (`409`, `PARTNER_IN_USE_ERROR`). A Merged Partner is never deleted (`409`, `PARTNER_MERGED_ERROR`).

**Response:** the deleted Partner row, as `GET /partners/pages` listed it.

## GET `/partners/:partnerId/logo`

Read the Partner's logo: a 64 × 64 PNG that Sintropix takes from the icon of the Partner's `website`. The app shows it in place of the Partner's initial.

**Response**

- `200` with the PNG itself (`Content-Type: image/png`). The response carries `Cache-Control: private, max-age=31536000, immutable`, so a browser keeps it for up to a year. The URL stays the same when the `website` changes, so a cached logo can outlive it. Add the website to the URL, for example `?website=acme.cl`, to fetch the new logo after a change.
- `404` with `PARTNER_LOGO_NOT_FOUND_ERROR` when the Partner has no `website`, or its site shows no usable icon. A `404` carries no cache header. A site that could not be reached is tried again on the next request; a site that answered with no usable icon is not.

```bash
curl -i "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners/$PARTNER_ID/logo" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

## Organization Partners

### GET `/api/organizations/:organizationId/partners`

List the Organization's Partners in name order, each with the Entities that use it. Use it to find a Partner another Entity already uses before you create one. Merged Partners are never listed.

This route lives under the Organization, not under an Entity. It needs the `books` read permission in the Organization. An API key confined to one Entity can call it for that Entity's Organization; any other Organization answers `404`.

**Query parameters.** Unknown parameters are rejected with `400`.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cursor` | uuid | No | The `nextCursor` of the previous page. Omit for the first page. |
| `limit` | integer | No | Rows per page, 1 to 100. Default `50`. |
| `search` | string | No | Case-insensitive contains-match on `name`, `legalName`, and `taxId`. Also matches [Aliases](#aliases), ignoring case, accents, punctuation, and spacing. At most 100 characters. |

**Response**

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `partners` | array | The Partners on this page (see below). |
| `nextCursor` | uuid or null | Pass as `cursor` to fetch the next page. Null on the last page. |
| `totalCount` | integer | Partners matching the search, across all pages. |

Each element of `partners` has the Partner's shared fields: `id`, `organizationId`, `representsEntityId`, `name`, `legalName`, `country`, `taxId`, `website`, `aliases`, `mergedIntoPartnerId`, `createdAt`, and `updatedAt`. It also has `entities`, the `{ id, name }` of each Entity that uses the Partner, in name order. A representing Partner never lists the Entity it represents. A row carries no open totals and no per-Entity settings: read those from the Entity routes.

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/partners?search=google" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

## Aliases

An Alias is another name that finds a Partner. Use Aliases when one counterparty appears under several spellings, such as `AMAZON WEB SERVICES` and `Amazon Web Services, Inc.`.

- Two names count as the same name when they differ only in case, accents, punctuation, or spacing.
- Each name belongs to at most one Partner per Organization. An Alias that matches another Partner's display name or Alias is refused with `409` (`PARTNER_ALIAS_TAKEN_ERROR`). The error `params` hold the `alias`, plus the `partnerId` and `partnerName` of the Partner that already has it.
- An Alias needs at least one letter or digit (`400`).
- Partner search on `GET /partners/pages` and in the app's Partner pickers matches Aliases.
- An Alias never sets a Partner on a Bank Movement.

Aliases also decide where Source Feed documents from foreign counterparties land. The SII gives every counterparty outside Chile the RUT `55555555-5`, so Sintropix finds a foreign Partner by the name the provider states. It first looks for the Partner that holds that name as an Alias. It then looks for a Partner whose display name is the same name, and takes the oldest if several match. It never picks a Merged Partner. Add a spelling as an Alias to route future documents with that spelling to the right Partner.

Set Aliases with the `aliases` field of `PATCH /partners/:partnerId`. The list replaces the current Aliases:

```bash
curl -X PATCH "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners/$PARTNER_ID" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "aliases": ["AMAZON WEB SERVICES", "AWS EMEA SARL"] }'
```

## Merge Partners

Merge a duplicate Partner into the Partner you want to keep. Both belong to the same Organization, and the merge runs in every Entity that uses the duplicate. The duplicate becomes a **Merged Partner**, and its name and Aliases become Aliases of the kept Partner. In the app, use the **Fusionar** row action on the Partners page.

<Warning>
  A merge cannot be undone.
</Warning>

### POST `/api/organizations/:organizationId/partners/:partnerId/merge`

Merge the Partner in the path into `targetPartnerId`, the Partner that stays. The merge runs all-or-nothing across every Entity, and the [Audit Trail](/api-reference/audit-trail) records every row it changes. There is no merge route under `/api/entities/:entityId`.

The route needs the `books` write permission in the Organization. An API key confined to one Entity can merge only a Partner that no other Entity uses, because a merge moves the books of every Entity that uses it. Otherwise it gets `403` with `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`.

**Body.** Unknown keys are rejected with `400`.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `targetPartnerId` | uuid | Yes | The Partner that stays. Must belong to the same Organization. |

**Response** (`200`): `{ "partner": <Organization Partner row>, "mergedPartner": <Organization Partner row> }`, in the shape [`GET /api/organizations/:organizationId/partners`](#get-api-organizations-organizationid-partners) lists. `partner` is the kept Partner after the merge. `mergedPartner` is the merged one, with `mergedIntoPartnerId` set. The moved lines, Invoices, and Bank Movements are not included; read them back through their own endpoints.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/organizations/$ORGANIZATION_ID/partners/$DUPLICATE_PARTNER_ID/merge" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "targetPartnerId": "0b6c0f2e-5a4d-4e8f-b1c3-7d9e2a4f6c88" }'
```

### What a merge moves

Every Entity that used the merged Partner now uses the kept Partner. In each of those Entities, Sintropix moves these records from the merged Partner to the kept Partner:

- Every Ledger Line in an Entry dated after that Entity's Lock Date, unless the Entry is deleted or cancelled.
- Every Invoice that is unbooked or whose Control Line moves.
- Every Bank Movement that is unbooked or whose bank line moves.

Lines on or before an Entity's Lock Date stay on the merged Partner, because the Lock Date freezes them. The Partner Ledger shows that history under the merged Partner's name.

The kept Partner takes these shared fields from the merged Partner only where its own value is empty: the tax identity (`country` and `taxId`), `legalName`, and `website`. When the merged Partner [represents](#representing-partners) an Entity, the kept Partner takes that role with the tax identity.

In each Entity, the kept Partner takes the merged Partner's `defaultPurchaseAccountId` when it has none there. It takes the merged Partner's Booking Template and `booksAutomatically` only when it has no template in that Entity. Otherwise, Sintropix removes the merged Partner's template.

The kept Partner also takes every [Payment Order](/api-reference/payment-batches) and [Expense Claim](/api-reference/expense-claims) of the merged Partner, and its [Bank Details](#bank-details) when the kept Partner has none.

The merge also clears every [Follow-up](/api-reference/follow-ups) that names the merged Partner. A Partner merged earlier into the merged Partner now points at the kept Partner.

### After a merge

A Merged Partner:

- Does not appear in `GET /partners/pages`, Partner search, or pickers. `GET /partners` still returns it.
- Is never matched by a Source Feed.
- Cannot be edited, deleted, merged, used in another Entity, or given a Booking Template (`409`, `PARTNER_MERGED_ERROR`).
- Cannot be named on a new or updated Ledger Line, Invoice, or Bank Movement. Those writes return `409` with `LEDGER_ENTRY_LINE_PARTNER_MERGED_ERROR`, `INVOICE_PARTNER_MERGED_ERROR`, or `BANK_MOVEMENT_PARTNER_MERGED_ERROR`. Use the Partner in `mergedIntoPartnerId` instead.

### When a merge is refused

| Code | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PARTNER_MERGE_INTO_ITSELF_ERROR` | `409` | `targetPartnerId` is the Partner in the path. |
| `PARTNER_MERGED_ERROR` | `409` | Either Partner is already a Merged Partner. `params.mergedIntoPartnerId` names the Partner it was merged into. |
| `PARTNER_MERGE_TAX_IDENTITY_CONFLICT_ERROR` | `409` | Both Partners have a tax identity and the two differ, so they are two legal persons. |
| `PARTNER_MERGE_LOCKED_OPEN_LINES_ERROR` | `409` | The merged Partner has lines with an Open Amount on or before the Lock Date of an Entity that uses it. `params.entityId` and `params.entityName` name that Entity, and `params.ledgerLockDate` is its Lock Date. `params.lines` lists each line with its Entry and, for a Control Line, its Invoice. Settle them in that Entity first. |
| `PARTNER_ALIAS_TAKEN_ERROR` | `409` | A name of the merged Partner is already an Alias of a third Partner. |
| `PARTNER_REPRESENTS_ENTITY_ERROR` | `422` | After the merge, the kept Partner would represent an Entity that uses either Partner. |
| `PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR` | `403` | The API key is confined to one Entity, and another Entity uses the merged Partner. |

A merge is also refused when an Invoice without a DTE would collide with an Invoice of the kept Partner that has the same direction, kind, and number.

## Bank Details

Bank Details state where a Partner receives a bank transfer. A [Payment Batch](/api-reference/payment-batches) pays each Partner to these details, and can't be marked Ready while a payee has none. A Partner holds at most one set. The name and RUT the transfer carries are the Partner's own `name` and `taxId`.

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bankCode` | string | CMF code of the bank, from [`GET /banks`](/api-reference/reference-data#get-banks). This can also be a savings cooperative or a prepaid card issuer, such as Mercado Pago. |
| `bankName` | string | Name of that bank. |
| `accountKind` | string | `checking` (cuenta corriente), `sight` (cuenta vista), or `savings` (cuenta de ahorro). |
| `accountNumber` | string | The account number at that bank. |
| `notificationEmail` | string or null | Email address the bank notifies of the transfer. |

Bank Details are shared by every Entity that uses the Partner. They appear as `bankDetails` on every Partner row. When the Partner itself is deleted, they go too.

### PUT `/partners/:partnerId/bank-details`

Set the Partner's Bank Details. The body states the whole set and replaces any details the Partner already has.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bankCode` | string | Yes | CMF code of a bank from `GET /banks`. An unknown code is refused with `400` and `PARTNER_BANK_DETAILS_BANK_UNKNOWN_ERROR`. |
| `accountKind` | string | Yes | `checking`, `sight`, or `savings`. |
| `accountNumber` | string | Yes | Trimmed, non-empty. |
| `notificationEmail` | string or null | No | A valid email address. Omit or send `null` for none. |

**Response:** the Partner row, as `GET /partners/pages` lists it, with its `bankDetails`.

```bash
curl -X PUT "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners/$PARTNER_ID/bank-details" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "bankCode": "016",
    "accountKind": "checking",
    "accountNumber": "12345678",
    "notificationEmail": "pagos@proveedor.cl"
  }'
```

### DELETE `/partners/:partnerId/bank-details`

Remove the Partner's Bank Details. A Partner without them is refused with `404` and `PARTNER_BANK_DETAILS_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`.

**Response:** the Partner row, as `GET /partners/pages` lists it, with `bankDetails: null`.

## Booking Templates

A Booking Template tells Sintropix how one Entity books a Partner's purchase invoices. It is an ordered list of lines. Each line takes one amount from the Invoice or its DTE and posts it to a Ledger Account on a side. With the Partner's `booksAutomatically` switch on in that Entity, Sintropix books each new purchase invoice of that Partner in that Entity when it is created, but only when the Invoice fits the template exactly.

Use a template for a vendor whose invoices you always book with the same entry shape, such as a hosting provider billed to one expense account plus IVA. Invoices that do not fit land unbooked, and you book them by hand with [`POST /invoices/:invoiceId/book`](/api-reference/invoices).

Each Entity keeps its own template and switch for a Partner, so a template saved in one Entity never books another Entity's invoices. Saving a template gives the Partner the `vendor` [role](#roles) in that Entity. The Partner's default purchase account is unaffected.

### Template lines

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `amountSource` | string | Where the line's amount comes from: `invoice_total` (the Invoice `total`), `dte_net_amount`, `dte_exempt_amount`, `dte_tax_amount`, or `dte_withheld_iva_amount` (the DTE's `netAmount`, `exemptAmount`, `taxAmount`, or `withheldIvaAmount`, where `taxAmount` is the whole IVA). |
| `accountId` | uuid | Ledger Account of the Entity the line posts to. |
| `side` | string | `debit` or `credit`. |

A template needs at least 2 and at most 2000 lines. Exactly one line uses `invoice_total`: the **Control Line**. It must follow the control line rules of [Book invoice](/api-reference/invoices) for a payable invoice: a `credit` on an open-item payable liability account. No other line may post to the Control Line's account.

While a template uses an account as its Control Line, that account's type, subtype, and tracking cannot change (`LEDGER_ACCOUNT_FACT_PINNED_ERROR`). An account any template line uses cannot be deleted.

### When an Invoice is booked automatically

Sintropix books a new Invoice from the template only when all of these hold:

1. The create request sent no `bookingEntryWithLines`. An explicit booking always wins.
2. The Invoice is `direction: "payable"` and `documentKind: "invoice"`. Credit notes are never booked automatically.
3. The same request creates the DTE. An Invoice whose DTE is attached later is not booked. A source-feed sync never books a DTE that lands with the [Standing](/api-reference/source-feeds#standing-and-tax-period) `claimed`, `annulment_requested`, or `annulled`.
4. The Invoice and its DTE are in the Entity's functional currency.
5. The Partner has `booksAutomatically` set to `true` in the Entity.
6. Every line's source has a value on the DTE, and none is negative. A DTE that withholds no IVA gives a `dte_withheld_iva_amount` line `0`.
7. The Control Line amount is not `0`. Other lines with an amount of `0` are dropped.
8. The remaining lines balance exactly. There is no rounding tolerance.

The booking follows every rule of Book invoice, including the Lock Date. If any condition fails, the Invoice and its DTE are still created, unbooked, and Sintropix stores no reason. In `POST /invoices/bulk` and the source-feed sync, one Invoice that does not fit never blocks the others. Invoices that exist before you turn the switch on are not booked.

The [Audit Trail](/api-reference/audit-trail) credits an automatic booking to the system, with the declared actor `booking-template`.

### GET `/partners/:partnerId/booking-template`

Read a Partner's template.

**Response:** `{ "partner": <Partner row>, "lines": [<line>] }`. The Partner row has the shape `GET /partners/pages` lists. `lines` is empty when the Partner has no template.

### PUT `/partners/:partnerId/booking-template`

Save a template. The body replaces the whole list of lines; the order of `lines` is the order of the template.

**Body:** `{ "lines": [<line>] }`. Unknown keys are rejected with `400`.

**Response:** the saved template, in the shape `GET` answers.

```bash
curl -X PUT "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners/$PARTNER_ID/booking-template" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "lines": [
      { "amountSource": "dte_net_amount", "accountId": "a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a7b-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d", "side": "debit" },
      { "amountSource": "dte_tax_amount", "accountId": "5c6d7e8f-9a0b-4c1d-8e2f-3a4b5c6d7e8f", "side": "debit" },
      { "amountSource": "invoice_total", "accountId": "3e7a9c1b-2d4f-4a6e-8b0c-1d2e3f4a5b6c", "side": "credit" }
    ]
  }'
```

With this template, an Invoice with a `total` of `1190000` and a DTE stating `netAmount` `1000000` and `taxAmount` `190000` is booked. The net and tax lines are debited, and the payable is credited. A DTE whose amounts do not add up to the total leaves the Invoice unbooked.

To also book the vendor's facturas de compra (type `46`), add a `credit` line with `dte_withheld_iva_amount` on the withheld IVA account. A 46 with a `total` of `1000000`, `netAmount` `1000000`, `taxAmount` `190000`, and `withheldIvaAmount` `190000` is booked with the withheld IVA credited. On a 33, which withholds no IVA, that line is `0` and is dropped, so the same template books both.

<Note>
The `wasabil` Source Feed lands every 46 with `withheldIvaAmount` null, because Wasabil no longer states which IVA is withheld. A `dte_withheld_iva_amount` line has no amount on these DTEs, so the entry does not balance and the Invoice lands unbooked. For a Wasabil vendor's 46s, book the withheld IVA `credit` line from `dte_tax_amount` instead. That template books the vendor's 46s with a total withholding, but not its 33s.
</Note>

Then turn the switch on:

```bash
curl -X PATCH "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/partners/$PARTNER_ID" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "booksAutomatically": true }'
```

### DELETE `/partners/:partnerId/booking-template`

Remove the template. This also turns `booksAutomatically` off.

**Response:** the Partner and its now empty `lines`, in the shape `GET` answers.

### Error codes

| Code | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PARTNER_BOOKS_AUTOMATICALLY_REQUIRES_BOOKING_TEMPLATE_ERROR` | `409` | `booksAutomatically: true` was sent for a Partner without a template. |
| `CONTROL_ACCOUNT_IN_ENTRY_LINES_MORE_THAN_ONCE_ERROR` | `400` | Another line posts to the Control Line's account. |

A Control Line on the wrong side or on an account Book invoice refuses as a payable control account is answered with the same error Book invoice returns.

## Status codes

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `200` | Success on `GET`, `PUT`, `PATCH`, `DELETE`, and merge. `DELETE` answers with the row as it was before. `GET /partners/:partnerId/logo` answers with the logo PNG. |
| `201` | Partner created. |
| `400` | Validation error (including an invalid RUT, an invalid website, an unknown country, or an unknown bank), or missing `x-audit-actor` on a key-authenticated mutation. |
| `403` | A merge with an API key confined to one Entity would move another Entity's books (`PERMISSION_DENIED_ERROR`). |
| `404` | Entity, Organization, or Partner not found, the Entity doesn't use the Partner, the Partner has no logo (`PARTNER_LOGO_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), or it has no Bank Details to delete (`PARTNER_BANK_DETAILS_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`). |
| `409` | Partner id already exists, another Partner of the Organization holds the tax identity, the Partner has open items or references and cannot be deleted, an Alias is taken, the Partner is merged, a merge is refused, or a Booking Template rule is violated. |
| `422` | The Partner represents the Entity (`PARTNER_REPRESENTS_ENTITY_ERROR`), or a change would edit a representing Partner's tax identity (`PARTNER_REPRESENTS_ENTITY_TAX_IDENTITY_ERROR`). |
