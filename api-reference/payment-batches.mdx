A Payment Batch (**Nómina** in the app) is a set of payments an Entity sends to its bank together, on one payment date. A batch names no paying account: you choose the [Bank Account](/api-reference/bank-accounts) a bank file pays from when you [download the file](#download-the-bank-file), because orders in other currencies don't leave from it. Each line of a batch is a **Payment Order** (**Pago**): it pays a purchase [Invoice](/api-reference/invoices), or it's a manual payment with no document.

Each Payment Order states its own currency. An order that pays an Invoice is in the Invoice's currency. A manual order is in the currency you choose. One batch can mix currencies, so it totals each currency apart and converts nothing.

The bank files carry only the orders in `CLP`. You pay an order in another currency at the bank by hand, such as a USD payment to a foreign vendor. It still counts in the batch's totals and appears in the movements spreadsheet. Its payee needs no Chilean RUT and no Bank Details.

A Payment Order records what you intend to pay. It posts nothing to the ledger. The ledger stays the record of what was actually paid: once the bank executes the transfers, book or match the resulting [Bank Movements](/api-reference/bank-movements) as usual. When Erwin reconciles, he reads the Ready batches whose payment date is near a Movement's date to tell who the payment is for and which Invoice it pays. He never matches a Movement that disagrees with the batch, and he ignores Drafts.

Use Payment Batches to prepare a supplier payment run:

1. List the [Payables to Plan](/api-reference/reports#payables-to-plan): the purchase invoices the Entity still owes.
2. [Create a Draft](#post-payment-batches) with the payment date. Optionally give it a [name and a label](#name-and-label).
3. [Add invoices](#post-payment-batches-paymentbatchid-invoice-payment-orders) and [manual payments](#post-payment-batches-paymentbatchid-payment-orders), and adjust amounts or memos. In the app, **Agregar cuentas por pagar** lists the payables in every currency and lets you search them by payee, document number, or Amount to Plan. Its **Fuente** selector and **Filtros** menu work as in **Compras**; see [Orders in the app](#orders-in-the-app).
4. [Mark it Ready](#post-payment-batches-paymentbatchid-mark-ready). This freezes the batch.
5. [Download the bank file](#download-the-bank-file) in the app, choosing the bank and, for BCI, the account to debit. Upload it to your bank's or Global66's portal.

Every route on this page lives under `/api/entities/:entityId/payment-batches`. Amounts are integers in the minor units of their currency.

## Draft and Ready

A batch has two states:

| State | `readyAt` | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | `null` | Add, edit, and remove orders. Change the payment date, the name, or the label. Delete the batch. |
| Ready | timestamp | Download the bank file. Remove an order the bank rejected. Change the name or the label. [Reopen](#post-payment-batches-paymentbatchid-reopen) it as a Draft. Every other write is refused with `409` and `PAYMENT_BATCH_READY_ERROR`. |

A batch starts as a Draft and becomes Ready only through [`POST /mark-ready`](#post-payment-batches-paymentbatchid-mark-ready). To fix a mistake in a Ready batch, [reopen it](#post-payment-batches-paymentbatchid-reopen): it becomes a Draft again, and you mark it Ready again when it's fixed. A Ready batch can't be deleted; reopen it first.

## Name and label

A batch can carry a **name** and a **label** to tell it apart from the others. Both are optional free text:

- **Name** (**Nombre**): up to 120 characters, such as *Proveedores segunda quincena*. In the app, it's the batch's title. A batch with no name is titled **Nómina del** followed by its payment date.
- **Label** (**Etiqueta**): up to 60 characters, such as *Proveedores* or *Remuneraciones*. Use it to group batches of the same kind. You choose the labels; Sintropix has no fixed list.

Sintropix trims both. A blank name or label is refused with `400`; send `null` to clear it. Neither is part of the payment: no bank file carries them, and you can change them on a Ready batch.

In the app:

- **Nueva nómina** asks for the name, the payment date, and the label. Click the title of a batch to rename it, and click its label, or **Agregar etiqueta**, to change it.
- You pick a label like a tag. The picker lists the labels already used on the Entity's batches, and **Buscar o crear etiqueta…** filters them as you type. To make a new label, type it and choose **Crear «…»**. The app doesn't offer to create a label that already exists with different capitalization, so one label isn't spelled two ways. Choose **Sin etiqueta** to clear the label.
- The **Pagos** list shows each batch's **Nombre** and **Etiqueta**. **Buscar nóminas** matches the name and the label. It ignores case and accents, and every word you type must match. Under **Filtros**, **Etiqueta** keeps only the batches with that exact label.

## Amount to Plan

The **Amount to Plan** of a purchase invoice is what it still owes minus what Payment Orders already plan for it, in the Invoice's currency:

- An unbooked invoice owes its `total`. A booked invoice owes the Open Amount of its Control Line.
- Every order that names the invoice counts, in every batch, Draft or Ready.
- An invoice [excluded from the books](/api-reference/invoices#exclude-an-invoice-from-the-books) owes nothing, so it has nothing to plan.

Editing an order can plan more than the invoice owes. Sintropix accepts it, and the invoice's Amount to Plan goes below zero. The app flags it as a warning.

<Note>
  If you match a partial payment of an invoice that a batch already plans, its Amount to Plan can show less than it still owes. Correct the order's amount in the Draft. The books aren't affected.
</Note>

## Orders in the app

The batch's table shows, for each order that pays an invoice:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Monto total** | The invoice's `total`. |
| **Saldo pendiente** | What the invoice still owes (`owedAmount`): its total while unbooked, the Open Amount of its Control Line once booked, and 0 when it's excluded from the books. |
| **Pago** | What this batch pays (`amount`). On a Draft, hover over it to copy or edit it. On a Ready batch, a lock shows instead. |

A manual order shows **—** under **Monto total** and **Saldo pendiente**. Lowering **Pago** doesn't change the other two columns.

**Agregar cuentas por pagar** shows the same **Monto total** and **Saldo pendiente** for each invoice. When orders in other batches already plan part of the balance, a line such as *$150.000 ya en nóminas* shows how much. Picking the invoice adds an order for what is left, its [Amount to Plan](#amount-to-plan).

To narrow the list, use the same controls as **Compras**:

- **Fuente** keeps the invoices of one [Source](/api-reference/sources).
- **Filtros** filters by **Documento tributario**, **Contacto**, **Moneda**, **Estado de pago**, **Estado SII**, **Seguimiento**, issue and due dates, **Período tributario**, **Monto**, and **Comentarios**. Each active filter shows as a chip you can remove.

A due-date filter keeps the invoices with no due date. The API takes the same filters on [Payables to Plan](/api-reference/reports#payables-to-plan).

## The Payment Batch object

Every route on this page except the list returns the batch with its orders.

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Batch id. |
| `entityId` | uuid | Owning Entity. |
| `paymentDate` | string (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | The date the payments are to be made. |
| `name` | string \| null | The batch's [name](#name-and-label). `null` when it has none. |
| `label` | string \| null | The batch's [label](#name-and-label). `null` when it has none. |
| `readyAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp \| null | When the batch was last marked Ready. `null` for a Draft, including a reopened one. |
| `readyBy` | uuid \| null | The user who marked it Ready. `null` for a Draft, or once that user is [deleted](/api-reference/onboarding#delete-onboarding-users-userid). |
| `createdBy` | uuid \| null | The user who created the batch. `null` once that user is deleted. |
| `createdByName` | string \| null | Name of that user. `null` once that user is deleted. |
| `paymentOrderCount` | integer | Number of orders in the batch. |
| `totals` | array | The orders' amounts summed per currency, as `{ currency, amount }`, in currency-code order. `amount` is in minor units, as a decimal string such as `"4820500"`. Amounts in different currencies are never converted or added together. Empty for a batch with no orders. |
| `paymentOrders` | array | The orders, in payee name order (see below). Not present on the list. |
| `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | Row timestamps. |

Each element of `paymentOrders`:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Order id. |
| `entityId` | uuid | Owning Entity. |
| `paymentBatchId` | uuid | The batch the order belongs to. |
| `partnerId` | uuid | The payee [Partner](/api-reference/partners). |
| `invoiceId` | uuid \| null | The Invoice the order pays. `null` for a manual order, or when the Invoice was deleted. |
| `currency` | string | ISO 4217 code of the order. An order that pays an Invoice is in the Invoice's currency. |
| `amount` | integer | Amount to pay, greater than zero. |
| `memo` | string \| null | Glosa: the message that travels with the transfer. |
| `payee` | object | `{ name, country, taxId, bankDetails }` of the payee. `bankDetails` is the Partner's [Bank Details](/api-reference/partners#bank-details), or `null` when it has none. |
| `invoice` | object \| null | `{ documentNumber, dueDate, total, owedAmount, amountToPlan, dteTypeCode, dteTypeLabel }` of the Invoice. `owedAmount` is what it still owes: its `total` while unbooked, the Open Amount of its Control Line once booked, and `0` when it's [excluded from the books](/api-reference/invoices#exclude-an-invoice-from-the-books). `amountToPlan` is its current [Amount to Plan](#amount-to-plan). `dteTypeCode` and `dteTypeLabel` name the SII type of the DTE behind it, or `null` when no DTE backs it. `null` when `invoiceId` is null. |
| `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp | Row timestamps. |

## GET `/payment-batches`

List every Payment Batch of the Entity, the latest payment date first.

**Response:** an array of Payment Batch objects without `paymentOrders`.

```bash
curl "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/payment-batches" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY"
```

## GET `/payment-batches/:paymentBatchId`

Read one batch with its orders.

**Response:** the Payment Batch object.

## POST `/payment-batches`

Create an empty Draft.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Client-generated id. Retrying with the same id is answered `409` with `PAYMENT_BATCH_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`, so a retry never creates a second batch. |
| `paymentDate` | string (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | Yes | The date the payments are to be made. |
| `name` | string \| null | No | The batch's [name](#name-and-label). Trimmed, 1 to 120 characters. |
| `label` | string \| null | No | The batch's [label](#name-and-label). Trimmed, 1 to 60 characters. |

Unknown fields, such as `bankAccountId`, are refused with `400`. A batch names no paying account.

**Response** (`201`): the Payment Batch object, with no orders.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/payment-batches" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "id": "7a8b9c0d-1e2f-4a3b-8c4d-5e6f7a8b9c0d",
    "paymentDate": "2026-10-15",
    "name": "Proveedores segunda quincena",
    "label": "Proveedores"
  }'
```

## PATCH `/payment-batches/:paymentBatchId`

Change the payment date of a Draft, or the name or the label of any batch.

**Body:** at least one of the fields below.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `paymentDate` | string (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | No | The new payment date. Draft only. |
| `name` | string \| null | No | The new [name](#name-and-label). Trimmed, 1 to 120 characters. `null` clears it. |
| `label` | string \| null | No | The new [label](#name-and-label). Trimmed, 1 to 60 characters. `null` clears it. |

On a Ready batch, a body with `paymentDate` is refused with `409` and `PAYMENT_BATCH_READY_ERROR`, and nothing changes. A body with only `name` or `label` goes through.

**Response:** the Payment Batch object.

```bash
curl -X PATCH "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/payment-batches/$PAYMENT_BATCH_ID" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "name": "Proveedores octubre", "label": null }'
```

## DELETE `/payment-batches/:paymentBatchId`

Delete a Draft and its orders. A Ready batch can't be deleted: [reopen it](#post-payment-batches-paymentbatchid-reopen) first.

**Response:** the Payment Batch object as it was before deletion, with its orders.

## POST `/payment-batches/:paymentBatchId/invoice-payment-orders`

Add purchase invoices to a Draft. Each invoice becomes an order for its current [Amount to Plan](#amount-to-plan), in the Invoice's currency, paid to the Invoice's Partner. Invoices in any currency can join the same batch.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `paymentOrders` | array | Yes | At least one `{ "id": "<uuid>", "invoiceId": "<uuid>" }`. `id` is the client-generated id of the new order. An `id` or an `invoiceId` listed twice is refused with `400`. |

Each invoice must be a Payable to Plan of the batch:

- A `payable` Invoice of kind `invoice`. A credit note or a receivable is refused with `PAYMENT_ORDER_INVOICES_NOT_PAYABLE_ERROR`.
- With an Amount to Plan above zero. Otherwise `PAYMENT_ORDER_INVOICES_NOTHING_TO_PLAN_ERROR`.

Each of these errors answers `409` and lists every offending invoice in `params.invoiceIds`, so you can fix them all in one pass. Nothing is added when any invoice is refused.

**Response** (`201`): the Payment Batch object.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/payment-batches/$PAYMENT_BATCH_ID/invoice-payment-orders" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "paymentOrders": [
      { "id": "1b2c3d4e-5f6a-4b7c-8d9e-0f1a2b3c4d5e", "invoiceId": "6f1c2b1e-0d1a-4c7b-9a3e-2b8f5d0e1a11" }
    ]
  }'
```

## POST `/payment-batches/:paymentBatchId/payment-orders`

Add a manual order, with no Invoice, to a Draft. Use it for a payment that no document backs yet, such as an advance, or a payment to a foreign vendor whose invoice comes after the payment.

In the app, **Agregar pago manual** has a **Nuevo contacto** button next to **Beneficiario**. Use it when the payee doesn't exist yet. It creates the Partner and selects it as the payee. If another Partner of the Organization already has that tax ID, it offers **Usar** with that Partner's name instead. The **Moneda** starts as the Entity's currency.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | uuid | Yes | Client-generated id of the order. Retrying with the same id is answered `409` with `PAYMENT_ORDER_ALREADY_EXISTS_ERROR`. |
| `partnerId` | uuid | Yes | The payee. A Merged Partner is refused with `409` and `PARTNER_MERGED_ERROR`. |
| `currency` | string | Yes | ISO 4217 code of the payment. An unknown code is refused with `400` and `UNKNOWN_CURRENCY_ERROR`. |
| `amount` | integer | Yes | Greater than zero, in minor units of `currency`. |
| `memo` | string \| null | No | Glosa for the transfer. Trimmed; a blank memo is refused. |

**Response** (`201`): the Payment Batch object.

## PATCH `/payment-batches/:paymentBatchId/payment-orders/:paymentOrderId`

Edit an order of a Draft.

**Body:** at least one of the fields below.

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `amount` | integer | No | Greater than zero. It may plan more than the invoice owes; see [Amount to Plan](#amount-to-plan). |
| `memo` | string \| null | No | Trimmed, non-empty. `null` clears it. |

**Response:** the Payment Batch object.

## DELETE `/payment-batches/:paymentBatchId/payment-orders/:paymentOrderId`

Remove an order. This works on a Draft and on a Ready batch, so you can take out a payment the bank rejected. Removing an order returns its amount to the invoice's Amount to Plan.

**Response:** the Payment Batch object.

## POST `/payment-batches/:paymentBatchId/mark-ready`

Mark a Draft Ready. No body. The batch must have at least one order, and every payee of an order in `CLP` needs a Chilean RUT and [Bank Details](/api-reference/partners#bank-details), because the bank file names each payee by them. The payee of an order in another currency needs neither.

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PAYMENT_BATCH_EMPTY_ERROR` | The batch has no orders. |
| `PAYMENT_BATCH_PAYEES_WITHOUT_RUT_ERROR` | Some payees of orders in `CLP` have no Chilean RUT: their `country` isn't `CL` or their `taxId` is empty. `params.partnerIds` lists them. |
| `PAYMENT_BATCH_PAYEES_WITHOUT_BANK_DETAILS_ERROR` | Some payees of orders in `CLP` have no Bank Details. `params.partnerIds` lists them. |

Each answers `409`. Fix the Partners and try again. A Draft can hold a payee that lacks them. In the app, the row of an order in `CLP` flags a missing RUT or missing Bank Details. The row of an order in another currency shows the payee's foreign tax id as is, without a warning.

Once Ready, the batch keeps its payment date, takes no new order, and its orders keep their amount. Its name and label can still change.

**Response** (`200`): the Payment Batch object, with `readyAt` and `readyBy` set.

## POST `/payment-batches/:paymentBatchId/reopen`

Return a Ready batch to Draft, to fix a mistake found after it was marked Ready, such as a wrong amount or payment date. No body.

The batch becomes a Draft again: `readyAt` and `readyBy` go back to `null`. You can then add, edit, and remove orders, change the payment date, or delete the batch. To download a bank file again, [mark it Ready](#post-payment-batches-paymentbatchid-mark-ready) again, which checks the payees' RUT and Bank Details anew.

Reopening a Draft changes nothing and answers the batch as it is, so a retry is safe.

<Warning>
  Reopening a batch doesn't reach the bank. If you already uploaded its file to your bank's or Global66's portal, those transfers are unchanged. Fix or cancel them in the portal, then upload a new file if you need one.
</Warning>

In the app, a Ready batch shows **Volver a borrador**. It asks you to confirm before reopening the batch.

**Response** (`200`): the Payment Batch object.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.sintropix.com/api/entities/$ENTITY_ID/payment-batches/$PAYMENT_BATCH_ID/reopen" \
  -H "x-api-key: $SINTROPIX_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-audit-actor: my-agent"
```

## Download the bank file

In the app, a Ready batch has a **Descargar** menu with two options. The browser builds each file from the batch, so the API has no download route.

- **Nómina para pago nacional (CLP)…**: the batch's transfers in `CLP`, in the format of the bank that pays them. It opens a dialog where you choose the **Banco**:
  - **BCI**: BCI's bulk transfer file (Pago a Proveedores), in `.xlsx`, ready to upload to BCI's portal. Choose the **Cuenta de cargo**, the account BCI debits, among the Entity's Bank Accounts in `CLP`. It needs an `accountNumber`. The file carries only the orders in `CLP`. When the batch also has orders in other currencies, the dialog says how many are left out, for example *1 pago en otra moneda queda fuera*. With no order in `CLP`, or a Cuenta de cargo with no `accountNumber`, **Descargar** is disabled and the dialog says why.
  - **Global66**: Global66's beneficiary template (PlantillaBeneficiarios), filled in, as `nomina-global66-<paymentDate>.xlsm`, ready to upload to Global66's portal to pay from a Global66 account. It asks for no account. It carries only the orders in `CLP` to a bank that Global66 lists (see [Global66 file](#global66-file)). When the batch has other orders, the dialog says how many are left out, for example *1 pago en otra moneda o a un banco que Global66 no recibe queda fuera*. When no order qualifies, **Descargar** is disabled.
- **Excel de movimientos**: a spreadsheet of every order, in every currency.

Every download reads the batch again first. If it was reopened in the meantime, for example from another tab, the app refuses to build the file and asks you to mark it Ready again. A bank file never carries amounts the batch no longer plans.

Pay the orders a file leaves out through another channel, such as your bank's portal.

### BCI file

Each order in `CLP` fills one row of the BCI file:

| BCI column | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Nº Cuenta de Cargo | The `accountNumber` of the **Cuenta de cargo** you chose. |
| Nº Cuenta de Destino, Banco Destino | The payee's Bank Details: account number and three-digit CMF bank code. |
| Rut Beneficiario, Dig. Verif. | The payee's RUT, split into body and check digit. |
| Nombre Beneficiario | The Partner's name, cut to 30 characters. |
| Monto Transferencia | The amount, in whole pesos. |
| Nro.Factura Boleta | The Invoice's document number. Empty for a manual order. |
| Nº Orden de Compra | Empty. |
| Tipo de Pago | `PRV` for a boleta de honorarios (BHE or BTE), `OTR` otherwise. |
| Mensaje Destinatario | The order's memo. Without one, the SII type and number of the document, for example `Factura Electrónica 10819`, or `Factura` and the number when no DTE backs the Invoice. Empty for a manual order with no memo. |
| Email Destinatario | The notification email of the payee's Bank Details. |
| Cuenta Destino inscrita como | `Cuenta` followed by the Partner's name, cut to 25 characters. |

### Global66 file

Each qualifying order fills one row of the **Cuenta Bancaria** sheet of Global66's template, from row 5. The template keeps its macros, validations, and other sheets. Its **¿Válido?** column checks each row when you run the template's macro, before you upload it.

| Global66 column | Value |
| --- | --- |
| País | `CL - Chile`. |
| Tipo Destinatario | `Persona` when the payee's RUT body is under 50.000.000, `Empresa` otherwise. |
| Nombre | For a `Persona`, the first word of the Partner's name. For an `Empresa`, the whole name. |
| Apellido | For a `Persona`, the rest of the Partner's name. Empty for an `Empresa`. |
| Tipo de Documento | `RUT`. |
| Número de Documento | The payee's RUT, body and check digit with no dots or dash, for example `76142436K`. |
| Tipo de Cuenta | `CC - Cuenta Corriente`, `CA - Cuenta de Ahorros`, or `CV - Cuenta a la Vista`, from the payee's Bank Details. |
| Número de Cuenta Bancaria | The account number of the payee's Bank Details. |
| Banco | The payee's bank, by Global66's own name and id, for example `16 - Banco de Chile`. |
| Modalidad Origen o Destino | `Destino`: the payee receives the exact amount, and Global66 charges its cost on top. |
| Monto | The amount, in whole pesos. |

Global66 takes transfers to these banks, named by their CMF code: Banco de Chile (`001`), Banco Internacional (`009`), BancoEstado (`012`), Scotiabank (`014`), BCI / MACHBANK (`016`), Banco Bice (`028`), HSBC (`031`), Santander (`037`), Itaú (`039`), JP Morgan (`041`), Banco Security (`049`), Banco Falabella (`051`), Banco Ripley (`053`), Banco Consorcio (`055`), Tanner (`062`), Coopeuch / Dale (`672`), Prepago Los Héroes (`729`), Prepago Tenpo (`730`), TAPP Caja Los Andes (`732`), Copec Pay (`741`), Prex (`743`), Fintual (`746`), and Mercado Pago (`875`). An order to any other bank is left out of the file.

Each file reads each payee's Bank Details when you download it. If you change a Partner's Bank Details after marking the batch Ready, download the file again.

## Status codes

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `200` | Success on `GET`, `PATCH`, `DELETE`, `POST /mark-ready`, and `POST /reopen`. |
| `201` | Payment Batch created, or orders added. |
| `400` | Validation error, including an unknown body field such as `bankAccountId`, an unknown currency on a manual order (`UNKNOWN_CURRENCY_ERROR`), or missing `x-audit-actor` on a key-authenticated mutation. |
| `404` | Entity, Payment Batch (`PAYMENT_BATCH_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), Payment Order (`PAYMENT_ORDER_NOT_FOUND_ERROR`), Partner, or Invoice not found. |
| `409` | The batch is Ready (`PAYMENT_BATCH_READY_ERROR`), an id already exists, an invoice isn't a Payable to Plan, the batch isn't complete for Ready, the Partner is merged, or the orders of one invoice would add up past 2^53 − 1 minor units (`PAYMENT_ORDER_INVOICE_PLAN_TOO_LARGE_ERROR`). |
