Every record the API returns has a page in the Sintropix web app at [app.sintropix.com](https://app.sintropix.com). When you mention a record to a user, include its link so they can open it in one click. The link is built from ids you already have in the API response; no extra call is needed.

<Tip>
  **For agents.** Whenever you cite an entry, invoice, movement, partner, or report in a reply, attach its app URL as a Markdown link. Users read the numbers in your reply, then click through to verify or act.
</Tip>

## URL grammar

Most authenticated pages live under the Entity they belong to:

```text
https://app.sintropix.com/{entityId}/<page>[?<params>]
```

- `{entityId}` is the same UUID you use in `/api/entities/{entityId}/...`. It is always the first path segment.
- `<page>` is one of the pages listed below. There is no locale prefix; the app picks the language itself.
- Query parameters are optional and default values are dropped. Pass ids as bare UUIDs and dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`.

Two areas live outside an Entity:

- **Configuración** pages that belong to the whole Organization live under `https://app.sintropix.com/organizations/{organizationId}/settings`. They are the Empresa page, Plan de cuentas, Integraciones, Dimensiones, Canales del agente, Tipos de cambio, and Log. Take `organizationId` from the Entity or the record. The Organization's pages live beside them, under `https://app.sintropix.com/organizations/{organizationId}`: **Chat** at `/chat`, **Bandeja** at `/notifications`, **Tareas** at `/tasks`, and **Intercompañía** at `/intercompany-entries`.
- **Tu cuenta**, the signed-in User's own settings, lives under `https://app.sintropix.com/account`.

Deep links survive login. If the user is signed out, the app shows the login form and then lands on the exact URL requested. A user without access to the Entity sees a "no access" screen rather than a redirect.

## Opening a record in a side panel

Invoices, bank movements, and open items do not have a page of their own. They open in a side panel on top of their list, and the panel is addressed with the `open` query parameter:

```text
https://app.sintropix.com/{entityId}/invoices?open={invoiceId}
https://app.sintropix.com/{entityId}/banks/{bankAccountId}?open={movementId}
```

The `open` parameter is the universal convention: whichever list page you are on, `?open=<id>` opens that record's panel.

## Resource to URL map

| API resource | App URL | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entity | `/{entityId}/home` | The Entity dashboard. Its settings are in its row on the Organization's Empresa page. |
| Organization | `/organizations/{organizationId}/settings` | The Empresa page: the Organization's logo and name, and one row per Entity (**Entidades legales**) with its settings. |
| Ledger Entry | `/{entityId}/entries/{entryId}` | Full-page detail. The list is `/{entityId}/entries`. |
| Invoice, `direction: receivable` | `/{entityId}/invoices?open={invoiceId}` | AR documents. Pick the page from the `direction` field. |
| Invoice, `direction: payable` | `/{entityId}/bills?open={invoiceId}` | AP documents. |
| Expense Claim | `/{entityId}/expense-renditions?expenseClaimStatus=submitted` | **Rendiciones**, on its **Por aprobar** view. Use `expenseClaimStatus=rejected` for **Rechazadas**. An approved claim is its Expense Rendition, at `/{entityId}/expense-renditions?open={invoiceId}`. The Approver and the booking setting are at `/{entityId}/settings/expense-claims`. |
| Attachment | `/{entityId}/invoices?open={invoiceId}` or `/{entityId}/bills?open={invoiceId}` | Attachments are shown inside the invoice panel. |
| Task | `/organizations/{organizationId}/tasks` | `staff` and `client` members. Every Task of the Organization, across its Entities. Add `assignee=me` for the caller's own Tasks (**Mías**), `view=board` for the board (**Tablero**), or `task={taskId}` to open one Task in its detail panel. A related Task also shows in the panel of each Invoice, Bank Movement, or Ledger Entry it relates to. |
| Chat message | `/organizations/{organizationId}/chat?open={messageId}` | The Organization's **Chat**, with the message's ERP Thread open beside it. Drop `open` for the main Chat. Every Member can open it. |
| Notification | `/organizations/{organizationId}/notifications` | The signed-in User's [Notifications](/api-reference/notifications) (**Bandeja**) in the Organization. |
| Intercompany Entries | `/organizations/{organizationId}/intercompany-entries` | **Intercompañía**, the Organization's list of intercompany entries. The sidebar offers it only in **Grupo**, so an Organization with one Entity doesn't show it. |
| Mailbox Conversation | `/organizations/{organizationId}/mailboxes/{integrationId}?open={conversationId}` | Opens the Conversation of a [Mailbox](/api-reference/integrations#mailbox) in its side panel. Drop `open` for the Mailbox's Conversations. `staff` and `client` members can open it. |
| Bank Account | `/{entityId}/banks/{bankAccountId}` | The reconciliation view for that account. `/{entityId}/banks` lists all accounts. `/{entityId}/settings/bank-accounts?open={bankAccountId}` opens the account's settings to edit its name, bank, and number. |
| Bank Movement | `/{entityId}/banks/{bankAccountId}?open={movementId}` | Use the `bankAccountId` field on the Movement. The panel's **Conciliación** tab is where the user reconciles it. |
| Bank Movements of every account | `/{entityId}/banks/movements` | Every Movement of the Entity, grouped by Bank Account. Add `status=unreconciled` for the ones still to reconcile, and `open={movementId}` to open one in its panel without leaving the list. |
| Partner | `/{entityId}/partner-ledger?partners={partnerId}` | Partner statement filtered to that Partner. `/{entityId}/partners` and `/{entityId}/vendors` are the customer and supplier lists. |
| Ledger Account | `/{entityId}/general-ledger?selectedAccountId={accountId}` | Movements of one account. The chart itself is **Plan de cuentas**, at `/organizations/{organizationId}/settings/chart-of-accounts`. It opens on the group chart (**Grupo**). Add `entity={entityId}` for one Entity's chart, and `open={accountId}` to open an account in it. |
| Open Item | `/{entityId}/receivables?open={entryId}` or `/{entityId}/payables?open={entryId}` | Pass the id of the Ledger Entry that carries the item. `/{entityId}/open-items` shows all. |
| Allocation | `/{entityId}/entries/{entryId}` | Allocations have no page. Link the Ledger Entry being settled. |
| Dimension | `/organizations/{organizationId}/settings/dimensions?dimension={dimensionId}` | Drop `dimension` for the list of every Dimension. |
| Source | `/{entityId}/sources/{sourceId}` | Documents imported by that Source. Sources are configured at `/{entityId}/settings/sources`. |
| Payment Batch | `/{entityId}/payment-batches/{paymentBatchId}` | `/{entityId}/payment-batches` lists every batch. It takes `search` (matches name and label) and `label` (keeps the batches with that exact label). |
| Source Feed | `/{entityId}/settings/sources` | Feeds are managed inside their Source's settings. |
| Integration | `/organizations/{organizationId}/settings/integrations?open={integrationId}` | Opens the Integration's side panel, with its details, the Bank Accounts or Source it feeds, and its actions. Drop `open` for the list of every Integration. Bank feeds are managed here too. |
| FX Rate | `/organizations/{organizationId}/settings/fx-rates` | |
| Agent Channel | `/organizations/{organizationId}/settings/agent-channels` | Staff only. |
| Audit Trail | `/organizations/{organizationId}/settings/log` | The Organization's **Log**, with an **Entidad** filter. A record's own history is in the **Actividad** tab of its panel. |
| API keys | `/account/api-keys` | The signed-in User's own keys. |
| Connected apps | `/account/connected-apps` | The apps the signed-in User approved. |
| Erwin | None | Erwin has no page. Link any Entity page, and the person opens Erwin from the **Erwin** button at the bottom right. |

## Reports

Reports are pages with the period in the query string. Omit a parameter to get the app's default, which is the current year to date. The Income Statement, the Income Statement by Dimension, and the Cash Flow run to the last day of the current month instead, so the current month counts in full. The Balance Sheet, the Eight-column Balance, and the General Ledger read the Entity's [Fiscal Year](/api-reference/reports#the-fiscal-year-in-reports) instead: a window must stay inside one Fiscal Year, and an omitted start or end falls on its bounds.

| Report | App URL | Parameters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Balance Sheet | `/{entityId}/balance` | `cutoffDate`, `presentationCurrency`, `presentationRate` |
| Eight-column Balance | `/{entityId}/eight-column-balance` | `exerciseStart`, `cutoffDate`, `presentationCurrency`, `presentationRate` |
| Income Statement | `/{entityId}/income-statement` | `from`, `to`, `presentationCurrency`, `presentationRate`, `groupBy` (`month`, `quarter`, `year`, or `none` for one column; the statement opens by `month` when you omit it), `dimension` (a Dimension id, to open accounts by its values). Add `drillDown={accountId}:{columnKey}` to open one cell, or `drillDown={accountId}:{columnKey}:{dimensionValueId}` to open one value's cell (`unassigned` for the lines with no value). |
| Income Statement by Dimension | `/{entityId}/income-statement-by-dimension` | `from`, `to`, `dimension` |
| General Ledger | `/{entityId}/general-ledger` | `from`, `to`, `selectedAccountId`, `sort` (`{column}:{direction}`, for example `debit:desc`). Columns are `entryDate`, `entryMemo`, `partnerName`, `debit`, `credit`, and `runningBalance`, and the direction is `asc` or `desc`. Omit it to sort by date, oldest first. |
| Journal | `/{entityId}/journal` | `from`, `to`, `search` |
| Partner Ledger | `/{entityId}/partner-ledger` | `from`, `to`, `partners`, `selectedAccountId` |
| Receivables aging | `/{entityId}/receivables` | `open` |
| Payables aging | `/{entityId}/payables` | `open` |
| Revenue Realization | `/{entityId}/revenue-realization` | `from`, `to`, `presentationCurrency`, `presentationRate` |
| Customer management | `/{entityId}/customers` | `from`, `to`, `search`, `onlyPending` (`true` by default). `/{entityId}/customers/{partnerId}` for one customer, with `from`, `to`, and `tab` (`invoices`, `unbilledReceivableLines`, `movements`). |
| Cash Flow | `/{entityId}/cash-flow` | `exerciseStart`, `cutoffDate`, `groupBy` (`month`, `quarter`, `year`). Add `drillDown={category}@{columnKey}`, or `drillDown={category}:{accountId}@{columnKey}` for one account, to open the entries of one cell. `columnKey` is the first day of the period column, or `total`. |
| Fixed Assets | `/{entityId}/fixed-assets` | `/{entityId}/fixed-assets/{fixedAssetId}` for one asset |

`presentationCurrency` and `presentationRate` must be passed together, exactly as in the [Reports API](/api-reference/reports).

## Filtering a list

List pages accept the same filters the user can set in the UI, so you can link to a precise view instead of the whole list.

| Page | Useful parameters |
| --- | --- |
| `/{entityId}/invoices`, `/{entityId}/bills` | `statusFilter` (`all`, `notPaid`, `paid`, `unbooked`, `booked`, `excluded`, `notExcluded`), `partnerId`, `currency`, `search`, `issueDateFrom`, `issueDateTo`, `dueDateFrom`, `dueDateTo`, `taxPeriodFrom`, `taxPeriodTo` (`YYYY-MM`), `paymentStatus` (`paid`, `partial`, `unpaid`), `totalMin` and `totalMax` (only with `currency`), `dteType`, `source`, `followUpKind` (`proposal`, `question`, `waiting`, `none`), `standing` (`accepted`, `pending`, `claimed`, `annulment_requested`, `annulled`, `observed`, `incompatible`) |
| `/{entityId}/entries` | `search`, `from`, `to`, `status` |
| `/{entityId}/banks/{bankAccountId}` | `status` (`unbooked`, `booked_pending_allocation`, `reconciled`, `excluded`, `unreconciled`, `notExcluded`), `followUpKind`, `partnerId`, `side`, `movementDateFrom`, `movementDateTo`, `amountMin`, `amountMax`, `search`, `groupBy` (`none`, `partner`, `movementMonth`, `status`) |
| `/{entityId}/banks/movements` | The same as one Bank Account, without `amountMin` and `amountMax`. `groupBy` also takes `bankAccount` |

## Example

An agent that just listed overdue payables in Chile might answer:

```markdown
Three supplier invoices are more than 60 days overdue:

- [F-1042 from Proveedora Andina, CLP 1,190,000](https://app.sintropix.com/9b2f…/bills?open=3f1c…)
- [F-1050 from Servicios Sur, CLP 480,000](https://app.sintropix.com/9b2f…/bills?open=7d8e…)
- [F-1061 from Logística Norte, CLP 2,350,000](https://app.sintropix.com/9b2f…/bills?open=9a0b…)

Full aging: https://app.sintropix.com/9b2f…/payables
```

The pattern is the same for every resource: take the Entity id from the route you called, take the record id from the response, and drop both into the URL for that resource.
