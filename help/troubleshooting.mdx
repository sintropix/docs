Most issues in Sintropix fall into a small number of predictable categories — bank connections that need refreshing, reconciliation queues that need attention after a first sync, or permission settings that need adjustment for a new team member. Use the sections below to diagnose and resolve the most common problems quickly. If you don't find your issue here, reach out to the support team.

## Bank Integration Issues

### Integration shows "Disconnected"

A "Disconnected" status almost always means your bank credentials have expired or the bank has revoked the platform's authorization token. This is a normal part of how open banking connections work and does not indicate a problem with your data.

**To reconnect:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations settings">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and locate the bank showing a red "Disconnected" badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Reconnect">
    Select **Reconnect** next to the integration. You will be redirected to your bank's authentication portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-authorize access">
    Log in with your bank credentials and approve read-only access for Sintropix. You will be returned to the integrations page once complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify sync resumes">
    The integration status should update to "Connected" within a few minutes. Sintropix will backfill any transactions missed during the disconnection window automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Transactions stopped syncing

If the integration shows "Connected" but new transactions are not appearing, try the following steps in order:

1. **Check the integration status** — go to **Settings → Integrations** and confirm the last successful sync timestamp. If it is more than a few hours old, the sync may be stalled.
2. **Trigger a manual refresh** — click the **Refresh** icon next to the integration to force an immediate sync attempt.
3. **Disconnect and reconnect** — if a manual refresh does not resolve the issue within 15 minutes, disconnect the integration entirely and reconnect it using the steps above.

<Tip>
  Some banks rate-limit API calls during peak hours. If a manual refresh fails, wait 30 minutes and try again before disconnecting.
</Tip>

---

### Duplicate transactions appearing

Duplicate transactions typically occur when the same bank account has been connected more than once — for example, under two different entities or through two different integration methods.

<Warning>
  Do not attempt to delete duplicate transactions manually, as this can create imbalances in your general ledger. Contact the Sintropix support team at support@sintropix.com and provide the entity name and account number. The support team will identify the duplicate connection and safely remove the duplicate entries.
</Warning>

## Reconciliation Issues

### Large number of unmatched transactions after first sync

Seeing a long reconciliation queue immediately after connecting your accounts for the first time is **expected behavior**. Sintropix needs historical context — invoice numbers, payroll cycles, recurring payment patterns — to confidently match bank movements to accounting entries. During the first week, work through the reconciliation queue manually. Each correction you make trains the matching engine, and the volume of unmatched items will decrease significantly after the first 5–7 days.

<Info>
  Focus first on recurring transactions (payroll, rent, SaaS subscriptions) — confirming these matches gives the engine the highest-confidence patterns to learn from.
</Info>

---

### Reconciliation suggesting wrong matches

If Sintropix proposes a match that is clearly incorrect:

1. **Decline the suggestion** — click **Reject** on the proposed match. Do not accept an incorrect match, as it will affect your general ledger accuracy.
2. **Search for the correct entry manually** — use the search bar in the reconciliation panel to find the correct invoice or journal entry by amount, date, or reference number.
3. **Confirm the correct match** — once you find the right entry, select it and confirm the match.

Sintropix's matching algorithm improves over time based on every correction your team makes. After a few weeks of use, the accuracy of suggested matches typically exceeds 90%.

---

### FX differences not posting correctly

If foreign exchange gain/loss entries are missing or posting to the wrong account, the most likely cause is an incorrect functional currency setting for the entity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Entity settings">
    Go to **Settings → Entities** and select the entity with the FX issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the functional currency">
    Confirm that the **Functional Currency** field matches the entity's primary operating currency (for example, CLP for Chile, MXN for Mexico).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and re-process">
    If the functional currency was incorrect, update it and save. Sintropix will recalculate FX differences for open periods. Note that changing the functional currency may require a review of previously closed periods.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Changing an entity's functional currency after transactions have already been posted is a significant accounting action. Consult your accountant before making this change on a live entity.
</Warning>

## Reports Issues

### Consolidated report shows unexpected numbers

If your consolidated P&L or Balance Sheet shows figures that don't match your expectations, the most common cause is **untagged intercompany transactions**. Sintropix only eliminates intercompany items that have been explicitly tagged.

**To diagnose:**

1. Go to **Multi-Entity → Eliminations** and review the list of tagged intercompany transactions for the period.
2. Compare against your known intercompany flows (management fees, loans, recharges).
3. For any missing items, locate the original transaction, open it, and tag it as intercompany with the correct counterpart entity.
4. Re-run the consolidated report — the tagged transactions will now be eliminated.

---

### Report shows a different amount than expected

Before escalating a report discrepancy, verify these three settings:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Entity filter">
    Confirm that the correct entities are selected in the entity filter at the top of the report. If you are viewing a consolidated report, ensure all intended subsidiaries are included. If you are viewing a single-entity report, ensure only one entity is selected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Date range">
    Check the date range selector. Sintropix defaults to the current calendar month. If you are comparing to a prior period or a custom fiscal period, update the range accordingly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reporting currency">
    Verify that the reporting currency in the report header matches your expectation. USD, CLP, and other currencies will produce different totals for the same underlying transactions. Change the reporting currency in the report settings if needed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Erwin (AI Assistant) Issues

### Erwin gives an answer that seems wrong

Erwin's responses are only as accurate as the data that has been imported and reconciled. If Erwin returns a figure that doesn't match your expectations:

1. **Check integration status** — go to **Settings → Integrations** and confirm all relevant bank and payroll connections are active and fully synced.
2. **Check for unreconciled items** — if your reconciliation queue has a large backlog, some transactions may not yet be reflected in the general ledger that Erwin reads.
3. **Clarify your question** — rephrase the query with specific entity names, currencies, and date ranges for the most precise answer.

<Tip>
  You can ask Erwin to explain how it reached an answer by adding "explain your reasoning" to any query. This helps you quickly identify if a data gap is causing an unexpected result.
</Tip>

---

### Erwin is slow to respond

Response time depends on the complexity of the query and the total volume of data across your entities. Simple lookups (e.g., "What is our cash balance today?") typically respond in 2–5 seconds. Complex analytical queries — multi-period forecasts, cross-entity comparisons, or revenue recognition schedules — may take **15–30 seconds**.

If Erwin appears to be loading for more than 60 seconds, refresh the page and resubmit your query. If the issue persists, check the [Sintropix status page](https://sintropix.com/status) for any active incidents.

## Account and Access Issues

### Team member can't see an entity

Entity visibility is controlled by role permissions. If a team member reports they cannot see an entity they should have access to:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team settings">
    Go to **Settings → Team** and find the team member's entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review entity permissions">
    Click **Edit** on their profile and review the **Entity Access** section. Confirm the entity in question is listed and the toggle is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Enable access for the missing entity and save. The team member will see the entity immediately on their next page refresh — no re-login required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

### Forgot password / can't log in

Use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page at [sintropix.com](https://sintropix.com) to receive a password reset email. Check your spam or junk folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

If you are unable to complete the reset (for example, because your work email address has changed), contact your account admin to update your email address, or reach out to support@sintropix.com for identity verification and manual account recovery.
