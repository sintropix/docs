## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Your Accounts">
    Link your bank accounts, payroll providers, expense platforms, and CRM in minutes. Sintropix supports 100+ integrations across LATAM and the US — from Santander and BCI to Mercury, Buk, HubSpot, and Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Data Is Captured Automatically">
    Every bank movement, invoice, payroll run, and expense lands in your general ledger without manual entry. Sintropix normalizes data across currencies, entities, and jurisdictions as it arrives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Reconciles for You">
    Erwin, your AI finance assistant, matches bank movements to invoices, payroll entries, and expense reports. Anomalies are flagged automatically so your team reviews exceptions, not every transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close Faster, Every Month">
    Because your ledger is updated daily, month-end becomes a review — not a rebuild. Work through a structured close checklist and finish in days instead of weeks.
  </Step>
</Steps>
